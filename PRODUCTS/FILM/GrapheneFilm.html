FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: What is the minimum order quantity?
Typically 1,000 units for standard orders. Sampling available at 10-50 units for evaluation.
Q: What is the lead time?
Standard modules: 7-15 days. Custom modules (new tooling): 25-35 days.
Q: Can I get samples for testing?
Yes. Samples are provided based on your application scenario, with full test reports. Sample costs are deducted from your first volume order.
Q: Do you have export certifications?
Modules can be CE, ROHS certified per target market requirements. XIHE provides full technical documentation support.
Q: How do you ensure batch consistency?
Automated printing line + in-line inspection + manual sampling + third-party periodic testing. Every batch is archived for traceability.

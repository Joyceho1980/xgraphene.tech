Questions Facilities Ask

Q: How is this different from a standard far-infrared sauna?

Standard saunas only generate heat. The XIHE CAPSULE integrates eight functional modules — graphene far-infrared, terahertz, pulsed magnetic field, photon light, negative ions, ozone sanitation, AI voice, and more. It is multi-physics field synergy, not single-mode heating.

Q: Is it complicated to operate? Does it require dedicated staff?

Not at all. It supports AI voice control (wake words: "Xiao Ai" or "Xiao Yi") — simply say "start system." The touchscreen also features intuitive ± controls. The 35-minute session ends automatically with power-off protection. For commercial settings, first-time guidance by staff is recommended.

Q: Is it safe? What about electromagnetic radiation?

Fully compliant with national electrical safety standards. The graphene module uses low-voltage design; terahertz and pulsed magnetic fields are physical fields with zero ionizing radiation. Ozone sanitization activates only when the capsule is unoccupied. All energy levels are incrementally adjustable to match individual tolerance.

Q: How do you maintain and sanitize the capsule?

Replace disposable linens between each session, then run ozone sanitization for 3-5 minutes. Wipe the mattress surface weekly with saline solution only (do not use alcohol). Close the pneumatic lid when not in use to extend strut lifespan.

Q: What types of commercial venues are a good fit?

Premium wellness centers, medical-grade health management facilities, beauty and anti-aging clinics, sports rehabilitation centers, corporate executive lounges, and five-star hotel spas. The CAPSULE helps venues establish a "cellular-level restoration" premium service tier.

Q: Are there any contraindications?

Yes. Including but not limited to: pregnancy, breastfeeding, internal metal implants (pacemakers, stents, surgical nails, etc.), less than 3 months post-surgery, epilepsy, cranial tumors, body temperature above 38°C, alcohol consumption, empty stomach, individuals under 15 or over 75 years of age. Remove all metal objects before use. Refer to the full product manual for the complete list. In commercial settings, strict compliance with screening protocols is required.

About This Hub

Sleep is the foundation of recovery and cellular health. During deep sleep, the brain clears metabolic waste through the glymphatic system, mitochondria undergo repair, and growth hormones are released. This hub connects sleep science to the broader picture of cellular energy and fatigue.

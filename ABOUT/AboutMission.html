The Problem
Most approaches only reach the surface.

The conventional industry is built on superficial heat — warming pads, infrared lamps, saunas. They make skin feel warm. They don't talk to cells.

I learned this the hard way. My father needed more than warmth. He needed a physical signal his cells could understand — precise, repeatable, and independent of human effort.

The Standard
A benchmark the industry didn't have.

XIHE became the lead drafter of China's national industry standard for graphene flexible electrothermal film — not because we wanted a title, but because the market had no definition of "good enough."

When you choose XIHE, you're not buying a device. You're adopting a measurable, peer-reviewed standard of precision — backed by IKKEM's national laboratory and supported by published research.

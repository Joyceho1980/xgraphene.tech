# Decision: Third-Party Story Use Policy

Date: 2026-06-08

## Core rule

XIHE may use third-party stories from platforms such as Quora as:

- inspiration
- audience-language input
- narrative pattern references
- question discovery material

XIHE should not use them as:

- direct customer testimonials
- undisclosed first-person XIHE stories
- proof of product efficacy

## Safe usage modes

### 1. Pattern extraction

Take the underlying problem pattern:

- exhausted after simple activity
- skipped dinner and felt clearer
- chronic itching and poor sleep

Then write a new educational opening in XIHE's own voice.

### 2. Composite scenario

Combine recurring themes from multiple real-world questions into one generalized opening such as:

- "Some people describe the same pattern: they are exhausted after short activity, sleep long hours, and still wake up tired."

This is safer than rewriting one identifiable person's story.

### 3. Attributed excerpt

If a specific post is especially valuable, use only a short attributed excerpt and make it clear it is from a public discussion source.

### 4. Story-inspired question

Use the story only to generate the page question and article angle.

Example:

- Quora story -> Why do some people feel exhausted after simple activity?

## Unsafe usage modes

- rewriting a Quora post as if it were Joyce's own story
- presenting a third-party story as a XIHE case study
- implying the person used XIHE products unless that is true and authorized
- preserving too many unique details that make the original author identifiable
- turning anecdote into medical proof

## Editorial language rule

When a story comes from third-party public content, use framing such as:

- "Some people describe..."
- "A common pattern in public health discussions is..."
- "In one public discussion, a person described..."

Avoid framing such as:

- "Our user found..."
- "One patient experienced..."
- "This case shows..."

unless the source and rights are clear.

## Why this matters

This protects:

- trust
- originality
- copyright safety
- scientific credibility
- brand ethics

## KB workflow rule

When importing a third-party story into the KB, record:

1. source platform
2. core question
3. emotional theme
4. science themes
5. red-flag medical issues
6. allowed use mode
7. forbidden use mode

## Best XIHE use

Third-party stories are strongest for:

- story openings
- question banks
- FAQ seeds
- LinkedIn hooks

The full educational answer should always come from XIHE's own KB and evidence framework.

## 1. Metabolism

### Core Human Pattern

People usually do not search for "metabolism" because they want a textbook definition.

They search because:

- energy feels inconsistent
- food does not seem to translate into usable energy
- weight, appetite, and fatigue feel connected
- aging seems to change how the body handles fuel

### Tier 1 Questions

1. Why is my metabolism so slow?
2. Why do I feel tired after eating?
3. Why does metabolism affect energy levels?
4. What is metabolic health?
5. Why does metabolism change with age?

### Tier 2 Questions

6. What is the difference between metabolism and digestion?
7. How does insulin resistance affect energy?
8. Why do some people seem to burn fuel faster?
9. What happens when metabolic flexibility is poor?
10. How does metabolism connect to mitochondria?

### Best Article Candidates

- Why Does Metabolism Affect Energy Levels?
- What Is Metabolic Health?
- Why Do I Feel Tired After Eating?
- Why Does Metabolism Change With Age?
- How Does Metabolism Connect to Mitochondria?

### FAQ Candidates

- What is the difference between metabolism and digestion?
- How does insulin resistance affect energy?
- What happens when metabolic flexibility is poor?

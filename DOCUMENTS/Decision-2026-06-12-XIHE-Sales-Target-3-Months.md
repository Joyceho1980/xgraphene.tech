# Decision: XIHE 3-Month Sales Target

Date: 2026-06-12

## Target

Over the next 3 months, XIHE's outbound operating goal is:

- 200 high-quality target accounts
- 2 paying customers

## Definition Of High-Quality Account

A high-quality account is one that has:

- clear segment fit
- identified buyer or likely buyer role
- known or inferable contact route
- pain-point hypothesis
- CRM record created or updated
- next action defined

## Operating Interpretation

The target is not raw lead volume.

The target is:

- qualified accounts
- credible outreach
- consistent follow-up
- real commercial conversion

## Execution Rule

When prioritizing work, prefer activities that improve:

- account quality
- route quality
- reply rate
- follow-up discipline
- meeting conversion

Do not optimize for vanity activity alone.

## Weekly Direction

Each week should move XIHE closer to:

- more qualified accounts in CRM
- more verified decision-maker routes
- more usable keyword intelligence
- more first-touch drafts
- more follow-up readiness

## Working Conclusion

This target is now the benchmark for the XIHE sales operating system.

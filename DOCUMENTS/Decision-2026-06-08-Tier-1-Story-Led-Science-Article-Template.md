# Decision: Tier 1 Story-Led Science Article Template

Date: 2026-06-08

## Context

The autophagy draft revealed a high-value XIHE article structure that may be more strategically important than generic definition pages.

This structure performs well across:

- SEO
- AI citation readiness
- brand building

when handled with scientific restraint.

## Strategic conclusion

XIHE should treat this as a Tier 1 article model:

- real story
- real question
- simple metaphor
- careful science explanation
- optional solution context
- clear disclaimer

This is stronger than publishing only generic pages such as:

- What Is Autophagy?
- What Is ATP?
- What Is Mitophagy?

because the story-led structure is:

- more memorable
- more human
- harder to copy
- easier to share
- still compatible with Quick Answers and internal links

## Core structure

Recommended pattern:

1. real story
2. question raised by the story
3. simple metaphor
4. scientific explanation
5. restrained solution layer
6. disclaimer

## Why this works

This structure combines:

- human attention
- conceptual clarity
- AI-readable framing
- educational depth

It resembles successful English-language health and longevity communication patterns:

- personal observation
- question
- interpretation
- research

## Best example from current article

The strongest metaphor in the autophagy draft is:

- hunger or fasting = factory slowdown
- autophagy = workers cleaning and recycling

This is a high-value explanatory device and can largely be preserved.

## Main risk rule

Do not let the story prove the mechanism.

Unsafe move:

- friend fasted for seven days
- eczema improved
- therefore autophagy caused the improvement

Safer move:

- this may help explain why some people notice changes during fasting or calorie restriction
- but outcomes vary and many factors may contribute

## High-risk wording to avoid

Avoid:

- doubled energy
- significant improvement
- cure
- reverse
- activate CcO directly
- anti-aging certainty

Prefer:

- may support
- may be associated with
- is being studied
- may help explain
- may indirectly support recovery
- effects likely vary by context and individual

## Product-layer rule

If a solution layer is included, it must remain:

- secondary
- cautious
- non-deterministic

Do not turn a science explainer into a product funnel.

Safer solution phrasing:

- some far-infrared applications are being studied for their relationship with local circulation, comfort, and recovery
- this is separate from proving that a specific autophagy process has occurred

## Hub placement guidance

For the autophagy article specifically:

Recommended primary homes:

- Aging Hub
- Recovery Hub

Not ideal as primary home:

- Mitochondria Hub

Reason:

- the user intent is more likely to be longevity, fasting, aging, or recovery
- not narrow mitochondrial taxonomy

## Tier 1 publishing criteria

An article qualifies as Tier 1 if it has:

- a strong human hook
- a simple but accurate metaphor
- one central question
- clear AI-readable Quick Answer
- strong internal-link potential
- restrained scientific wording

## XIHE house style implication

This suggests XIHE's strongest future article type may be:

- story-led science explainer

rather than:

- pure dictionary-style science definition

## Template headline logic

The page title should usually be:

- a clear question
- or a short reflective claim

The emotional or vivid story belongs in the article body, not in an overlong headline.

## Operational note

Use this template especially for:

- aging
- fasting
- recovery
- fatigue
- sleep
- behavior change
- inflammation-related personal experience

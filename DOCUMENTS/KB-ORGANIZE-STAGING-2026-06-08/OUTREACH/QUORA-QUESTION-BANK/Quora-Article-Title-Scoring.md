# Quora Article Title Scoring

Status: Active scoring guide
Created: 2026-06-08

## Purpose

Not every Quora question should become a website article.

Use this scoring system to identify the questions that are strong enough for:

1. Article titles.
2. FAQ blocks.
3. Hub expansion.
4. Story-led editorial pieces.

## Scoring Factors

Score each factor 1-5.

| Factor | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Human language clarity | Awkward or vague | Sounds exactly like a real person asks it |
| Evergreen value | Temporary trend | Useful for years |
| Hub fit | Doesn't map well | Clearly fits a XIHE hub |
| Evidence availability | No KB support | Strong KB concept/evidence support |
| Internal-link value | Isolated topic | Connects many hubs |
| AI/GEO potential | Hard for AI to answer/cite | Clear answer with FAQ potential |

## Score Interpretation

| Total Score | Decision |
|---:|---|
| 26-30 | Promote to article title |
| 21-25 | Strong FAQ or article candidate |
| 16-20 | Use for Quora answer only |
| 10-15 | Archive unless strategically useful |
| Below 10 | Ignore |

## What Makes A Great Article Title

Great:

- "Why am I tired even after sleeping 8 hours?"
- "What is ATP in simple terms?"
- "Why does recovery take longer as we age?"
- "How do mitochondria produce energy?"
- "What is the difference between breathing and cellular respiration?"

Weak:

- "Mitochondrial bioenergetic regulatory substrate optimization"
- "Graphene FIR product benefits"
- "Best wellness technology to buy"

## Promotion Rule

If promoted to article, the final website title can be cleaned up slightly, but keep the human wording.

Do not turn human questions into sterile SEO titles.

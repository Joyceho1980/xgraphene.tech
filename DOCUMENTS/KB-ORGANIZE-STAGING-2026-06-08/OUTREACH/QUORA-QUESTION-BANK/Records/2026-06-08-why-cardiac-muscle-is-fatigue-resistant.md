## Exact Quora Question

Why cardiac muscle are nonfatigue?

Why is cardiac muscle fatigue-resistant?

## Why This Question Matters

This question connects directly to the relationship between mitochondria, oxygen delivery, muscle specialization, and fatigue.

It also needs careful wording: cardiac muscle is highly fatigue-resistant under normal physiological conditions, but it is not magically incapable of failure. If oxygen delivery is impaired or cardiac workload exceeds supply, heart muscle can become injured or dysfunctional.

## Quora Answer Draft

Cardiac muscle is better described as **fatigue-resistant**, not truly "nonfatigue."

It can keep working for a lifetime because it is built for continuous aerobic activity.

Several features make this possible:

1. **Very high mitochondrial density**

Heart muscle cells contain many mitochondria. This allows them to regenerate ATP continuously through aerobic metabolism.

2. **Rich oxygen supply**

The heart has a dense capillary network and receives constant blood flow through the coronary circulation. Since cardiac muscle depends heavily on oxygen, delivery is critical.

3. **Mostly aerobic metabolism**

Cardiac muscle relies mainly on oxidative phosphorylation rather than short bursts of anaerobic metabolism. This is efficient for long-duration work.

4. **Rhythmic contraction and relaxation**

The heart does not contract like a skeletal muscle holding a heavy weight continuously. Each beat includes a relaxation phase, which helps preserve blood flow and allows partial recovery between contractions.

5. **Specialized muscle structure**

Cardiac muscle is designed for endurance and rhythm, not voluntary explosive power. Skeletal muscle must do many jobs: sprinting, lifting, posture, fine control, and endurance. That is why skeletal muscle has different fiber types.

But cardiac muscle is not invincible.

If oxygen delivery falls, as in severe coronary blockage, or if workload becomes too high for too long, cardiac muscle can be damaged. So the better statement is:

**Cardiac muscle is highly fatigue-resistant because it has dense mitochondria, strong oxygen supply, and metabolism optimized for continuous rhythmic work.**

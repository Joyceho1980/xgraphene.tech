## Mission

The XIHE English KB exists to make XIHE the most trusted educational authority in:

- Graphene far-infrared science
- Cellular energy
- Mitochondrial health
- ATP and bioenergetics
- Microcirculation
- Recovery science
- Inflammation
- Metabolism
- Sleep
- Healthy aging

## The Core Standard

Build authority before attention.

The KB should help XIHE become citable by humans, search engines, and AI systems because it is clear, structured, restrained, and evidence-aware.

## Editorial Principles

1. Never invent evidence.
2. Never promote weak evidence into strong claims.
3. Never turn story into proof.
4. Never write empty marketing copy.
5. Always preserve the human question.
6. Always separate source, interpretation, claim, and publication.
7. Always keep the claim weaker than or equal to the evidence.

## Human Writing Rule

Human story is part of the system.

The KB should not become sterile SEO machinery. XIHE needs a human voice because science without lived meaning becomes hard to remember.

Story may:

- Open an article.
- Clarify why a question matters.
- Create emotional resonance.
- Help a reader feel understood.

Story must not:

- Prove a scientific claim.
- Imply clinical outcomes.
- Replace citations.
- Turn product use into a medical result.

## Evidence Rule

All new scientific claims use EC classes:

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| EC1 | Human randomized controlled trial |
| EC2 | Human observational or experimental study |
| EC3 | Animal study |
| EC4 | Cellular / in vitro / basic biology |
| EC5 | Material / engineering characterization |
| EC6 | Review / theoretical / context |

Details live in:

`00-STRATEGY/Evidence-Taxonomy-Bridge-EC-System.md`

## Publishing Rule

No important website page should be published without a publishing record in:

`06-PUBLISHING-MAPS`

Minimum requirements:

1. Parent hub.
2. Concept source.
3. Evidence source.
4. EC class.
5. Citation risk.
6. Approved claim language.
7. Quick Answer.
8. FAQ.
9. Internal links.
10. Compliance boundary.

## Growth Rule

New material enters through:

`99-INBOX`

Nothing new should be scattered directly into the KB unless it already has a defined role.

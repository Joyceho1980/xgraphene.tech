## Claim Strength Rules

### EC1 - Human RCT

Allowed:

- "A randomized controlled study reported..."
- "Clinical research observed..."
- "In the studied population..."

Required:

- Sample size.
- Population.
- Study duration.
- Limitation.

Forbidden:

- "Treats disease."
- "Cures."
- "Guaranteed outcome."
- Generalizing beyond the study population.

### EC2 - Human Observational / Experimental

Allowed:

- "A human study observed..."
- "Preliminary human data suggest..."

Required:

- State observational or non-RCT design.
- State limitation.

Forbidden:

- Definitive efficacy claims.

### EC3 - Animal

Allowed:

- "Animal research suggests..."
- "A preclinical study reported..."

Required:

- State animal model.
- Avoid human outcome language.

Forbidden:

- "Works in humans."
- "Treats."
- "Clinical effect."

### EC4 - Cellular / In Vitro

Allowed:

- "Laboratory research suggests..."
- "Cellular studies indicate..."

Required:

- State model type.
- Use plausibility language.

Forbidden:

- Human outcome claims.

### EC5 - Material / Engineering

Allowed:

- "Material testing shows..."
- "Spectral analysis reported..."
- "The measured emissivity was..."

Required:

- Keep claim technical.

Forbidden:

- Health outcome claims.

### EC6 - Review / Theoretical

Allowed:

- "A review describes..."
- "Theoretical models suggest..."

Required:

- Use as context only.

Forbidden:

- Treating as direct product evidence.

## Required Claim Review Questions

Before adding a claim:

1. What exact evidence supports it?
2. What evidence class is it?
3. Is the evidence XIHE-owned, partner-related, independent, or competitor field context?
4. What is the safest public version?
5. What stronger version must never be used?
6. Which website pages can use it?
7. Is a limitation sentence required?

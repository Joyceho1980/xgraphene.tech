# Story to Science Bridge Template

Date created: 2026-06-08
Purpose: Preserve human writing while keeping scientific claims clean.

## Principle

Story is not evidence.

Story helps the reader feel the question. Evidence helps the page answer it responsibly.

## Template

```markdown
# Story Bridge: [Story Title]

Status: Draft / Approved / Used
Story Source:
Target Website Article:
Parent Hub:

## Story Scene

What happened? Keep it specific and human.

## Human Insight

What did this moment reveal?

Examples:

- Energy is not only physical output; it shapes patience, attention, and boundaries.
- Recovery is not laziness; it is biological maintenance.
- Aging is not only time passing; it is a changing energy budget.

## Science Bridge

What scientific concept can help explain the human insight?

Examples:

- Cellular energy.
- ATP demand.
- Mitochondrial function.
- Microcirculation.
- Sleep and recovery.
- Inflammation and energy drain.
```

## Story Rules

1. Keep one insight per story.
2. Do not use story to prove a health outcome.
3. Do not add product claims inside the story.
4. Link story to one primary science article.
5. Keep the reader's dignity intact. No fear, no shame, no exaggeration.

## Best Uses

| Story Type | Best Website Use |
|---|---|
| Founder story | About page, brand philosophy, resonance essays |
| Personal reflection | Journal article intro, LinkedIn, fatigue/recovery articles |
| Caregiving story | Recovery, fatigue, healthy aging |
| Boundary/energy story | Fatigue, cellular energy, stress and recovery |
| Nature metaphor | Graphene FIR, resonance, bioenergetics |

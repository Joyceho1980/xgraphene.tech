# Cellular System Foundation

Status: Active foundation layer
Created: 2026-06-08
Role: Neutral cellular biology backbone for the XIHE Knowledge Base

## Why This Layer Exists

The XIHE KB currently has strong graphene FIR, material science, and application-oriented evidence. But to become a true global authority, XIHE also needs a complete neutral cellular system.

This layer explains how cells work before any technology is introduced.

Graphene FIR belongs later as a possible physical interaction or intervention layer. It should not be the starting point for every biological explanation.

## Core Principle

Cellular biology first.

Graphene FIR second.

XIHE interpretation third.

Website publishing last.

| Mitochondria | How do cells produce ATP and regulate metabolism? |

| Ion Gradients and Membrane Potential | Why do cells need electrical gradients? |

| Microcirculation and Oxygen Delivery | How do oxygen and nutrients reach cells? |
| Metabolism and Nutrient Flux | How do cells choose between storage, repair, and energy production? |
| Autophagy and Cellular Cleanup | How do cells remove damaged components? |
| Sleep and Recovery Biology | Why is recovery an active cellular process? |
| Cellular Aging | Why does cellular function change over time? |
| Systems Integration | How do energy, flow, inflammation, metabolism, and repair interact? |

## What This Layer Must Not Do

Do not use this layer to make product claims.

Do not insert graphene into every concept.

Do not claim that cellular mechanisms prove human outcomes.

Do not treat animal or cellular evidence as clinical proof.

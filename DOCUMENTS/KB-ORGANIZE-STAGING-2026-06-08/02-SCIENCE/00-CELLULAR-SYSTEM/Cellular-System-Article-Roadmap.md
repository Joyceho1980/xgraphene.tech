# Cellular System Article Roadmap

Status: Active roadmap
Created: 2026-06-08
Purpose: Build a complete neutral biology foundation for future website and KB content.

## Article Standard

Each article should include:

1. Human question.
2. Quick Answer.
3. Neutral biology explanation.
4. Evidence references where appropriate.
5. Related concepts.
6. Website hub mapping.
7. Compliance boundary.
8. Optional technology bridge, only after the biology is complete.

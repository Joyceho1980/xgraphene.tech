# Source Register - P0 Cellular Energy Foundation Pack

Status: Active source register
Created: 2026-06-08

## Purpose

This register records neutral biology sources used to build the P0 Cellular Energy Foundation Pack.

These sources support general biology explanations only. They do not support XIHE product claims.

## Source Policy

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Evidence Class | EC6 unless the source is an original experiment |
| Citation Risk | Green for neutral educational use |
| Product Claim Use | Not allowed |
| Technology Bridge Use | Only as background, not as evidence that a technology changes biology |

## Claim Boundary

Allowed:

- "ATP is a central cellular energy carrier."
- "Mitochondria participate in ATP production and cellular metabolism."
- "Oxidative phosphorylation uses electron transport and proton gradients to support ATP synthesis."
- "Microcirculation helps deliver oxygen and nutrients to tissues."

Not allowed:

- "XIHE increases ATP."
- "Graphene FIR improves mitochondrial function in humans."
- "Graphene FIR enhances oxidative phosphorylation."
- "XIHE treats microcirculatory disease."

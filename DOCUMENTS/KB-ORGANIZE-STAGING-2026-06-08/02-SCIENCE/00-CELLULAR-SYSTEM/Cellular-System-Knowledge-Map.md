Status: Active architecture
Created: 2026-06-08

## Master Model

The human body can be understood as a cellular energy system.

Cells must continuously:

1. Produce ATP.
2. Maintain ion gradients.
3. Import oxygen and nutrients.
4. Remove waste.
5. Repair damaged structures.
6. Communicate with neighboring cells.
7. Respond to stress.
8. Balance inflammation and resolution.
9. Adapt to changing energy demand.
10. Preserve function over time.

When these processes are well coordinated, tissues function efficiently.

When they are disrupted, people may experience fatigue, poor recovery, low resilience, sleep disruption, inflammatory burden, metabolic strain, or age-related decline.

This does not mean one cause explains every symptom. It means cellular energy provides a useful organizing lens.

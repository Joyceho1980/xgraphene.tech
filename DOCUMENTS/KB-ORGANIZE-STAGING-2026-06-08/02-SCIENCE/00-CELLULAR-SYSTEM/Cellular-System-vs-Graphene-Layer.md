# Cellular System vs Graphene Layer

Status: Active positioning rule
Created: 2026-06-08

## The Problem

If the KB starts every explanation with graphene, the science can feel product-driven.

That weakens credibility.

The better structure is:

```text
Biology first.
Evidence second.
Technology third.
XIHE interpretation fourth.
```

## Correct Relationship

Graphene FIR is not the cellular system.

Graphene FIR is a material and physical-energy platform that may interact with parts of the cellular system under specific conditions.

## Layer Separation

| Layer | Question | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Cellular System | How does biology work? | How do mitochondria produce ATP? |
| Evidence | What has been observed? | What did a study measure? |
| Technology | What physical mechanism might interact with biology? | What is FIR emissivity? |
| XIHE Interpretation | What does this mean for XIHE's platform? | Why precision FIR engineering matters |
| Website | How do we explain this clearly? | Quick Answer + FAQ + internal links |

## Writing Rule

For core biology pages:

1. Explain the biology without graphene.
2. Link to related biological concepts.
3. If relevant, add a small "Technology Bridge" near the end.
4. Put graphene/FIR in related reading, not as the opening claim.

## Example: Bad Structure

```text
Graphene FIR improves mitochondria because XIHE technology emits 9.4um wavelengths.
```

Why this is weak:

1. Starts with product/technology.
2. Overstates causal certainty.
3. Skips the biology.
4. May imply a human outcome without evidence.

## Example: Better Structure

```text
Mitochondria produce ATP through electron transport and oxidative phosphorylation. This process depends on oxygen delivery, membrane potential, redox balance, and cellular demand. Some research explores whether physical energy modalities, including far-infrared exposure, can influence cellular environments related to mitochondrial function, but evidence strength depends on the study model.
```

Why this is stronger:

1. Biology comes first.
2. Evidence boundary is clear.
3. Technology is contextual, not forced.
4. It is AI-citable and scientifically safer.

## Hub Implication

The website should have two connected but distinct pathways:

### Biology Pathway

Cellular Energy -> ATP -> Mitochondria -> Microcirculation -> Inflammation -> Recovery -> Aging

### Technology Pathway

Graphene FIR -> 9.4um -> Emissivity -> Material Testing -> Biological Research -> Clinical Evidence

They should connect, but not collapse into one.

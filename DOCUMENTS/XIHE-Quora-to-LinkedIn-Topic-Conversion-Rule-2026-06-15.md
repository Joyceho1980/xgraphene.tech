# XIHE Quora -> LinkedIn Topic Conversion Rule

Date: 2026-06-15
Purpose: define how Quora questions should be converted into LinkedIn content topics, angles, and platform-fit outputs.

## Core Principle

Quora captures confusion.

LinkedIn upgrades confusion into insight.

So the system should work like this:

- Quora = what people are asking
- LinkedIn = how to think about what they are asking
- Website = where the deeper mechanism and evidence live

## Main Rule

Do not copy a Quora question directly into LinkedIn as a literal Q&A post.

Instead:

1. identify the human confusion
2. identify the hidden biological or systems tension
3. convert that tension into a LinkedIn-native framing

## What Quora Gives Us

Every good Quora question contains at least one of these:

- a symptom confusion
- a false assumption
- a mechanism curiosity
- a systems-level contradiction

That is the raw material for LinkedIn.

## Conversion Model

### Step 1: Capture the Human Question

Examples:

- Why am I still tired after sleeping?
- Why does inflammation slow healing?
- How does mitochondrial dysfunction affect fatigue?

### Step 2: Find the Hidden Tension

Examples:

- sleep duration vs recovery completion
- inflammation vs resolution timing
- energy production vs energy regulation

### Step 3: Convert Into LinkedIn Framing

Do not keep the question.

Turn it into:

- a statement
- a contradiction
- a systems observation
- a founder insight

## Approved Conversion Patterns

### Pattern 1: Question -> Contradiction

Quora:

- Why am I still tired after sleeping?

LinkedIn:

- Sleep duration does not guarantee recovery.
- Fatigue after sleep is often a recovery completion problem, not a time problem.

Best for:

- Founder personal profile
- System Thinker posts

### Pattern 2: Question -> Mechanism Reframe

Quora:

- Why does inflammation slow healing?

LinkedIn:

- Inflammation is not the problem. Failed resolution is.
- Recovery depends not only on repair, but on whether the inflammatory phase resolves on time.

Best for:

- Founder science posts
- Company page science translation posts

### Pattern 3: Question -> System Insight

Quora:

- How does mitochondrial dysfunction affect fatigue?

LinkedIn:

- Fatigue is not just a lack-of-energy story.
- In many cases, it is an energy regulation story.

Best for:

- Founder science observer posts
- Educational credibility posts

### Pattern 4: Question -> Industry Implication

Quora:

- Can physical modalities influence immune response?

LinkedIn:

- Recovery tools should not be framed only as comfort technologies.
- The real question is whether a recovery environment can influence biological regulation in meaningful ways.

Best for:

- Founder strategy posts
- Company page positioning posts

## Platform Routing Rule

Not every Quora-derived topic should go to the same LinkedIn destination.

### Founder Profile

Use when the topic becomes:

- a system observation
- a founder-level thought
- a cross-domain biological insight
- a philosophical or strategic interpretation

Examples:

- sleep duration vs recovery completion
- biology rewards time, not speed
- fatigue as an energy regulation story

### Company Page

Use when the topic becomes:

- a research-backed mechanism summary
- an application translation
- an evidence-led industry statement
- a science-to-standard explanation

Examples:

- inflammatory resolution in recovery environments
- M1/M2 as a recovery biology concept
- graphene/FIR relevance only after mechanism is clearly framed

## Topic Priority Rule

Not every Quora question deserves LinkedIn adaptation.

Adapt only if the question has at least one of these:

- high repetition
- strong XIHE relevance
- clear hidden tension
- ability to become a system insight

Do not adapt if the question is:

- too basic
- too narrow and consumer-specific
- impossible to elevate without sounding generic

## Writing Shift Rule

Quora tone:

- direct
- explanatory
- step by step
- neutral scientific answer

LinkedIn tone:

- higher-level
- more selective
- less explanatory
- more framing-driven

So the conversion is:

- from answer -> insight
- from explanation -> interpretation
- from symptom -> system

## Output Types

Each strong Quora question can generate up to 3 LinkedIn outputs:

### 1. Founder System Thinker

Use the contradiction or hidden tension.

Example:

- The market measures recovery in time.
- Biology measures it in completion.

### 2. Founder Science Observer

Use the mechanism carefully.

Example:

- Recovery is not only structural.
- It is also a timing problem inside immune regulation.

### 3. Company Page Science Translation

Use the evidence or industry implication.

Example:

- Inflammation is not inherently negative in recovery.
- The key issue is whether the system transitions from defense into resolution on time.

## Do Not Do This

Do not:

- paste Quora answers into LinkedIn
- open a LinkedIn post with the exact Quora question
- keep Quora's explanatory paragraph structure
- turn LinkedIn into FAQ format

Reason:

Quora rewards explanation.

LinkedIn rewards recognition and framing.

|---|---|---|---|

| inflammation / healing | response vs resolution | mechanism reframe | founder or company |

## Workflow

For every strong Quora question:

1. save the question
2. identify hidden tension
3. decide if it is adaptable
4. route to founder or company page
5. rewrite into LinkedIn-native framing
6. optionally route deeper mechanism to KB or website page

## One-Sentence Summary

Quora tells us what people are asking; LinkedIn should tell them what that question really means at the systems level.

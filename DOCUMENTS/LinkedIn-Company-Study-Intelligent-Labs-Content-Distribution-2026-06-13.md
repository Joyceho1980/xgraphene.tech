# LinkedIn Company Page Study: Intelligent Labs Content Distribution

Date: 2026-06-13

## Sample reviewed

Visible organic posts on the Intelligent Labs company page were reviewed as a batch, including multiple recent posts on:

- kids probiotics and prebiotics
- CoQ10 and cellular energy
- magnesium formulations
- persistent fatigue and mitochondrial function
- immune support and sleep
- consistency and daily wellness routines
- probiotic stability and formulation

## Distribution pattern observed

The page does not behave like a pure science feed.

It consistently blends:

- light science explanation
- practical daily-life application
- soft system-level value framing

## Rough content balance

Observed across the sampled posts:

- Technology / science: medium to high presence
- Application / routine / lifestyle: very high presence
- System value / business framing: moderate but secondary

In practical terms, the page feels closer to:

- 20-30% mechanism
- 50-60% application
- 10-20% system value

## Dominant layer analysis

When each post is scored by its main emphasis, the page skews toward:

- application: 9 posts
- technology: 3 posts
- system value: 1 post

Important caveat:

- technology is usually embedded inside the application story
- system value is often implied rather than stated loudly
- the page is intentionally practical, not academically split

## Main structural pattern

Most posts follow this shape:

1. human problem
2. practical wellness context
3. light mechanism
4. nutrient or product example
5. soft benefit framing
6. gentle CTA or question

## What makes the distribution work

- The reader never feels trapped in technical detail.
- The science is always translated into routine value.
- The page keeps moving toward everyday usability.
- The company feels like a wellness support system, not a lecture series.

## XIHE adaptation lesson

XIHE should use the same translation logic, but with its own category:

- graphene science
- recovery application
- system value

The page should feel like a recovery system company, not a materials-only page.

## Working conclusion

The account's real strength is not one post.

It is the repeated pattern of:

science -> practical life -> soft business relevance

# Decision: The LinkedIn Template Rule Behind Swipe Files

Date: 2026-06-08

## Observation

The existing `LINKEDIN-CONTENT` templates and swipe files suggest a deeper rule:

LinkedIn posts are not mini-articles.

They are discovery hooks built around one memorable idea.

## The real rule

The reader should remember:

- the idea
- the problem
- the shift in understanding

Not:

- the product
- the technical spec
- the company pitch

## What the templates are actually teaching

Across the current files, the repeated pattern is:

1. Hook
2. Reader problem or larger trend
3. Scientific or research-backed frame
4. Personal or practical bridge
5. Simple takeaway
6. Gentle link to website or KB

This is true even when the visible structures look different.

## Core platform logic

LinkedIn rewards:

- clarity
- identity
- one strong idea
- relevance to a professional audience
- human voice with light evidence

It does not reward:

- dense explanation
- multi-claim educational dumping
- product-first copy

## XIHE interpretation

For XIHE, the strongest LinkedIn rule should be:

Lead with:

- a human observation
- a real problem
- a changed understanding
- or a forward-looking industry shift

Then support it with:

- one scientific thread
- one evidence cue
- one practical takeaway

Then stop.

Do not try to compress the full article into the post.

## The deepest pattern in the swipe material

The swipe file examples repeatedly use this movement:

- old assumption
- new understanding
- one piece of evidence
- why it matters in real life

This is more important than the exact format.

## Important warning

Do not copy the external style too literally.

Some swipe-file posts use:

- dramatic all-caps hooks
- trend language
- future-of framing
- heavy confidence

This can work in general B2B LinkedIn, but XIHE operates closer to health and science trust.

So XIHE should borrow:

- structure
- rhythm
- clarity
- story-to-science flow

But should soften:

- hype
- certainty
- sweeping claims

## Practical XIHE rule

Each LinkedIn post should answer:

1. What is the one thing the reader should remember?
2. What human problem makes this idea matter?
3. What is the one scientific thread that supports it?
4. What is the calm, non-sales ending?

If a post tries to teach three or four ideas at once, it should become a website article instead.

## Best XIHE formula

For most posts:

- story or sharp question
- reframed understanding
- one science explanation
- one takeaway
- one link

## Example

Not ideal:

- mitochondrial function
- autophagy
- FIR
- microcirculation
- aging
- product

all in one short post

Better:

- I used to think self-discipline was willpower.
- Learning about energy changed that.
- Meal timing and recovery biology may shape how we feel the next morning.
- Sometimes understanding works better than forcing.
- full article link

## Final rule

Study other posts for:

- mechanism of attention

Not for:

- exact wording
- exact confidence level
- exact emotional tone

XIHE should adapt the structure, not clone the persona.

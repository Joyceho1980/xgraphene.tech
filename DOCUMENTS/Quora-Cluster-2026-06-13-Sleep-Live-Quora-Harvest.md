The Sleep hub should continue to prioritize the questions that express a human experience, not just a sleep fact.

1. Why do I still feel tired after enough sleep?

3. Why does sleep affect recovery?
4. What happens during deep sleep?
5. What happens if you regularly only get 3 hours of sleep per night?

## What to keep out of the Sleep hub

The following are real Quora questions, but they are not strong Sleep hub anchors:

- dream interpretation questions
- grammar questions
- body position questions
- children or pet safety edge cases

Those can stay as answer-only items or be archived for later.

## Strategic note

The strongest Sleep questions are rarely about sleep length alone.

They are about:

- waking up unrestored
- recovery that feels incomplete
- energy that does not match time asleep
- the body feeling harder to reset than before

That is the language the hub should keep using.

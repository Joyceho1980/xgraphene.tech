# Decision: Personal Experience Case Study Article Strategy

Date: 2026-06-08

## Context

The user evaluated a story-led article about itching, a graphene FIR pad, and an attempt to understand the possible mechanism behind symptom relief.

This article type is strategically important because it combines:

- original lived experience
- strong reader attention
- natural question framing
- potential knowledge graph expansion

## Decision Summary

Do not delete this kind of article.

Instead, classify it as a:

- Personal Experience Case Study

Use it inside a science hub with strong medical-risk controls, internal links, and careful wording.

## Title Strategy

Current long emotional title may work for social platforms, but it is too long and too narrative-heavy for a Knowledge Hub page title.

Recommended Hub-style titles:

- Can Far-Infrared Help Relieve Itching?
- Why Did My Itching Improve After Using a Graphene FIR Pad?

Rule:

- H1 should be concise, searchable, and question-based
- the full personal story should move into the article body

## Best Hub Placement

Primary candidates:

- Inflammation Hub
- Recovery Hub

Recommended primary home:

- Inflammation Hub

Reason:

- itching is more naturally legible through inflammation and local discomfort
- easier to connect to mechanistic education
- easier to control medical wording

Secondary cross-links:

- Microcirculation
- Cellular Energy
- Graphene FIR
- Recovery

## Knowledge Graph Connections

This article should not remain isolated.

Recommended conceptual path:

- Itching
- Inflammation
- Microcirculation
- Cellular Energy
- Recovery
- Graphene FIR technology

Recommended "Related Reading" block:

- What Is Inflammation?
- Understanding Microcirculation
- How Cellular Energy Supports Recovery
- Graphene Far-Infrared Technology Explained

## Content Value Assessment

This article type can be more strategically valuable than generic definition content such as:

- What Is ATP?

Reason:

- generic science definitions are widely available on the web
- original lived experience is harder to copy
- story-led question pages may create stronger reader engagement
- they can still support the broader knowledge graph if properly linked

## XIHE Interpretation

The highest-value content mix is not:

- only foundational explainers

It is:

- foundational explainers
- original story-led case studies
- Quora-style human questions
- evidence review pages

## Risk Control

Current medical-risk assessment for this article type remains moderate unless strengthened.

Needed safeguards:

- explicit uncertainty
- no direct causal claims from anecdote
- no universal symptom promise
- caution that not all itching has the same cause
- avoid implying diagnosis or treatment

## Editorial Pattern

Recommended page structure:

1. H1
2. Quick Answer
3. Scientific framing
4. Personal story
5. Possible mechanisms
6. Limits and caution
7. FAQ
8. Related Reading

## Strategic Note

This may become one of XIHE's most distinctive assets because it is:

- personal
- memorable
- difficult to replicate
- more human than generic educational pages

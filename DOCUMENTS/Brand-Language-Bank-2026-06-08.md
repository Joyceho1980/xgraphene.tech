# XIHE Brand Language Bank

Date: 2026-06-08

## Purpose

Collect the lines that feel emotionally true, brand-consistent, and reusable across:

- homepage
- about page
- LinkedIn
- founder story
- brand manifesto
- campaign copy

These are not hype lines.

They are XIHE's core language assets.

## Tier 1 Gold Line

We may not be able to cure everything.

But helping people recover a little more energy,
a little more comfort,
and a little more quality of life —
is meaningful.

## Why this line works

- honest
- restrained
- human
- emotionally credible
- medically safer than exaggerated promises

## Best use

Use in:

- About page
- founder statement
- brand manifesto
- homepage philosophy block
- LinkedIn pinned introduction

Do not use as:

- aggressive product CTA
- discount campaign slogan
- hard-sell ad headline

## Short companion lines

- Cellular Energy Matters.

## Expanded brand line

We believe health is not only about living longer.

It is about having the energy to enjoy the years you have.

## Functional brand line

If we can help someone sleep a little better,
move a little easier,
recover a little faster,
and live with a little more energy —
that matters.

## Tone rule

Preferred qualities:

- warm
- calm
- serious
- supportive
- non-miraculous

Avoid:

- world-leading miracle language
- exaggerated certainty
- cure-all tone
- flashy anti-aging claims

## Writing rule

If a line sounds moving because it tells the truth plainly, keep it.

If a line sounds impressive because it promises too much, remove it.

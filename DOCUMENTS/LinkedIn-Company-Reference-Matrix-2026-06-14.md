# XIHE LinkedIn Company Reference Matrix

Date: 2026-06-14

## How to use this matrix

Choose the surface you want XIHE Company Page to feel like, then borrow the corresponding pattern.

## Reference accounts

### 1. Intelligent Labs

Best for:

- application-first wellness posts
- light mechanism inside practical routines
- soft daily-life use cases

Borrow:

- human problem -> practical use -> light science -> soft CTA

Do not copy:

- supplement-specific phrasing
- product-heavy repetition

### 2. Gaia Herbs

Best for:

- brand warmth
- community tone
- retail or rollout announcements

Borrow:

- mission language
- human-friendly brand tone
- simple, accessible wellness framing

Do not copy:

- herbal product positioning
- category-specific consumer language

### 3. Thorne

Best for:

- institutional credibility
- expert presence
- event and conference signaling

Borrow:

- authority signals
- science-linked positioning
- external validation

Do not copy:

- supplement authority tone without adaptation
- overly broad wellness claims

### 4. Ritual

Best for:

- proof-led positioning
- standards and transparency
- founder / leadership narrative

Borrow:

- proof over promises
- trust architecture
- clear philosophy language

Do not copy:

- direct supplement-brand storytelling
- narrow product authority framing

## XIHE choice by post type

### If the post is about recovery value

Use the Intelligent Labs pattern:

- human problem
- practical use
- light mechanism
- soft CTA

### If the post is about institutional trust

Use the Thorne pattern:

- expert signal
- event or collaboration
- credibility
- industry relevance

### If the post is about standards and proof

Use the Ritual pattern:

- proof
- transparency
- philosophy
- trust architecture

### If the post is about community or rollout

Use the Gaia Herbs pattern:

- brand warmth
- accessibility
- mission language
- everyday use case

## XIHE default synthesis

XIHE Company Page should combine:

- Intelligent Labs for application clarity
- Thorne for credibility
- Ritual for proof and standards
- Gaia Herbs for warmth

But the final voice must remain XIHE:

- graphene science
- recovery applications
- system value
- calm institutional trust

## Working rule

Do not ask which benchmark to imitate by habit.

Choose based on the post goal:

- application
- credibility
- proof
- warmth

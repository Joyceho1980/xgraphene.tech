# XIHE Signature Writing Style Guide

Date: 2026-06-15
Owner: XIHE Content Operations
Applies to: Founder-level communication, especially Joyce Ho LinkedIn posts

## Core Principle

All founder-level content must satisfy three rules:

### 1. No Over-Explaining

The goal is not to lecture.

The goal is to describe a system.

Avoid:

- "This means that..."
- "It works by... therefore..."

Prefer:

- "What we observe is..."
- "This suggests..."
- "The system appears to..."

### 2. No Premature Certainty

This rule is especially important for biology, medicine, and mechanism-related content.

Avoid:

- proves
- treats
- is effective for

Prefer:

- associated with
- observed alongside
- may influence

### 3. Preserve the Feeling of Ongoing Research

Content should remain inside a state of ongoing understanding.

The voice should sound like careful work in progress, not final doctrinal truth.

### Avoid List

These words weaken scientific credibility:

- cure
- treat
- heal
- breakthrough
- miracle
- revolutionary
- guarantees
- proven
- works for everyone

## Structure Templates

### System Thinker Template

Use for:

- trends
- AI vs biology
- macro observations
- industry shifts

Structure:

1. global shift
2. contradiction
3. system explanation
4. insight

Template:

AI makes X faster.
But Y does not follow speed.
These are different systems.
The gap is where the real work happens.

### Science Observer Template

Use for:

- papers
- mechanisms
- cytokines
- FIR
- macrophage polarization

Structure:

1. biological problem
2. known mechanism
3. emerging literature
4. careful framing
5. open question

Template:

In biology, X is understood as a dynamic process.
It involves A -> B -> C transitions.
Recent preclinical studies suggest...
However, the exact boundary conditions remain unclear.
The key question is where modulation begins.

### Founder Philosopher Template

Use for:

- brand philosophy
- patience
- liubai
- meaning
- time and signal

Structure:

1. observation
2. contradiction
3. key concept word
4. minimal reflection

Template:

The system rewards speed.
Biology rewards time.

留白.

Not emptiness.
A space where signals appear.

## Tone Control

### Sentence Length Rule

- 70% short sentences
- 20% medium sentences
- 10% long sentences

LinkedIn readability comes from rhythm, not complexity.

### Founder Voice Rules

- each paragraph should stay within 3 to 5 lines
- each post should carry no more than one core concept word, such as 留白 or inflammatory window
- no more than one rhetorical question per post

## Content Architecture

Default weekly system:

- Tuesday: System Thinker -> reach / visibility
- Thursday: Science Observer -> credibility
- Sunday: Founder Philosopher -> brand memory

## Brand Positioning Effect

If used consistently for 4 to 8 weeks, this system should gradually create three external impressions:

- he thinks in systems
- he understands biology deeply but cautiously
- he has a distinct philosophy layer built around time, signal, and liubai

## One Sentence Summary

You are not writing content.

You are building a recognizable thought system.

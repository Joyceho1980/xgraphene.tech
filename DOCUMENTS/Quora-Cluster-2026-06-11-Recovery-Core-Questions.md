# Quora Cluster: Recovery Core Questions

Date: 2026-06-11

## Cluster Goal

Capture the human language around recovery so the Recovery hub can answer the real question people are asking:

Why does the body not bounce back the way it used to?

This cluster should feed:

- Recovery hub expansion
- Sleep hub cross-links
- Fatigue bridge pages
- Cellular Energy bridge pages

## Core Human Pattern

Most recovery questions are not really about exercise alone.

They are about:

- not bouncing back after effort
- needing more rest than before
- feeling worse the next day
- recovery taking longer with age
- sleep not fully restoring energy

## Priority Questions

### Tier 1

1. Why does recovery take so long?
2. Why do I need more recovery than before?
3. Why do I feel worse the day after exercise?
4. What does poor recovery feel like?
5. Why do I feel like I never fully recover?

### Tier 2

6. Why does recovery get harder with age?
7. Why does sleep not fully restore me?
8. How long should recovery take after a hard workout?
9. What is recovery capacity?
10. Why does stress slow recovery?
11. Can cellular energy affect recovery?
12. Can poor recovery be linked to mitochondria?

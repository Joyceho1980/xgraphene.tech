# LinkedIn Article Template

Date: 2026-06-13

## Purpose

Use LinkedIn as a recognition and routing layer.

The article should create a feeling of:

- "This is about me"
- "This is useful"
- "I want the deeper explanation"

## Recommended structure

### Title

Use a human question or tension.

Examples:

- Why does a normal workday sometimes feel more exhausting than exercise?
- Why does recovery sometimes feel slower than effort?
- What is fatigue actually telling us?

### Opening

Start with lived experience.

Keep it concrete.

Example pattern:

- You sit all day.
- You do not think of it as heavy labor.
- But by evening, the body still feels spent.

### Middle

Add one scientific layer, but keep it light.

Example pattern:

- attention
- decisions
- stress
- adaptation
- energy demand

### Open loop

Leave the reader with one unanswered layer.

Example:

There is a deeper layer to how cellular energy and recovery interact.

That is where the full pattern becomes easier to see.

### CTA

Send readers to the website for the deeper explanation.

Example:

We explored this in more detail in our Knowledge Hub:

https://www.xgraphene.tech/SCIENCE/KNOWLEDGE/Mitochondria/what-is-mitochondrial-dysfunction.html

### Closing question

End with a simple question.

Example:

What drains you more: physical work, or sustained mental load?

## Writing rules

- one idea per article
- short paragraphs
- calm, intelligent tone
- no white-paper voice
- no ad voice
- no full mechanism on LinkedIn
- website link appears near the end

## Claim boundary

Use measured language only.

Prefer:

- suggests
- observed
- may help explain
- associated with

Avoid:

- cure
- treat
- reverse
- guarantee
- heal

## Goal

LinkedIn should create curiosity and trust.

The website should deliver the full answer.

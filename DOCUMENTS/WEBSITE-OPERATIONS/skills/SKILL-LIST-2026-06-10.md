## Recommended Content Pattern

For clarity-first pages such as `Are Mitochondria a Disease?`, use:

- left column: title, subtitle, and short clarification copy
- right column: hero image and one short caption
- keep the image clean and emotionally aligned with the article question
- avoid placing long labels inside the image

## Working Rules

When a website workflow becomes repeatable, convert it into:

1. a written operating rule
2. a reusable skill
3. a project checklist document

## Current Website Checklist

- confirm page type
- confirm hero role
- keep the hero visually clean
- keep long explanations in the body
- verify SEO title, description, canonical, OG, and Twitter tags
- verify internal links to related pages
- check image paths and asset placement
- preview before publish

# Decision: Hero Image Agent v2 Becomes a Science Storytelling Visual Director

Date: 2026-06-10

## Decision

The hero image workflow for XIHE Knowledge Hub is no longer treated as a literal hero image generator.

It is now treated as a science storytelling visual director.

## Why

The earlier approach was still too close to:

- literal science illustration
- infographic behavior
- keyword-matching visuals

That produced technically acceptable images, but the pages still felt too much like:

- blog posts
- medical notes
- generic science pages

The new direction is to generate the image for the human search behind the article.

## Core Rule

The reader is not searching for mitochondria.

The reader is searching for their energy.

Generate the image for the search, not the science.

## Visual Priority Stack

1. Human emotion
2. Human experience
3. Story
4. Atmosphere
5. Science

## Required Hidden Question Step

Before generating any hero image, identify the hidden human question behind the title.

Examples:

- `Are Mitochondria a Disease?` -> `Am I sick?`
- `What Is Mitochondrial Dysfunction?` -> `Why do I feel different?`
- `What Does Mitochondrial Disease Feel Like?` -> `Is anyone else experiencing this?`
- `How Do You Find Out If You Have a Mitochondrial Disease?` -> `How do I get answers?`

## Visual States Allowed

Each hero must clearly express one main state:

- curiosity
- fatigue
- recovery
- vitality
- resilience

## What This Changes

- hero images should be chosen around the human question, not the science keyword
- diagnosis pages should show search, reading, waiting, or decision points
- dysfunction pages should show lived energy slowdown rather than cell diagrams
- clarification pages should show distinction and uncertainty resolution rather than healing imagery

## Expected Result

This should raise the visual quality of the XIHE Knowledge Hub from:

- competent science visuals

to:

- science storytelling visuals with stronger emotional and editorial authority

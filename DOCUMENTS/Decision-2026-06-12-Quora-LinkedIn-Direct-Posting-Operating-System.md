# Decision: Quora and LinkedIn Direct Posting Operating System

Date: 2026-06-12

## Core decision

XIHE should treat Quora and LinkedIn as direct publishing surfaces, but not as the source of truth.

The source of truth remains the KB.

The working order is:

1. KB question or evidence note
2. website article or answer framework
3. Quora reply
4. LinkedIn derivative post
5. record the result back into the KB

## What “direct posting” means

Direct posting means:

- the final copy is written for the target platform
- the post is published natively on that platform
- the KB still stores the source logic, claim boundaries, and link map

It does not mean:

- copying the same text everywhere
- turning LinkedIn into a mini website article
- letting Quora or LinkedIn become the source of scientific truth

## Platform roles

### Quora

Role:

- question discovery
- answer distribution
- human language capture
- topic validation

Best use:

- answer existing questions
- cluster repeated questions
- turn strong questions into website articles later

Quora is best when the answer:

- is self-contained
- is direct in the first 2 to 4 sentences
- includes one clear rationale
- keeps evidence boundaries visible

### LinkedIn

Role:

- audience entry point
- authority signaling
- short-form distribution
- founder or company voice

Best use:

- drive attention back to the website or KB logic
- frame one idea clearly
- avoid over-explaining

LinkedIn should never make a stronger claim than the website.

## Workflow

### 1. Capture

Record:

- exact Quora question or LinkedIn theme
- source URL if relevant
- hub category
- claim boundary
- article potential

### 2. Map

Assign the item to one of the following:

- `Industry Insight`
- `Case Study`
- `Founder Narrative`
- `Foundation Explainer`
- `Question Answer`

### 3. Draft

Use the KB to draft the canonical answer first.

Then derive:

- Quora reply
- LinkedIn short post
- optional article

### 4. Publish

Post natively on the platform.

For each post, keep the copy platform-native:

- Quora = answer first
- LinkedIn = hook first

### 5. Record

After publishing, log:

- platform
- title or question
- URL
- date
- derivative used
- performance note

## Content Families

### Industry Insight

Best use:

- company account
- research summary
- market observation
- science-backed trend framing

Purpose:

- show XIHE understands the category
- turn science into business relevance

Structure:

1. one market or science observation
2. one interpretation
3. one practical implication
4. one calm closing line

Good topics:

- recovery market shifts
- sleep and energy behavior
- microcirculation and recovery workflow
- graphene FIR as a support layer in a broader recovery system

### Case Study

Best use:

- company account when the evidence is formal
- founder account when the story is personal and honest

Purpose:

- show how a pattern appears in real life
- connect science to lived experience or workflow

Important rule:

- do not present third-party stories as XIHE’s own
- use composites when rights or context are unclear
- do not turn anecdote into proof

Structure:

1. context
2. the problem pattern
3. what changed or was observed
4. what remains uncertain
5. why it matters

### Founder Narrative

Best use:

- founder account
- first-person reflection
- internal questioning

Purpose:

- create human trust
- make the brand memorable
- show why the topic matters personally

Good topics:

- why energy changed how I think
- what I learned from repeated fatigue questions
- why recovery became a serious topic
- what studying mitochondria changed in my own thinking

Important rule:

- founder narrative is not scientific proof
- keep it honest and specific
- keep product talk secondary

## KB Planning Rules

Every important topic should eventually have:

- concept note
- evidence note
- question bank entry
- article framework
- LinkedIn derivative map
- Quora answer draft
- claim boundary

## Recommended mapping

### Quora -> KB -> Website -> LinkedIn

Use when:

- the question is strong
- the phrasing is human
- the topic is reusable

### KB -> LinkedIn -> Quora

Use when:

- a strong scientific idea already exists
- the post needs distribution first
- the Quora question is still being discovered

## Practical posting rule

Quora answer:

- answer the question directly
- add one plain explanation
- end with a careful boundary

LinkedIn post:

- lead with the human hook
- state one insight
- stop early
- link back to the website or KB article only if helpful

## Channel-specific guidance

### Quora

- answer questions, do not pitch
- use plain language
- keep evidence visible
- use the KB as the source of truth

### LinkedIn

- simplify, do not amplify
- one idea per post
- founder voice for lived experience
- company voice for research and industry insight

## What to avoid

- posting the same long article everywhere
- making LinkedIn sound like a white paper
- making Quora sound like an ad
- claiming product outcomes without evidence
- using story as proof

## Operating conclusion

XIHE can run a clean content machine if each layer has one job:

- KB = thinking and truth control
- website = durable authority
- Quora = question discovery and answer distribution
- LinkedIn = short-form reach and trust building

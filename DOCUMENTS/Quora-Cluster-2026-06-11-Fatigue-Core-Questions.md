# Quora Cluster: Fatigue Core Questions

# Date: 2026-06-11

## Cluster Goal

Capture the human language around fatigue so the Fatigue hub can answer the question people actually ask:

Why am I always tired?

This cluster should feed:

- Fatigue hub expansion
- Sleep and Recovery cross-links
- Cellular Energy bridge pages
- FAQ blocks and short Quora answers

## Core Human Pattern

Most fatigue questions are not really about being "busy" or "lazy."

They are about:

- waking up tired
- feeling tired after sleep
- crashing after lunch
- getting more irritable when energy is low
- feeling drained by small tasks

## Priority Questions

### Tier 1

1. Why am I always tired?
2. Why am I tired even after sleeping 8 hours?
3. Why do I feel tired after sleeping longer?
4. Why do I feel tired after lunch?
5. Why do small things make me irritable when I’m tired?

### Tier 2

6. Why do some people seem to have endless energy?
7. Why does fatigue get worse when sleep is poor?
8. Can low cellular energy cause fatigue?
9. Can poor recovery make fatigue worse?
10. What is the difference between tiredness and fatigue?
11. Why do I feel tired when nothing seems wrong?
12. Can fatigue be linked to mitochondria?

# Decision: Mitochondria Explore Band Separation

Date: 2026-06-09

## Change

The "Explore This Hub" section was moved into the `card-band` treatment so it reads as a distinct section instead of visually blending into "Why This Matters."

## Reason

- The page needed a clearer section break.
- Readers should immediately see that the Explore block is the next step in the reading path.
- The change improves scanning and makes the Hub feel more structured.

## Reusable Rule

For long-form Hub pages, use a different background treatment or a clear band break between conceptual explanation sections and navigation/pathway sections.

# Decision: Mitochondria Hub Value Loop

Date: 2026-06-09

## What Changed

The XIHE Mitochondria Hub was updated to include a clearer value loop:

1. Explain what mitochondria are.
2. Separate mitochondrial dysfunction from mitochondrial disease.
3. Explain diagnosis, seriousness, and management.
4. Add an explicit bridge to graphene far-infrared research as a secondary topic.

## Content Principle

The page should not imply that graphene far-infrared cures or repairs mitochondrial disease.

Preferred bridge language:

- Graphene far-infrared may be relevant to circulation, recovery, and mitochondrial-function-related pathways.
- It is a research and support topic, not a cure claim.
- It should be presented after the medical boundaries are clear.

## Implementation Note

Added a new section to the hub page:

- "Why This Leads to Graphene FIR"

This section closes the knowledge loop by guiding readers from mitochondria and disease boundaries into the related far-infrared research page.

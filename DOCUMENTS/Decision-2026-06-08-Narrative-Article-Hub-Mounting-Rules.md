# Decision: Narrative Article Hub Mounting Rules

Date: 2026-06-08

## Purpose

Narrative articles should not float outside the XIHE Knowledge Hub structure.

They must still belong to a subject hub, while also carrying a recognizable article type.

## Core model

Each narrative article has:

1. one Primary Hub
2. zero to three Secondary Hub links
3. one Article Type label
4. one Related Reading block

## Rule 1: Choose the Primary Hub by the reader's main question

Do not choose the hub by the product mentioned in the story.

Choose the hub by the article's main educational intent.

Examples:

- itching, irritation, local discomfort -> Inflammation Hub
- better sleep, recovery after fatigue, rest quality -> Recovery Hub
- long-term habits, self-regulation, aging, energy conservation -> Healthy Aging Hub
- mitochondria as the main educational topic -> Mitochondria Hub
- meal timing, appetite, glucose handling, metabolic state -> Metabolism Hub

## Rule 2: Secondary Hubs are for mechanism bridges

After selecting the primary home, add secondary links based on concepts used in the explanation.

Common bridge logic:

- Inflammation -> Microcirculation
- Microcirculation -> Cellular Energy
- Cellular Energy -> Recovery
- Recovery -> Healthy Aging
- Mitochondria -> ATP
- Metabolism -> Mitochondria
- Sleep -> Recovery
- Stress -> Recovery
- Stress -> Healthy Aging

Use only the hubs that are genuinely discussed in the article.

## Rule 3: Article Type is independent from Hub

Recommended narrative labels:

- Personal Energy Journal
- Personal Experience Case Study
- Narrative Analysis
- Founder Reflection

Use labels to describe style and editorial role, not scientific topic.

## Rule 4: Related Reading must follow the story's explanation path

Do not attach random links.

Related Reading should help the reader move from:

- story
- to mechanism
- to broader concept

Recommended pattern:

1. one definition page
2. one mechanism page
3. one adjacent system page
4. one broader recovery/aging page
5. one technology page only if relevant and carefully phrased

## Rule 5: Product mention never determines hub placement

If a story mentions a graphene FIR product, that does not automatically make the page belong to Graphene FIR or Technology Platform.

Product references should remain secondary unless the main article is specifically about the technology itself.

## Rule 6: Protect scientific clarity

Narrative article structure should separate:

- what happened
- what the author felt
- what science may help explain
- what remains uncertain

Do not let the story perform the job of evidence.

## Decision tree

Ask in this order:

1. What is the reader really asking about?
2. What system best explains the story?
3. Which hub should own that system?
4. Which other hubs help complete the explanation?
5. Which label best describes the article style?

## Final rule

Hub decides:

- topical authority
- knowledge graph location
- internal linking role

Article Type decides:

- tone
- structure
- editorial expectation

This allows XIHE to keep a clean knowledge graph while still building a distinctive human voice.

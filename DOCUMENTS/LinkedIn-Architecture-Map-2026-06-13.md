# XIHE LinkedIn Architecture Map

Date: 2026-06-13

## System Overview

XIHE LinkedIn should operate as a distributed cognition system, not as one blended content stream.

The roles are:

- Founder profile
- Company page (IKKEM/XIHE)
- Website knowledge hub
- Content agents
- Sales / CRM follow-up

## Core principle

- Founder creates meaning
- Company creates trust
- Website creates structure
- Agents create scale

## Role split

### Founder profile

Use for:

- thought leadership
- cross-domain thinking
- narrative insight
- comments and discussions

Do not use for:

- company announcements
- product updates
- institutional reporting

### Company page

Use for:

- institutional trust
- research updates
- technology milestones
- collaboration announcements

Do not use for:

- personal thinking
- casual daily posts
- opinion-led content

Recommended content structure:

- 40% technology: graphene, FIR, mechanism, studies
- 40% application: recovery, clinic, sports, wellness
- 20% system value: business model, differentiation, outcomes

Fallback if needed:

- 50% graphene science
- 30% recovery applications
- 20% business and system narrative

Translation rule:

- translate graphene into recovery value
- do not isolate graphene as an academic subject
- position XIHE as a recovery system company

### Website knowledge hub

Use for:

- SEO landing pages
- structured knowledge
- AI-readable content graph
- conversion entry point

### Agents

Use for:

- keyword mining
- content generation
- LinkedIn posting
- comment engagement
- lead capture
- CRM update

## Distribution rule

- Founder: 70%
- Company page: 20%
- Website: 10%

## Content flow

Quora / LinkedIn feed

→ Founder post

→ Comment engagement

→ Website knowledge hub

→ CRM / sales follow-up

→ outbound

## Linking rule

LinkedIn → website links should appear only when they function as:

1. concept extension
2. hub entry
3. deep mechanism page

The link should feel like continuation, not conclusion.

## Agent rule

Agents must never:

- mix founder voice with company voice
- post scientific explanation from the personal profile
- use the website as an unstructured blog

Agents must:

- route content by intent
- preserve the role split
- keep the ecosystem coherent

## Working conclusion

This structure turns LinkedIn into a routing layer for the XIHE knowledge graph, not just a posting channel.

# Quora Cluster: Sleep and Recovery Core Questions

Date: 2026-06-10

## Cluster Goal

Use Quora to capture the human language around sleep, tiredness, and recovery so the Sleep hub can answer the real question people are actually asking.

This cluster should feed:

- Sleep hub expansion
- Recovery hub connections
- Cellular Energy bridge pages
- FAQ blocks for Sleep / Recovery / Fatigue

## Core Human Pattern

Most sleep questions are not really about sleep duration.

They are about:

- waking up tired
- sleep that does not feel restorative
- poor recovery after stress or exertion
- low energy that does not match the amount of sleep

## Priority Questions

### Tier 1

1. Why do I still feel tired after enough sleep?
2. Why do I wake up tired?
3. Why does sleep affect recovery?
4. What happens during deep sleep?

### Tier 2

5. Why can't I sleep deeply?
6. How does sleep affect cellular energy?
7. Can poor sleep affect mitochondria?
8. What is restorative sleep?

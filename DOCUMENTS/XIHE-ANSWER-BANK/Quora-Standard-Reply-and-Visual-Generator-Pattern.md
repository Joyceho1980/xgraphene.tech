# XIHE Quora Standard Reply and Visual Generator Pattern

Date: 2026-06-13

## Purpose

This document stores the standard Quora reply pattern for XIHE.

It is designed to be reusable by a future content generator that can output:

- Quora answer text
- supporting image brief
- subtle internal link target
- claim boundary
- optional KB record notes

The goal is to make answers feel human first, scientific second, and commercial only as a quiet next step.

## Core principle

Quora answers should do four things:

1. answer the question directly
2. turn the feeling into a clear explanation
3. bridge to cellular energy or recovery only when useful
4. leave the reader with a calm next step, not a sales push

## Standard response shape

Use this order unless the question strongly suggests a different structure:

1. direct answer
2. human explanation
3. mechanism bridge
4. nuance or boundary
5. optional gentle internal link

## Standard answer style

Preferred traits:

- short paragraphs
- concrete language
- everyday examples
- one clean metaphor
- no jargon pile-up
- no overclaiming
- no hard sell

### Style cues

- write like a scientist explaining something important to a friend
- translate the body into human language
- keep the first paragraph useful on its own
- use ATP, mitochondria, or recovery science only when they actually clarify the problem

## Copy-ready master pattern

```markdown
Because [human cause in simple language].

Even when [common assumption], the body is still doing real work: [3 to 5 visible tasks or pressures].

That is why [felt result] shows up later, often when the person gets home.

At a deeper level, this is also an energy story. Deep inside the cells, mitochondria produce ATP, the molecule that powers thinking, movement, recovery, and other processes the reader never notices.

When demand stays high for too long, the body can notice the strain before the mind gives it a name.

That does not automatically mean something is wrong.

It usually means the energy systems have been working harder than they appear from the outside.

[Optional gentle boundary sentence about when to speak with a clinician.]
```

## Human-language modules

Use these as building blocks:

- the bill keeps running
- the system is not idle
- the crash comes later
- recovery has not caught up
- energy is being spent faster than it is being restored
- the body notices before the mind does

## Suggested bridge phrases

These phrases can lead into mitochondria or ATP without sounding forced:

- cellular energy
- ATP
- mitochondrial function
- recovery science
- why energy feels different
- energy systems

## Boundary module

Use one calm boundary when the topic is health-related:

```markdown
If the fatigue is persistent, severe, or comes with symptoms such as dizziness, shortness of breath, or significant mood changes, it is worth discussing with a qualified healthcare professional.
```

## Internal link rule

Use only one natural link when it helps the reader.

Preferred anchor styles:

- cellular energy
- mitochondrial dysfunction
- recovery science
- why energy feels different

Do not add multiple links in a short Quora answer.

## Supporting image rule

For Quora, the image is a cognitive translation layer.

It should not be decorative.

It should make the reader feel the problem before the biology is explained.

### Image brief formula

`subject + setting + emotional state + visual restraint + no text`

### Preferred image types

- tired professional at home after work
- person sitting quietly at dusk
- calm desk scene with visible depletion
- reflective recovery moment
- subtle abstract energy metaphor with a human anchor

### Avoid

- medical infographics
- doctor-on-screen clichés
- lab imagery
- exaggerated stock-photo smiles
- product-like advertising visuals

## Standard image brief example

```markdown
A professional sitting quietly on a sofa at dusk after work.
The room is calm and dimly lit.
No phone. No laptop. No obvious illness.
Just visible exhaustion.
Warm evening light through a window.
A feeling of emotional and physical depletion.
Cinematic photography.
Natural realism.
Recovery has not started yet.
No text, no labels.
```

## Generator output schema

When automation is built, each Quora output should return:

- `question`
- `direct_answer`
- `mechanism_bridge`
- `boundary`
- `internal_link_anchor`
- `internal_link_target`
- `image_brief`
- `claim_boundary`
- `kb_record_note`

## Record keeping

When a Quora answer is posted, save:

- exact question
- published answer
- image brief used
- link target used
- any performance note

## Example target use

This pattern is especially strong for questions like:

- Why am I tired when I come home from work?
- Why do I still feel tired after sleep?
- Why does my brain get tired when studying?
- Why does recovery feel slower than it used to?

## Final test

Before publishing, ask:

- Does this answer the question in the first few sentences?
- Does it feel human?
- Does it bridge naturally into science?
- Does the image help understanding?
- Is the link quiet and useful?
- Would the answer still stand alone if the link were removed?

If the answer to the last question is no, rewrite it.

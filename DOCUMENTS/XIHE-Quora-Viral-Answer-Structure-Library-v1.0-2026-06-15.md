## Purpose

This library defines the default structural templates for high-quality Quora answers.

The point is not to improvise every answer.

The point is to instantiate structured biological reasoning templates.

## Core Rule

Future Quora writing should follow three steps:

1. Choose the right template
2. Fill the variables
3. Adjust tone for Quora clarity

## Template 1: Sleep / Fatigue

Use for:

- Why am I tired after sleeping?
- Why is sleep not refreshing?
- Why do I wake up fatigued?

Structure:

1. Human problem
2. False assumption
3. Biological reframing
4. Mechanism layer
5. Failure point
6. Insight

Template logic:

### 1. Human Problem

People often feel tired even after sleeping enough hours.

### 2. False Assumption

The assumption is that sleep duration equals recovery.

### 3. Biological Reframing

In biology, sleep is not a passive state.

It is an active recovery process.

### 4. Mechanism Layer

During sleep, mitochondrial activity supports ATP restoration and cellular energy balance.

### 5. Failure Point

If sleep is fragmented or disrupted, recovery cycles may remain incomplete.

### 6. Insight

Sleep quality is not defined by duration, but by whether biological recovery cycles are completed.

## Template 2: Inflammation

Use for:

- What is inflammation?
- Why does inflammation slow healing?
- What is chronic inflammation?

Structure:

1. Human framing
2. Correction
3. System framing
4. M1/M2 axis
5. Dysfunction point
6. Insight

Template logic:

### 1. Human Framing

Inflammation is often seen as something harmful.

### 2. Correction

In reality, it is a necessary biological response.

### 3. System Framing

Inflammation is a dynamic regulatory state, not a fixed condition.

### 4. M1/M2 Axis

The system shifts between pro-inflammatory and repair-associated states.

### 5. Dysfunction Point

Problems occur when this transition fails or becomes prolonged.

### 6. Insight

Inflammation is not the problem.

Failed resolution is.

## Template 3: Mitochondria

Use for:

- What do mitochondria do?
- Why do I feel low energy?
- What causes fatigue at cellular level?

Structure:

1. Human experience
2. Reframe
3. Core mechanism
4. System layer
5. Failure mode
6. Insight

Template logic:

### 1. Human Experience

Fatigue is often felt as lack of energy.

### 2. Reframe

But energy in biology is not a static resource.

### 3. Core Mechanism

Mitochondria regulate ATP production, which powers cellular activity.

### 4. System Layer

Energy is continuously regulated, not simply stored.

### 5. Failure Mode

When mitochondrial efficiency decreases, fatigue becomes systemic.

### 6. Insight

Fatigue is not lack of energy, but reduced energy regulation efficiency.

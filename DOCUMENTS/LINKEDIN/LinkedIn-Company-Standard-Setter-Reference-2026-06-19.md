## Company page writing rule

For company page posts:

1. lead with the standard or benchmark
2. explain the measurable variables
3. connect the standard to recovery infrastructure or workflow value
4. keep the tone scientific and restrained

## Avoid

- founder-style philosophy
- generic recovery language
- mechanism-only posts with no category authority signal

## XIHE LinkedIn Relationship Potential Rule v1

Date: 2026-06-16

## Purpose

This note adds the missing long-term layer to the LinkedIn comment system.

The comment score tells us whether Joyce should engage with a post.

The relationship score tells us whether the person deserves repeated attention.

## Why this matters

Some people are worth a single comment.

Some people are worth building a thread of recognition with.

This distinction matters because LinkedIn is not only about post-by-post action.

It is also about long-term network positioning.

## Relationship Potential definition

Score each person from `0` to `5`.

### 5

- founder
- technical authority
- strategic validator
- adjacent recovery or biological systems thinker

### 4

- strong operator
- clinician
- workflow owner
- recurring relevance to XIHE themes

### 3

- useful signal
- relationship value depends on future posts

### 2

- weak adjacency
- low interaction potential

### 1

- occasional signal only

### 0

- no realistic long-term value

## Queue behavior

- `5` = High Value Relationship Queue
- `4` = Priority Relationship Queue
- `3` = Monitor Queue
- `0-2` = do not keep in active relationship memory

## Practical rule

If a person has:

- `Score >= 8`
- `Relationship Potential >= 4`

then they should be treated as a long-term contact, not just a comment target.

That means:

- follow them
- watch their future posts
- prefer quality over frequency
- note any CRM overlap

## One-sentence summary

Relationship Potential tells XIHE who should become part of the long-term attention graph, not just the current comment queue.

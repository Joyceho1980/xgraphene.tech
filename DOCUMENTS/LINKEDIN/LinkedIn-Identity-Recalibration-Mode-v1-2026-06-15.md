## XIHE LinkedIn Identity Recalibration Mode v1

Date: 2026-06-15

## Purpose

This file defines the current LinkedIn operating mode for Joyce.

The account is still in a transition period.

LinkedIn is still partially modeling the account using older luxury and jewelry signals.

So the current job is not full optimization.

The current job is input correction.

## Core diagnosis

This is an identity lag problem.

LinkedIn does not update account identity in real time.

It updates through lagged reinforcement:

- prior follows
- prior dwell time
- prior comments
- prior profile graph

That means the current feed is not a reliable expression of Joyce's intended identity.

## Current operating phase

### Phase 1: Input Recalibration

Working window:

- next 2 to 4 weeks

Goal:

- clean the input distribution
- stabilize topic identity
- retrain the recommendation layer through repeated health and recovery signals

This phase comes before full-scale adaptive optimization.

## Phase 1 hard rules

### 1. Topic anchoring is mandatory

Only actively engage with:

- sleep
- fatigue
- inflammation
- immune regulation
- mitochondria
- cellular energy
- recovery systems
- rehab workflow
- human performance regulation

Everything else should default to:

- ignore
- or passive follow only if strategically necessary

### 2. Language consistency is mandatory

Comments should stay inside one coherent explanation layer:

- cellular
- system
- regulation
- recovery timing

Do not jump into random styles or unrelated language systems.

### 3. Feed correction matters more than feed harvesting

During this phase, LinkedIn behavior itself is the training signal.

So:

- what Joyce comments on matters
- what Joyce follows matters
- what Joyce spends time reading matters

The point is not just to find good posts.

The point is to teach LinkedIn what this account is now.

## What not to do in Phase 1

- do not optimize for engagement volume
- do not chase generic visibility
- do not comment on off-topic posts just because the score is passable
- do not widen topic coverage too early

## Phase 1 action rule

For now:

- only act on `Must Comment` opportunities
- avoid low-value `Can Comment` unless the CRM reason is very strong
- keep follow behavior tightly tied to the target identity graph

## Phase 2 trigger

Only move to a broader adaptive mode when at least two of these are true:

1. feed is at least `60%` health / recovery / science / operator relevant
2. recommended people are shifting into recovery, biotech, clinician, and operator clusters
3. suggestions and company pages are less dominated by jewelry / luxury remnants
4. comment opportunities are appearing naturally inside the intended domain

## Phase 2 meaning

Phase 2 is when the full LinkedIn comment engine can operate more freely.

At that point:

- `Can Comment` becomes more useful
- feed scan becomes less distorted
- adaptive preferences begin to matter more

## Working summary

Right now, the LinkedIn system should treat the account as:

- not fully converged
- still correcting its input distribution
- in training, not yet in steady-state

## One-sentence summary

The current LinkedIn goal is not to maximize activity.

It is to rewrite the behavioral dataset LinkedIn uses to model Joyce.

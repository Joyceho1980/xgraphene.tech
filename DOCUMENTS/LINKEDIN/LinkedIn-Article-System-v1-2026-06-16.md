## XIHE LinkedIn Article System v1

Date: 2026-06-16

## Purpose

This file defines how XIHE should use LinkedIn Articles.

Articles are not the same as posts.

Posts create repeated cognitive signals.

Articles create deeper anchor assets.

## Role of LinkedIn Articles

LinkedIn Articles should do four jobs:

1. build depth
2. create a bridge from insight to explanation
3. support the founder or company identity with long-form structure
4. route the right readers into the XIHE website knowledge layer

## Frequency

Default cadence:

- 1 article every 2 to 3 weeks

Do not publish articles too frequently.

Articles should feel deliberate.

## Relationship to posts

Use this chain:

1. test a theme in short posts
2. if the theme feels strong, expand it into an article
3. use the article to route into the website

This prevents weak long-form output.

## Article positioning rule

A LinkedIn Article is not:

- a website article copy-paste
- a knowledge dump
- a sales brochure

It is:

- a cognitive warm-up layer
- a structured insight asset
- a trust bridge into XIHE's deeper evidence pages

## Best article families for XIHE

### 1. Recovery system framing

Examples:

- Why Recovery Feels Harder Than Work Itself
- Why Inflammation Is Not the Problem. Failed Resolution Is.

### 2. Deep-tech founder framing

Examples:

- Why Speed and Biology Do Not Follow the Same Clock
- Why Some Technologies Need Patience Before They Need Scale

### 3. Graphene-to-value translation

Examples:

- Why Graphene Should Be Understood as Recovery Infrastructure, Not Just Material Science
- Translating Graphene from Material Physics into Recovery Value

## Standard article structure

### 1. Hook

Start with a human or industry tension.

### 2. Experience or visible problem

Make the situation legible before explaining anything.

### 3. Light mechanism or systems layer

Add one scientific or systems concept.

### 4. Open loop

Do not resolve everything.

Leave one deeper layer unfilled so the website link becomes a continuation, not an ad.

### 5. Website bridge

Use one subtle link to the relevant XIHE page.

### 6. Short closing question or reflection

Optional.

Only if it feels natural.

## Link rule

The website link should appear late, not early.

Preferred functions:

- concept continuation
- evidence continuation
- knowledge hub entry

Avoid:

- multiple links
- obvious CTA language

## Voice rule

Founder articles should still keep one primary mode:

- System Thinker
- Science Observer
- Founder Philosopher

Do not blend all three heavily in one article.

## Visual rule

LinkedIn Articles should use cleaner visuals than posts.

Preferred:

- atmospheric founder image
- quiet science visual
- restrained high-end graphic

Avoid:

- busy infographics
- conference snapshots with no framing
- product-heavy images

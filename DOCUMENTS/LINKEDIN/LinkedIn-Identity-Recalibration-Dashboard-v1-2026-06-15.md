## XIHE LinkedIn Identity Recalibration Dashboard v1

Date: 2026-06-15

## Purpose

This dashboard tracks whether LinkedIn is still modeling Joyce through the old graph or is converging toward the intended recovery/science identity.

It is a manual monitoring board for now.

## Current phase

- `Phase 1: Input Recalibration`

## Weekly checkpoints

Review these once per day briefly, and summarize once per week.

### 1. Feed relevance

Question:

- Of the first 10 visible feed posts, how many are actually relevant?

Relevant means:

- recovery
- science
- sleep
- inflammation
- mitochondria
- health operator
- rehab workflow
- human performance

Score:

- `0-3` = poor
- `4-6` = transitional
- `7-10` = strong

### 2. Suggested people quality

Question:

- Are suggested people shifting toward clinicians, operators, founders, researchers, or still showing old luxury/jewelry graph remnants?

Score:

- `0` = mostly old graph
- `1` = mixed
- `2` = mostly intended graph

### 3. Suggested company quality

Question:

- Are suggested company pages shifting toward medtech, clinics, recovery, health, research?

Score:

- `0` = mostly old graph
- `1` = mixed
- `2` = mostly intended graph

### 4. Comment opportunity quality

Question:

- Of the posts scanned today, how many produced real `Must Comment` opportunities in the intended domain?

Score:

- `0` = none
- `1` = one real opportunity
- `2` = two or more real opportunities

### 5. Relationship potential quality

Question:

- Of today's good targets, how many belong in the high-value relationship queue?

Score:

- `0` = none
- `1` = one candidate
- `2` = two or more candidates

This is not the same as comment opportunity quality.

Comment opportunity asks:

- should Joyce reply now?

Relationship potential asks:

- should Joyce keep this person in long-term attention memory?

### 5. Profile signal quality

Question:

- Are profile views, reactions, or interactions starting to come from the intended industry?

Score:

- `0` = unclear or irrelevant
- `1` = mixed
- `2` = clearly improving

## Transition rule

Stay in Phase 1 until:

- Feed Relevance is `>= 6` for at least 5 of 7 recent check days
- Suggested People is `>= 1`
- Suggested Companies is `>= 1`

Only then begin relaxing the tight topic filter.

## Relationship queue rule

If a target gets:

- a strong comment score
- and `Relationship Potential >= 4`

then move that person into the high-value relationship queue.

That queue is for:

- repeated follows
- future comment opportunities
- CRM cross-checking
- slower, higher-quality relationship building

## Interpretation guide

### If feed stays poor

- keep using `/li-watch`
- reduce trust in `/li-scan`
- keep topic anchoring strict

### If feed improves but suggestions are still mixed

- continue Phase 1
- do not widen topics yet

### If feed and suggestions both improve

- move toward broader comment scanning
- allow more `Can Comment` activity

## One-sentence summary

This dashboard tells us whether LinkedIn is still remembering the old account identity, or beginning to learn the new one, and which people are worth keeping in long-term view.

## Post Copy

Recovery is not only about warmth.
It is about what happens in the biological environment when inflammation begins to resolve.

In preclinical research, graphene far-infrared has been associated with changes in inflammatory signaling and macrophage polarization under study conditions.

In one animal study published in Advanced Science, researchers reported a shift toward M2-associated repair signaling and a reduction in post-surgical adhesions. In another preclinical study published in IJMS, investigators observed modulation of oxidative stress, chemokines, and macrophage activity.

The key question is not simply whether tissue feels warm.
The deeper question is whether the recovery environment is being biologically supported.

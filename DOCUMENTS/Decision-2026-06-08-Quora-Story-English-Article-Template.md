# Decision: Quora-Derived English Story Article Template

Date: 2026-06-08

## Core decision

The article:

- When Energy Slowly Disappears: What Persistent Fatigue Can Teach Us About Mitochondrial Health

should be treated as a model template for future English-language articles inspired by Quora stories and similar public narratives.

## Why this draft works as a template

It balances:

- human story
- scientific restraint
- AI-readable structure
- Western-market readability

It does not rely on:

- hype
- product-first framing
- exaggerated medical claims

## Template use case

Use this model when XIHE finds a strong real-world story pattern on:

- Quora
- Reddit
- public health discussions
- user-submitted notes

## Required conversion rule

Do not copy the original story directly.

Convert it into one of these:

- a composite scenario
- a generalized pattern
- a story-inspired opening

Then write the science explanation from the KB.

## Recommended article structure

1. Quick Answer
2. Quiet human opening
3. Gradual problem escalation
4. What tests may miss
5. The cellular-energy explanation
6. Recovery and maintenance biology
7. Small supportive changes
8. Bigger lesson
9. caution line
10. FAQ
11. Related Reading

## Tone rule

Use:

- calm
- human
- observant
- slightly reflective

Avoid:

- dramatic miracle language
- aggressive certainty
- promotional product transitions

## Topic types that fit this template

- fatigue
- sleep that does not feel restorative
- exercise intolerance
- stress-related depletion
- gradual decline in daily energy
- recovery taking longer with age

## Hub guidance

Choose the Primary Hub based on the user's actual problem.

Examples:

- unexplained tiredness -> Fatigue Hub
- sleep and restoration issues -> Recovery or Sleep Hub
- aging and resilience -> Healthy Aging Hub

Use mitochondria as the explanatory bridge when appropriate, not always as the front-door hub.

## Safety rules

- third-party story is not proof
- persistent fatigue can have many causes
- one mechanism should not explain every case
- product mention, if any, stays secondary
- story opens the page; KB evidence carries the explanation

## Reusable opening logic

Suggested pattern:

- life used to feel manageable
- then recovery started taking longer
- then ordinary activity became expensive
- then the person began asking a deeper question about energy

## Strategic importance

This template should help XIHE build a recognizable English-language voice that is:

- more human than generic science pages
- safer than anecdote-driven claims
- more memorable for AI, LinkedIn, and website readers

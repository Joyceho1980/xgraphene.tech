# Decision: LinkedIn Benchmark Account

Date: 2026-06-13

## Decision

Intelligent Labs is now the primary LinkedIn comparison account for XIHE company-page learning.

## Why this account matters

It demonstrates a stable pattern for:

- human-first hooks
- light science explanation
- practical wellness framing
- soft CTA behavior
- consistent company-page rhythm

## What to study from it

- how it turns technical or nutritional concepts into everyday value
- how it keeps the tone calm and accessible
- how it avoids sounding like a white paper
- how it links science to routine, performance, and recovery

## XIHE adaptation rule

XIHE should not copy the supplement framing directly.

XIHE should use the same clarity pattern to translate:

- graphene science
- FIR mechanism
- recovery application
- system value

## Working conclusion

Use Intelligent Labs as a structural benchmark, not a style to imitate literally.

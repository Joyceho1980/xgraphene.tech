### Hook

Why does a normal workday sometimes leave you feeling more exhausted than a workout?

### Draft copy

You sit at a desk all day.

You do not run.

You do not lift anything heavy.

And yet by evening, the body feels drained.

That is because energy is not only spent through movement.

It is also spent through attention, decisions, stress, posture, and constant adaptation.

Deep inside the cell, mitochondria help produce ATP, the energy currency the body relies on for thinking, movement, and recovery.

When demand stays high for too long, recovery can start to lag behind.

That is one reason fatigue is often an energy story before it becomes anything else.

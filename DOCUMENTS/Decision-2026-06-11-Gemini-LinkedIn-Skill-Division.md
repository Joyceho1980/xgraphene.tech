# Decision: Gemini, LinkedIn, And XIHE Skill Division

Date: 2026-06-11

## Decision

The outbound workflow is now divided into three clear layers:

1. Gemini
2. LinkedIn
3. XIHE internal skill and Google workflow

## Role Of Gemini

Gemini is used for:

- pain-point analysis
- business-model summarization
- workflow-gap hypothesis
- contact-role prioritization after targets are identified

Gemini should not be treated as the source of final factual truth for:

- direct contact routes
- final public evidence confirmation
- exact email patterns

## Role Of LinkedIn

LinkedIn is used for:

- finding likely decision makers
- understanding role seniority
- mapping the buying committee
- identifying whether a person is on the operations, rehab, outreach, or research side

LinkedIn is best used after the account has already been selected.

## Role Of XIHE Skill And Google Workflow

XIHE's own skill and public-search workflow are used for:

- official site discovery
- service-page reading
- domain confirmation
- email-pattern detection
- route-quality judgment
- public-contact verification
- CRM-ready execution steps

The working order remains:

1. company first
2. person second
3. pattern third
4. verification fourth
5. outreach fifth

## Practical Split

### Gemini asks

- what pain is most likely here
- which department would care
- what angle is commercially strongest

### LinkedIn asks

- who likely owns this problem
- who influences the decision
- who can validate the workflow fit

### XIHE skill asks

- what is the official route
- what does the website prove
- what is the likely email pattern
- is the route good enough to launch outreach

## Working Conclusion

Gemini helps think.

LinkedIn helps identify people.

XIHE skill and Google help prove and execute.

# Video Brief - Why Do I Wake Up at 4:30 AM Every Night?

Date: 2026-06-21

## Source Article

- `WEBSITE/pages/SCIENCE/KNOWLEDGE/Sleep/why-do-i-wake-up-at-430am.html`

## Goal

Turn the article into a short editorial video that feels human, calm, and believable.

The video should not feel like a medical ad.
It should feel like a thoughtful sleep story told through real moments.

## Core Message

The article is not really about a clock.
It is about a sleep system that never fully powers down.

## Recommended Format

- Length: 60 to 90 seconds
- Style: cinematic editorial, quiet, premium, human-first
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 for website and YouTube
- Pacing: slow to medium, with room for breathing pauses

## Visual Direction

- Night scenes with soft shadows
- One repeated wake-up moment to establish the pattern
- A late-night thinking loop
- A quiet evening walk as the turning point
- A calmer dawn transition to recovery

Avoid:

- hospital visuals
- medical diagrams
- alarmist text overlays
- stock-photo sleep imagery

## Narration Script

### Opening

I kept waking up at 4:30 every morning.

Not once in a while.
Every night, for more than two months.

### Problem

At first I thought it was stress.
Then anxiety.
Then just bad sleep.

But the pattern was too consistent to ignore.

### Insight

My mind felt awake, but my body still felt heavy.

That was the clue.
This was not just short sleep.
It was shallow recovery.

### Turning Point

One night, I was too tired to keep monitoring sleep.

I stopped trying to control every minute.
I fell asleep without noticing.

And for the first time in months, I did not wake at 4:30.

### Change

Later, one simple habit helped more than thinking harder.

A slow walk before bed.
No phone.
No planning.
No pressure.

Just enough time for the body to exit the day.

### Close

If you keep waking up at the same hour every night, it may not be random.

It may be a sign that your system is still partly on duty.

Sometimes the best fix is not more effort.
It is a quieter evening.

## Editorial direction

- Use lived-experience language before mechanism language.

## Messaging principle

- Mitochondrial disease is not simply about being tired.
- It is about what happens when the body's ability to produce, manage, and recover energy is impaired.
- Vitality is the deeper concept the reader should remember.

# Decision: Mitochondria Hub Visual Lightening

Date: 2026-06-09

## Goal

Make the Mitochondria Hub feel less dense and less card-heavy while keeping the page educational, AI-readable, and structurally complete.

## Changes

- Converted the "Why This Matters" section into a split layout.
- Added a visual concept column with three small icon tiles:
  - Normal biology
  - Functional strain
  - Medical diagnosis
- Reduced the visual weight of the Explore section's secondary reading items.
- Reframed the secondary items as a lighter reading path rather than a dense block of cards.

## Principle

Use more icon-led breathing room and fewer heavy cards so readers can move through the page without fatigue.

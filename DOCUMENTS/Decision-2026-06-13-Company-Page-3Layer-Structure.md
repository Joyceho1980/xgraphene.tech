# Decision: XIHE Company Page Three-Layer Structure

Date: 2026-06-13

## Decision

The XIHE LinkedIn company page should not use a generic 80/20 balance.

Instead, its content should follow a three-layer structure:

- 40% Technology
- 40% Application
- 20% System Value

If a stricter balance is needed, a fallback mix of 50/30/20 may be used.

## Why this matters

XIHE is not positioning as a materials-only company.

The company page should translate graphene into recovery value.

That means the page must show:

1. the science
2. the real-world recovery use case
3. the business and system implications

## Content interpretation

### Technology

Use this section for:

- graphene
- FIR
- mechanism
- studies

### Application

Use this section for:

- recovery environments
- clinics
- sports settings
- wellness use cases

### System Value

Use this section for:

- differentiation
- business model
- user experience
- outcome framing without medical claims

## Strategic conclusion

If the company page only talks about graphene, it reads like a material company.

If it translates graphene into recovery system value, it reads like a new category company.

## Benchmark lesson

The Intelligent Labs benchmark shows that the strongest company pages often make application the dominant reading experience, while keeping science inside the application narrative rather than separating it into a standalone academic block.

That means XIHE should still preserve the 40/40/20 structure as a planning model, but in execution the application layer should feel like the main reading surface, with technology woven into it and system value appearing as a quiet third layer.

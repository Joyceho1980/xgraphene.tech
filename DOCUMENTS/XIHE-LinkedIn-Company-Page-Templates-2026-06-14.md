# XIHE LinkedIn Company Page Templates

Date: 2026-06-14

## Purpose

These templates translate the benchmark patterns into XIHE-specific company page writing.

Use the template that best matches the post goal.

## Template 1: Intelligent Labs style

Best for:

- practical recovery value
- everyday use cases
- application-led science

Structure:

1. human problem
2. practical use case
3. light mechanism
4. XIHE system relevance
5. soft CTA

Example draft:

```text
Why do some recovery environments feel supportive at first glance, but not actually change how people use them day to day?

The answer is often not about adding more equipment.
It is about creating a system that people can realistically use consistently.

That is where graphene-based far-infrared design becomes interesting.
It is not just a material question.
It is a question of how the environment supports recovery behavior over time.

For XIHE, the goal is to make recovery feel more usable, more repeatable, and more integrated into real routines.

That is the difference between a product and a recovery system.
```

## Template 2: Gaia Herbs style

Best for:

- warmth
- brand storytelling
- community tone

Structure:

1. brand or mission note
2. human-friendly context
3. practical wellness framing
4. quiet company perspective
5. gentle CTA

Example draft:

```text
At XIHE, we believe the best recovery environments are the ones people can actually return to.

That means designing with real daily life in mind:
busy schedules,
changing energy,
and the need for something that feels calm, intuitive, and easy to keep using.

Graphene far-infrared technology is one part of that story.
The larger goal is to support a recovery environment that feels practical in the real world, not just impressive on paper.

We are building around that idea every day.
```

## Template 3: Thorne style

Best for:

- institutional credibility
- expert presence
- event / collaboration updates

Structure:

1. external signal
2. expert or institutional context
3. technology relevance
4. XIHE positioning
5. direct but calm CTA

Example draft:

```text
As the conversation around recovery environments continues to mature, one thing is becoming clear:
the market is moving toward more evidence-led, experience-driven solutions.

That is why the science behind graphene and far-infrared technology matters.
It helps explain why certain environments are being explored for recovery-oriented use cases across wellness, sports, and clinic settings.

At XIHE, we view this as part of a larger shift:
from isolated materials to integrated recovery systems.

We will keep sharing what we learn as the field develops.
```

## Template 4: Ritual style

Best for:

- proof
- standards
- transparency

Structure:

1. standards or proof statement
2. why it matters
3. technology or study context
4. XIHE trust position
5. soft proof-oriented CTA

Example draft:

```text
The hardest part of building trust in a new category is not making promises.
It is setting standards.

That is why we care so much about how graphene far-infrared technology is framed and evaluated.
If the language is too vague, the category loses credibility.
If the language is too broad, the value gets lost.

XIHE is focused on translating the science into a recovery system story that stays clear, grounded, and useful.

Proof and clarity come first.
```

## Selection guide

- use Template 1 when the post should feel practical and application-led
- use Template 2 when the post should feel warm and human
- use Template 3 when the post should feel authoritative and externally credible
- use Template 4 when the post should feel proof-led and standards-based

## Company page rule

Whatever template is used, the post must still follow the XIHE company-page order:

1. technology
2. application
3. system value

Do not stop at graphene alone.

Do not write as if XIHE is only a materials company.

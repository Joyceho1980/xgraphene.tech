# XIHE B2B Sales Strategy

## Objective
Use a research-backed trust signal plus AI-agent-driven outbound to build a repeatable B2B sales flywheel in six months.

## Starting point
- No customers
- Cold start
- CRM and agentic workflow are already in place

## Market tiers

### Tier 1: Sports recovery and rehab clinics
- Primary market entry point
- Shortest decision chain
- Best for proving clinical value and building early references

### Tier 2: OEM / device brands
- Scale engine
- Larger order potential
- Strong fit for modular integration and product differentiation

### Tier 3: Wellness / elder care / long-horizon institutional buyers
- Slower cycle
- Useful as a strategic reserve and later-stage expansion market

## Core customer logic

### Sports recovery clinics
- Buyer profile: founder, rehab director, senior PT, clinic operator
- Pain points:
  - recovery adherence
  - measurable outcomes
  - differentiation
  - patient experience
  - operational burden
- Sales angle:
  - clinical data + business value

### OEM / brand partners
- Buyer profile: product manager, R&D, CEO
- Pain points:
  - product differentiation
  - parameter ceiling
  - compliance / registration
- Sales angle:
  - product upgrade + technical integration + registration support

### Wellness / elder care
- Buyer profile: operator, wellness lead, procurement
- Pain points:
  - premium experience
  - safety
  - long-term service value
- Sales angle:
  - recovery environment + comfort + service uplift

## Channel roles

### AI Agent
- Market scan
- Lead discovery
- First-touch email
- A/B testing
- Response detection

### Founder / technical expert
- High-value conversation
- Technical proof
- Field qualification
- Contract support

### No-channel rule
- No general channel partners yet
- Do not introduce distribution before the core message and process are proven

## Pricing logic
- OEM: tiered volume pricing plus sampling fee that can offset future order value
- Finished product: clear standard pricing and trial bundles
- Future stage: hardware plus data subscription

## Content-to-sales loop
- Break long science content into:
  - 3-minute summary
  - one-page data note
  - anonymized practice overview
  - short white paper excerpt
- Reuse sales objections as future content topics

## Agent optimization priorities
1. A/B test micro-strategies
2. Add defensive language handling
3. Log replies structurally
4. Track reply quality, not just volume

## North star metrics
- Effective reply rate
- Strategy win rate
- Unsubscribe / complaint rate
- Strategic accounts entering deep conversation

## Phased goals

### Phase 1: Validation
- Find the first repeatable winning approach
- Get early replies and deep conversations

### Phase 2: Replication
- Expand the winning approach to OEMs and adjacent markets
- Build a stronger content loop

### Phase 3: Ecosystem
- Become the default recovery technology layer
- Build a hardware + data moat

## Working conclusion
The system should behave like a calm, precise, self-improving digital spearhead.
Its job is not volume.
Its job is to create strategic conversations with the right people.

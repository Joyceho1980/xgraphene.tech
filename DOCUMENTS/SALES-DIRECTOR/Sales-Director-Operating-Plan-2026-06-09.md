# XIHE Sales Director Operating Plan

Date: 2026-06-09

## Mission
Build a repeatable B2B pipeline for XIHE's graphene FIR product line in North America.

## Revenue Priority
1. CABIN for clinics, recovery centers, and premium wellness venues.
2. CAPSULE for destination wellness, resorts, and higher-ticket experiences.
3. PORTABLES for clinics and smaller operators that need a lower-friction entry point.
4. FILM and OEM opportunities where the buyer already has a product or installation business.

## Ideal Customer Profiles
### Tier 1
- Sports medicine and orthopedic practices
- Physical therapy and rehab clinics
- Cryotherapy and recovery centers
- Integrative / functional health clinics

### Tier 2
- Luxury wellness resorts and spa groups
- Senior living operators
- Hospitality properties with spa or recovery programming

## Positioning
Lead with this message:
- XIHE is a premium, science-led recovery and wellness platform.
- We fit beside existing services, not against them.
- Buyers get a differentiator they can package, price, and explain.

Do not lead with vague miracle language. Lead with:
- professional wellness
- recovery workflow
- premium guest experience
- technical specs
- deployment fit
- partner revenue

## Sales Motion
### 1. Account Selection
Use direct research to build a named-account list.
- Google search
- LinkedIn company pages
- clinic / resort / facility websites
- local directory and map results

### 2. Qualification
Qualify each account on:
- Segment fit
- Existing recovery / wellness stack
- Size and service scope
- Buying authority
- Installation fit
- Budget capacity
- Compliance sensitivity

### 3. Outreach Angle
Use one of three angles:
- Revenue: add a premium recovery offering
- Differentiation: stand out from other clinics or resorts
- Operational fit: a new service line with a clear use case

### 4. Conversion Path
Move prospects through:
1. Short intro email or DM
2. Spec sheet / product overview
3. 20-minute discovery call
4. Pilot or demo discussion
5. Commercial proposal

## Outreach Cadence
Day 1: first email
Day 3: short follow-up
Day 6: LinkedIn or site form follow-up
Day 10: call or voicemail
Day 14: final follow-up with a clear next step

## Messaging Rules
- Use plain English first, technical language second.
- Avoid disease-treatment claims in cold outreach.
- Keep claims tied to product, workflow, and buyer value.
- For wellness and hospitality, emphasize experience and differentiation.
- For clinics, emphasize service expansion and patient journey.

## Sales Assets To Use
1. 1-page product overview
2. Technical spec sheet
3. Comparison sheet vs common recovery alternatives
4. Installation / deployment checklist
5. Partnership / OEM overview
6. Short proof pack with website links and research references

## Weekly Operating Rhythm
- Monday: prospect research and account scoring
- Tuesday: outbound batch 1
- Wednesday: follow-up batch
- Thursday: calls and discovery meetings
- Friday: pipeline review, notes, next-account selection

## First 30 Days
### Week 1
- Build 50 target accounts.
- Sort into sports medicine, recovery, integrative, resort, and senior care.
- Prepare one outreach template per segment.

### Week 2
- Send first outreach batch.
- Log responses.
- Refine the offer based on objections.

### Week 3
- Book discovery calls.
- Identify pilot candidates.
- Tighten the case for each segment.

### Week 4
- Push proposal-ready accounts.
- Build a repeatable follow-up system.
- Keep adding accounts so the pipeline never stalls.

## Success Metrics
- 50 new accounts researched per week
- 20+ outbound touches per day
- 10-15% reply rate target
- 3-5 discovery calls per week once the list is warm
- 1-2 proposal-ready opportunities per week as the system matures

# Deep-100 Strategy

## Core decision
XIHE will not optimize for broad volume in early outbound.
Instead, the sales system will focus on a Deep-100 list:
- 100 strategically valuable accounts
- each account worth deep recon, custom analysis, and tailored outreach
- each account capable of becoming a long-term partner, not a one-off buyer

## Strategic model

This is a hedgehog strategy, not a fox strategy.

- Fox strategy: many accounts, many messages, low depth, low leverage
- Hedgehog strategy: one clear niche, deep trust, high relevance, repeatable value

For XIHE, the niche is:
- premium sports medicine
- orthopaedic / rehab networks
- recovery-forward operators
- innovation-friendly leaders who value experience, performance, and differentiation

## Deep-100 qualification criteria

An account belongs in Deep-100 if it has most of the following:
- network effect: multiple locations, schools, athletes, or referral nodes
- innovation intent: leadership has shown openness to new ideas
- clinical credibility: recognizable team, academic posture, or authority
- long-term expansion potential: not a one-time purchase
- value alignment: responds to quality, differentiation, and patient experience

## Working operating model

### Stage 1: Deepen trust
- Collect recon.
- Analyze human hooks.
- Send custom first-touch email.
- Goal: secure a real conversation.

### Stage 2: Deepen value
- Capture the account's unique pain points.
- Map internal decision flow.
- Build a tailored collaboration concept.
- Goal: trial, pilot, or first small engagement.

### Stage 3: Deepen bond
- Keep delivering useful insights.
- Invite them into product and concept feedback.
- Turn the customer into a partner or advocate.

## Agent design implications

The system should optimize for:
- recon depth, not just list size
- conversation quality, not just reply count
- knowledge-base refinement from real account conversations

## North star metric

Do not center the team on raw send volume.
Track:
- how many strategic accounts enter deep conversation each month

Suggested milestone targets:
- Month 1: 3
- Month 3: 10
- Year 1: 50

## Working conclusion

XIHE is not building a mass outbound engine.
It is building a strategic account partner system.
Every Deep-100 account deserves a serious recon brief, a tailored angle, and a custom pathway into the CRM.

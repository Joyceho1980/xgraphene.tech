# XIHE Prospecting and SEO Keyword Library

Date: 2026-06-11

## Why This Library Exists

XIHE keyword work should serve two systems at the same time:

1. outbound sales prospecting
2. website SEO and GEO content operations

That means keyword mining is not only for finding companies.

It is also for:

- understanding how target buyers describe themselves
- discovering how markets label modalities and recovery services
- finding what commercial pain points show up repeatedly
- deciding what XIHE should write about on the website

## Operating Principle

Keywords should be organized as reusable assets.

Every useful keyword cluster can be reused in:

- Google prospecting
- LinkedIn-supported search
- Google dorking
- CRM tags
- outreach angles
- article topics
- landing pages
- FAQ and GEO content

## Layer 7: Combined Search Formulas

These are practical combinations for company discovery.

### Company Discovery

- `"PEMF" "physical therapy"`
- `"cryotherapy" "recovery center"`
- `"red light" "sports medicine"`
- `"infrared sauna" "wellness center"`
- `"sports recovery" founder`

### Decision-Maker Discovery

- `"Company Name" founder`
- `"Company Name" owner`
- `"Company Name" "director of operations"`
- `"Company Name" "practice administrator"`
- `site:linkedin.com/in "Company Name" founder`

### Email Route Discovery

- `site:companydomain.com "@companydomain.com"`
- `site:companydomain.com (contact OR team OR about) "@companydomain.com"`
- `filetype:pdf "Company Name" email`
- `filetype:pdf "companydomain.com" contact`

## How This Connects to SEO

Each keyword cluster can become website content in one of four ways:

1. knowledge article
2. segment page
3. FAQ page
4. glossary / modality comparison page

Examples:

- `PEMF` -> adjacent-modality comparison content
- `recovery environment` -> positioning page / educational explainer
- `patient experience` -> rehab-focused business insight article
- `circulation support` -> science education content
- `healthy aging` -> pillar / hub content

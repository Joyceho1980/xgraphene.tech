# XIHE Email Collection Skill Blueprint

Date: 2026-06-11

## Core Principle

The XIHE email collection workflow should not start from random email hunting.

It should start from account understanding.

The correct order is:

1. Google-search the company
2. Identify the likely key person through LinkedIn-supported Google search
3. Use Google dorking to infer the company email pattern
4. Verify the email with a validation tool
5. Store the result into CRM
6. Then move into pain-point analysis and outreach generation

## Why This Order Matters

If the process starts from email hunting alone, quality drops fast.

The XIHE model is:

- target-account-first
- pain-point-led
- route-quality-aware

That means:

- the company must be understood first
- the decision-maker hypothesis must come before the email guess
- verification happens before send

## Skill Stages

### Stage 1: Company Research

Goal:

- confirm official website
- confirm country, city, segment, and service model
- confirm whether the account is relevant to XIHE

Output:

- company name
- website
- country
- city
- segment
- modality stack
- pain-point hypothesis

### Stage 2: Key Person Identification

Goal:

- identify the best reachable decision-maker or operating contact

Search path:

- Google + LinkedIn indexed pages
- official team pages
- about pages
- leadership pages

Output:

- decision maker name
- role
- LinkedIn route
- confidence level

### Stage 3: Email Pattern Discovery

Goal:

- find public evidence of the company email naming pattern

Search path:

- site search on official domain
- Google dorking on domain
- PDF / file search
- public role inboxes
- third-party mentions with domain email traces

Output:

- visible sample email
- domain pattern
- guessed target email if justified
- route type: direct / role-based / generic / form

### Stage 4: Verification

Goal:

- avoid sending into dead or low-confidence addresses

Output:

- verification result
- send confidence

### Stage 5: CRM Writeback

Required fields:

- company
- website
- decision maker
- position
- email
- route type
- domain pattern
- industry
- country
- city
- status
- priority
- next follow-up
- notes

### Stage 6: Outreach Readiness

Only after the above is complete:

- run pain-point analysis
- choose XIHE angle
- generate first-touch draft

## Operational Rule

The workflow is:

- company first
- person second
- pattern third
- verification fourth
- outreach fifth

Do not compress everything into one step.

The tool stack can change later.

The sequence should not.

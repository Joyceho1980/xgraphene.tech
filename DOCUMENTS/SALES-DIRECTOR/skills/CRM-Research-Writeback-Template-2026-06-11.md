Date: 2026-06-11

Use this template after Google research, Gemini analysis, and route verification.


## Route Type Definitions

- `direct`: named person email with acceptable confidence
- `role-based`: operations / partnerships / admin route with useful internal routing potential
- `generic`: low-priority general inbox such as `info@`
- `form`: only contact form available
- `unknown`: still not ready for launch

## Verification Status Definitions

- `verified`
- `likely valid`
- `unverified`
- `risky`
- `invalid`

## XIHE Angle Definitions

- `recovery environment`
- `premium add-on`
- `patient experience`
- `member experience`
- `service differentiation`
- `cash-pay wellness`
- `OEM integration`

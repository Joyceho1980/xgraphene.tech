# Google + Gemini Prospecting SOP

Date: 2026-06-11

## Objective

Use Google for raw evidence collection.

Use Gemini for structured interpretation.

Use CRM for persistence and follow-up control.

This workflow is for XIHE outbound customer development.

## Core Division Of Labor

### Google

Google is the fact-finding layer.

Use it to collect:

- official company website
- service pages
- about / team / leadership pages
- LinkedIn-indexed public pages
- PDF files
- contact pages
- visible email samples
- domain and route clues

### Gemini

Gemini is the interpretation layer.

Use it to produce:

- company segment classification
- business model summary
- current modality stack summary
- likely pain-point hypothesis
- likely decision-maker priority list
- route-quality judgment
- XIHE angle recommendation

### CRM

CRM is the operating system layer.

Use it to save:

- company
- role
- route
- verification status
- pain point
- XIHE angle
- next action

## Recommended Workflow

### Step 1: Google company search

Search for:

- company name
- website
- service model
- modality stack
- location

Questions to answer:

- what do they sell
- who do they serve
- what recovery or wellness modalities already exist
- are they relevant to XIHE

### Step 2: Google + LinkedIn role search

Search for:

- founder
- owner
- clinic director
- operations lead
- partnerships lead

Questions to answer:

- who likely evaluates new technology
- who influences commercial adoption
- what is the best route if the named person is not reachable

### Step 3: Google dorking for email evidence

Search for:

- visible email samples
- contact pages
- PDF files
- role inboxes
- domain naming patterns

Questions to answer:

- is there a confirmed domain
- is there a visible email pattern
- is the best route direct, role-based, form, or generic

### Step 4: Gemini structured analysis

Feed Gemini:

- website findings
- role findings
- modality findings
- email evidence

Ask Gemini to output:

- company segment
- business model
- likely pain points
- decision-maker ranking
- route confidence
- recommended XIHE angle

### Step 5: Email verification

Before outreach, verify:

- direct email candidate
- role inbox if used

### Step 6: CRM writeback

Save the record with:

- route type
- domain pattern
- verification result
- pain-point hypothesis
- recommended XIHE angle
- outreach readiness

### Step 7: Outreach generation

Only after the above is complete:

- generate draft
- choose proof line
- schedule follow-up

## Operating Rule

The sequence is:

1. Google facts
2. Gemini structure
3. verification
4. CRM
5. outreach

Do not collapse these into one step.

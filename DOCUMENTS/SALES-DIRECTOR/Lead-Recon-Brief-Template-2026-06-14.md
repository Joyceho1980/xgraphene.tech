# Lead Recon Brief Template

Use this template for every strategic lead before first-touch outreach.

```text
[Lead Recon Brief]

1. Company name:
2. Company website:
3. Company size / number of locations:
4. Core business focus:

5. Target contact name:
6. Title:
7. LinkedIn URL:
8. Other social profiles:

9. Recent activity (last 3 months):
   - Posts or articles
   - Comments or reposts
   - Company news: funding, expansion, new equipment, partnerships

10. Public signals:
    - What topics seem to matter to them?
    - What pain points have they voiced?
    - What operating beliefs or management views have they shown?

11. Personal background signals:
    - Education
    - Prior roles
    - Sports / hobbies / endurance events

12. Existing technology or equipment:
    - Modalities, devices, or service stacks visible on site or social

13. Other observations:
```

## Usage rule
- Fill as much as possible.
- Do not force weak data.
- Use the brief to determine the human hook before writing email.

# Pre-Engagement Recon Framework

## Purpose
This framework defines how XIHE should collect and use public pre-outreach intelligence before writing a first-touch email.

The goal is not to collect more data for its own sake.
The goal is to find the human hook that makes a cold email feel specific, relevant, and worth replying to.

## Why recon matters
- Most cold emails fail because they look generic.
- A good recon pass lets us anchor the email in what the contact already cares about.
- Public signals often reveal values, fears, priorities, and business timing.

## Recon brief format

Use this structure for high-value leads:

```text
[Lead Recon Brief]

1. Company name:
2. Company website:
3. Company size / number of locations:
4. Core business focus:

5. Target contact name:
6. Title:
7. LinkedIn URL:
8. Other social profiles:

9. Recent activity (last 3 months):
   - Posts or articles
   - Comments or reposts
   - Company news: funding, expansion, new equipment, partnerships

10. Public signals:
    - What topics seem to matter to them?
    - What pain points have they voiced?
    - What operating beliefs or management views have they shown?

11. Personal background signals:
    - Education
    - Prior roles
    - Sports / hobbies / endurance events

12. Existing technology or equipment:
    - Modalities, devices, or service stacks visible on site or social

13. Other observations:
```

## Human-hook analysis

When a recon brief is available, analyze it for:
- Core values
- Emotional triggers
- Technology sensitivity
- Professional identity
- Personal identity hooks

The output should answer:
- What does this person care about?
- What would make them ignore the email?
- What would make them feel understood?
- What language mirrors their own worldview?

## Email design rules from recon

### Use recon to:
- open the email with a real reference
- mirror the contact's language
- choose the right pain point
- decide whether the angle should be technical, operational, or commercial
- avoid mismatched claims or tone

### Do not use recon to:
- overload the first email with too many facts
- sound creepy or over-observant
- force personal details into the message when they are irrelevant

## Agent workflow

For high-value leads:
1. Collect public recon signals.
2. Write a short recon brief into CRM.
3. Run the lead through pain-point compression.
4. Decide whether the account needs a custom email or a template.
5. Draft the email from the recon signal and the pain map.

For lower-priority leads:
1. Collect a minimal recon brief.
2. Use the best-fit template.
3. Personalize with one or two public details only.

## Priority rule

- Top 30% of leads: manual or semi-manual human analysis after recon.
- Remaining leads: template-driven send with light personalization.

## Output standard

Every high-value recon pass should produce:
- a short human-hook summary
- one recommended opener
- one forbidden angle
- one suggested email draft direction

## Working conclusion

The recon layer is the sniper scope.
Without it, even a good offer becomes blind outreach.

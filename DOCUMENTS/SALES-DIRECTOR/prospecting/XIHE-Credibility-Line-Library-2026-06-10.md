# XIHE Credibility Line Library

Date: 2026-06-10

## Purpose

This file standardizes credibility language for XIHE outbound sales.

Use these lines to keep messaging:

- consistent
- accurate
- commercially strong
- appropriate to the buyer and stage

## Rule

Do not use every credibility point in one email.

For first-touch emails, use:

- one institutional line
- optionally one product/regulatory line

For follow-up materials, use:

- a broader stack of institutional, regulatory, and clinical-context lines

## Tier 1: Default First-Touch Lines

### Option A: institutional default

`XIHE is backed by Jiageng Innovation Laboratory and is the lead drafter of China's graphene electrothermal film industry standard.`

Best for:

- most first-touch emails
- PT clinics
- recovery operators
- wellness buyers

### Option B: institutional + ownership clarity

`XIHE is backed by Jiageng Innovation Laboratory, which holds 55% of the company, and is the lead drafter of China's graphene electrothermal film industry standard.`

Best for:

- recovery centers
- operators who care about platform credibility

### Option C: institutional + regulatory

`XIHE is backed by Jiageng Innovation Laboratory, and relevant XIHE products are registered as Class II medical devices in China.`

Best for:

- PT / rehab buyers
- buyers who respond to clinical seriousness more than brand story

## Tier 2: Stronger First-Touch Lines

### Option D: institutional + regulatory + standards

`XIHE is backed by Jiageng Innovation Laboratory, relevant XIHE products are registered as Class II medical devices in China, and the company is the lead drafter of China's graphene electrothermal film industry standard.`

Best for:

- more serious clinical or operator audiences
- second paragraph credibility support

### Option E: regulatory + clinical-context

`Relevant XIHE products are registered as Class II medical devices in China and have been used in clinical settings there alongside broader recovery applications.`

Best for:

- PT / sports rehab
- clinical-style recovery buyers

## Tier 3: Follow-Up / Deeper Materials

### Option F: full institutional line

`XIHE comes from the Jiageng Innovation Laboratory ecosystem: IKKEM holds 55% of the company, its Director is Academician Zheng Nanfeng, and the platform sits within a broader advanced-materials and translational-science environment.`

Best for:

- follow-up deck
- one-pager
- technical introduction

### Option G: advanced credibility line

`XIHE's platform is backed by Jiageng Innovation Laboratory, supported by China Class II medical-device registration on relevant products, and built around high-emissivity graphene FIR systems for professional recovery settings.`

Best for:

- second-touch materials
- distributor or clinic partner conversations

## Segment Guidance

### PT / sports rehab

Best lines:

- Option C
- Option D
- Option E

Why:

- these buyers care more about patient-facing seriousness than abstract materials prestige

### Recovery centers

Best lines:

- Option A
- Option B
- Option D

Why:

- they need credibility, but commercial distinctiveness matters more than heavy clinical framing

### Membership / luxury wellness

Best lines:

- Option A
- Option B

Why:

- too much regulatory language can slow the emotional/commercial pitch

## Wording To Avoid

Do not default to:

- `medical quality`
- `medical standard graphene`
- `FDA-equivalent`
- `clinically proven` unless the exact basis is being stated

## Safer Product Language

Preferred:

- `professional-grade graphene FIR systems`
- `professional recovery systems`
- `graphene recovery systems`

Use carefully:

- `medical-grade graphene recovery systems`

Only use that when:

- the audience and context justify it
- the supporting basis is clear
- it does not imply FDA clearance or US medical-device authorization

## Fast Selection Rule

If unsure:

1. PT / rehab: use Option C
2. recovery center: use Option B
3. luxury wellness: use Option A

# Email Language Calibrations For US Recovery Buyers

Date: 2026-06-10

## Purpose

Refine XIHE's first-touch outbound language so it matches how different US recovery buyers actually think.

This document captures practical lessons from direct review of Apto PT, Oasis Cryo, and Fire & Ice style outreach.

## Core Insight

US buyers do not all respond to the same recovery language.

The product can stay the same.

The buyer lens changes:

- PT owner thinks about care credibility, evidence, and patient outcomes
- recovery operator thinks about differentiation and memorable experience
- membership wellness operator thinks about member value and lifestyle positioning

## 1. PT / Sports Rehab Clinics

### What they care about first

- clinical credibility
- evidence awareness
- patient-friendliness
- workflow fit
- outcomes and care logic

### Better language

- recovery environment
- recovery workflow
- patient-friendly
- evidence-aware
- adjunct to PT or rehab

### Weaker language

- premium recovery session
- luxury recovery experience
- spa-like wording

### Why

PT owners are more likely to ask:

- does this fit patient care?
- does this help the workflow?
- is this credible?

They are less likely to respond first to:

- does this feel premium?

## 2. Recovery Centers

### What they care about first

- differentiation
- higher-value packaging
- repeat visits
- category distinctiveness

### Better language

- premium recovery room
- differentiated and memorable
- complementary to the current stack
- stronger reason to book

### Weaker language

- too much `science-led`, `science-driven`, or `science-backed` phrasing when it sounds generic

### Why

Many US recovery operators have already been pitched by:

- PEMF sellers
- red light sellers
- sauna sellers
- cryo sellers

The issue is not whether they have heard a science claim.

The issue is whether XIHE sounds commercially distinct.

## 3. Membership / Luxury Wellness Operators

### What they care about first

- member value
- retention
- package depth
- lifestyle alignment
- premium differentiation

### Better language

- premium member experience
- member value
- recovery suite
- more complete offering
- dedicated FIR environment

### Strong framing

Ask whether the experience becomes more valuable when recovery extends beyond cryo, sauna, and red light.

That is stronger than discussing the device in isolation.

## 4. Positioning Rule

Keep this line of thinking:

- complementary, not competitive

Avoid this line of thinking:

- better than PEMF
- better than cryo
- better than red light

Why:

US recovery operators are already flooded with one-modality comparison selling.

If XIHE sounds like another comparison pitch, the email is easy to ignore.

## 5. CTA Rule

### Weak / overused

- I would be happy to send a short overview.

### Better

- If this is something your team has looked at before, I would be curious to hear how you currently think about FIR within your recovery offering.
- Even if the timing is not right, I would be interested in getting your reaction to where you think recovery technology is heading over the next few years.

### Why

This sounds more like:

- founder to founder
- operator to operator

and less like:

- sales rep to prospect

## Bottom Line

The most effective first-touch email does not start with:

- what XIHE is

It starts with:

- how the buyer makes money
- what pressure the buyer likely feels
- where XIHE fits without threatening the current stack

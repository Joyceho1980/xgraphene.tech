# XIHE Outreach Template

Use this template for cold outreach to B2B prospects.

## Subject Lines
- Premium recovery upgrade for your facility
- A new wellness offering for your clients
- Can XIHE fit your recovery workflow?
- Adding a premium recovery room without starting from zero

## Core Email
Hi [Name],

I’m reaching out from XIHE. We build graphene FIR recovery and wellness systems for clinics, recovery centers, and premium hospitality operators.

I looked at [Company] and saw a strong fit because you already serve clients who care about recovery, wellness, mobility, or premium experience. XIHE can add a differentiated service line that sits beside your current offering.

If helpful, I can send:
- a 1-page product overview
- technical specs
- deployment options
- a short partnership deck

If this is relevant, I’d suggest a 20-minute intro call to see whether CABIN, CAPSULE, or PORTABLES fits your model.

Best,
[Your Name]
XIHE

## Segment Variants
### Sports Medicine / PT
Focus on:
- recovery workflow
- premium patient experience
- service expansion
- facility differentiation

### Integrative / Functional Health
Focus on:
- wellness positioning
- practice differentiation
- non-pharmacological recovery support
- premium client retention

### Resorts / Hospitality
Focus on:
- guest experience
- premium add-on revenue
- wellness programming
- differentiated spa offerings

### Senior Living
Focus on:
- comfort
- premium wellness amenity
- resident experience
- activity and recovery support

## Rules
- Do not lead with disease claims.
- Do not oversell.
- Keep the email short.
- Always ask for a simple next step.

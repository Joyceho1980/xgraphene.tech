# Google Dorking Verification Run

Date: 2026-06-10

## Objective

Test whether the Google dorking workflow materially improves contact discovery on three live XIHE target accounts:

1. Apto Physical Therapy
2. Oasis Cryo & Health Spa
3. Phorm Fitness / Fire & Ice Wellness Center

## Result Summary

The workflow is worth keeping.

But the result is mixed by account type.

### Overall verdict

- strong improvement for accounts with detailed official team or about pages
- moderate improvement for accounts with rich official contact pages but no named leadership
- weak improvement when the site is highly promotional and does not expose internal people

## What Worked Best

Most useful search layers in practice:

1. official contact page
2. official about/team page
3. modality-specific service page
4. footer/contact details repeated across the site

## What Was Less Effective

These were weaker in this run:

- generic Google wildcard-style email hunting
- expecting every site to expose named leadership publicly
- relying on one search pattern instead of reading the site structure

## XIHE Rule Going Forward

Keep the workflow, but use it correctly:

- first use it to confirm fit and route quality
- then use it to upgrade from generic inbox to named person where possible
- do not assume Google dorking alone will always reveal the final decision-maker

## Decision

Approved to add into the XIHE sales skill as a standard contact-discovery workflow.

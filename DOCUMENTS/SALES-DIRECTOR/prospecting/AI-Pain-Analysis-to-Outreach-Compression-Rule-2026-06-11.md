# AI Pain Analysis To Outreach Compression Rule

Date: 2026-06-11

## Objective

AI-generated research can be more aggressive than first-touch outbound should be.

This rule defines how XIHE converts internal AI pain analysis into safe, credible, human-sounding outreach.

## Core Rule

Internal analysis is allowed to be broader.

External outreach must be narrower.

## Keep These Elements

Keep:

- business-model understanding
- recovery-stage bottleneck ideas
- patient or member experience pain
- service differentiation pain
- workflow-fit observations
- premium add-on framing
- complementary positioning

## Compress These Elements

Compress:

- strong causality claims
- strong treatment language
- strong physiological certainty
- numerical or wavelength-specific mechanism claims unless directly required and supported
- language that sounds like a scientific pitch deck instead of a founder email

## Remove These Elements

Remove from first-touch outreach unless explicitly supported and strategically necessary:

- direct ATP optimization claims
- strong anti-inflammatory outcome claims
- disease-treatment implications
- highly technical mechanism-first framing
- dramatic claims about speed or clinical superiority

## Rewrite Pattern

### Internal version

`This institution may face a functional recovery plateau and could benefit from a technology that improves mitochondrial ATP generation and microcirculation.`

### Compressed outreach version

`Groups like this often look for complementary ways to support the slower stage of recovery once patients move beyond the acute phase and into functional rehab.`

## Preferred External Language

Use:

- recovery environment
- recovery workflow
- complementary support
- patient-friendly
- non-invasive
- fits beside existing modalities
- differentiated experience
- premium add-on

Avoid as default first-touch wording:

- mitochondrial optimization
- entropy reduction
- cellular repair acceleration
- inflammation resolution
- resonance therapy certainty

## Output Rule

Every AI pain analysis should produce two outputs:

1. internal hypothesis version
2. outreach-safe compressed version

The compressed version is the only version allowed to feed first-touch email generation.

# XIHE Customer Discovery SOP

Date: 2026-06-12

## Purpose

Turn keywords into qualified accounts, then into CRM-ready outreach targets.

This SOP is the operating path for XIHE's customer discovery machine.

## Objective

For each keyword, produce:

- real companies
- real decision makers
- real contact routes
- fit scores
- CRM tags
- outreach angles

## Operating Sequence

### Step 1: Select the keyword

Start with a keyword that implies buying intent.

Priority tiers:

#### Tier 1: Direct buying intent

- `recovery suite`
- `recovery room`
- `cash-pay wellness`
- `PEMF clinic`
- `sports recovery center`

#### Tier 2: Buildout or timing intent

- `new wellness center`
- `coming soon recovery center`
- `new PT location`
- `expanding facility`

#### Tier 3: Niche or expansion intent

- `canine rehab`
- `veterinary recovery`
- `athlete recovery`
- `biohacking center`

### Step 2: Generate search formulas

Use Gemini or the keyword intelligence skill to produce:

- Google search formulas
- LinkedIn search formulas
- company discovery queries
- expansion / buildout queries

### Step 3: Search for companies

Search the open web for:

- official website
- location pages
- service pages
- coming soon / new location language
- membership or cash-pay language

The first question is not "Is the company real?"
The first question is "Does this company match the keyword's intent?"

### Step 4: Find the likely buyer

Use the company site and LinkedIn to identify:

- owner
- founder
- clinic owner
- rehab director
- performance director
- operations lead
- administrator

### Step 5: Determine the buying trigger

Use public evidence to infer why they may buy now.

Common triggers:

- New Facility
- Expansion
- Renovation
- Membership Launch
- Cash-Pay Pivot
- Insurance Pressure
- Return-to-Play Demand
- Pet Wellness Launch
- Veterinary Expansion
- Premium Experience Upgrade

### Step 6: Map the pain point

Write one clear pain hypothesis.

Examples:

- needs higher revenue per square foot
- needs a premium recovery offering
- needs to differentiate from commodity PT
- needs lower-labor passive recovery services
- needs a new non-insurance revenue stream

### Step 7: Map the knowledge base

Before product mapping, connect the pain point to XIHE's knowledge stack.

Use this chain:

Pain Point
-> Biology Layer
-> Research Layer
-> Application Layer
-> Product Layer

### Step 8: Score the account

Use the CRM field spec and scoring system.

High-value signals:

- Cash Pay
- Membership
- New Facility
- Expansion
- Recovery Suite language
- PEMF already present
- Premium Wellness positioning
- Return-to-Play focus
- Pet Wellness expansion

### Step 9: Write CRM fields

Store these fields:

- account_name
- website
- segment
- business_model
- facility_stage
- buying_trigger
- pain_point
- economic_justification
- product_fit
- outreach_angle
- fit_score
- next_action

### Step 10: Draft outreach

The first email should:

- mention one clear pain hypothesis
- use one or two proof points only
- sound like it was written after reading the prospect
- avoid a generic template

## Search Priority Rules

### Highest priority accounts

- new facility or expansion
- cash-pay or hybrid business model
- existing PEMF / cryo / red light / infrared stack
- premium membership positioning
- return-to-play focus

### Medium priority accounts

- operating clinic with room to upgrade
- wellness operator with add-on services
- pet wellness concept or pilot

### Lower priority accounts

- generic wellness language only
- no visible facility signal
- no business model signal
- consumer-only or low-intent traffic

## Output Format

For every keyword run, produce:

1. keyword interpretation
2. target account list
3. buyer roles
4. buying triggers
5. pain point hypothesis
6. knowledge mapping note
7. fit score
8. CRM tags
9. outreach angle
10. next action

## Example Run

Keyword:

- `recovery suite`

Expected output:

- companies with new recovery rooms or suite buildouts
- owners, founders, or rehab directors
- trigger: new facility or expansion
- pain: underutilized space, differentiation, revenue per square foot
- knowledge mapping: passive recovery environment -> premium service line -> product fit
- fit score: high
- CRM tags: `SEG_Facility_Buildout`, `TRIGGER_New_Facility`, `FIT_High`
- outreach angle: recovery suite layout and ROI

## Operating Rule

Do not stop at keyword analysis.
The purpose is to move from signal to company to buyer to outreach.

## Next Step

After the SOP is stable, use it to run a live account discovery batch and write the results into CRM.

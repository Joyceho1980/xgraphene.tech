# Email Verification and US B2B Data Pricing Update

Date: 2026-06-09

## Executive Answer

### 1. Do we need to pay for email verification?

Not to start testing.

For real outbound, yes.

Practical rule:

- under 50 to 100 checks per month: free tiers are enough for workflow testing
- once we send real prospecting emails at scale: pay for verification
- verification cost is small compared with bounce damage and domain reputation loss

### 2. Does US B2B data include decision-maker emails?

Yes.

But the data is not equal across segments.

Best coverage:

- software
- SaaS
- agencies
- larger multi-location businesses
- funded or visible growth companies

More mixed coverage:

- local clinics
- independent wellness operators
- founder-led medical businesses
- businesses where the decision-maker is visible but the direct email is not

### 3. How do these tools usually charge?

There are two main charging models:

- contact data platforms: charged by seat and/or credits to reveal contact data
- email verification tools: charged by validation credits

## Current Public Pricing Snapshot

Verified against public pages on 2026-06-09.

### Apollo

Best use:

- US decision-maker discovery
- job title filtering
- verified business emails
- phones and role lookup in one workflow

Public pricing page currently shows:

- Free: 900 credits per seat per year, granted monthly
- Basic: $49 per seat per month billed annually
- Professional: $79 per seat per month billed annually
- Organization: $119 per seat per month billed annually, 3-seat minimum

Public credit notes currently show:

- email access: 1 credit
- phone access: 8 credits

What this means for us:

- yes, Apollo is meant to give us direct contact data for buyers and decision-makers
- strong fit if we want US account research plus outreach list building in one tool

### Hunter

Best use:

- domain-first research
- finding company email patterns
- finding professional emails when we know the person and company
- verification in the same workflow

Public pricing currently shows:

- Free: 50 credits per month
- Starter: $34 per month billed annually
- Growth: $104 per month billed annually
- Scale: $209 per month billed annually

Public credit notes currently show:

- 1 credit to find an email
- 0.5 credit to verify an email

What this means for us:

- yes, Hunter can surface professional contacts and decision-maker emails
- but it is stronger for domain-led discovery than for broad US account intelligence

### Lusha

Best use:

- quick contact lookup
- Chrome extension workflows
- revealing verified contact details fast

Public pricing page currently shows:

- Free: 40 credits per month

Public pricing references currently show:

- Starter: about $49.90 per month monthly, about $37.45 per month annual
- Professional: about $69.90 per month monthly, about $52.45 per month annual
- Premium: about $399.90 per month monthly, about $299.95 per month annual

Public credit notes currently show:

- verified email reveal: 1 credit
- phone reveal: 10 credits

What this means for us:

- yes, Lusha is explicitly built around revealing verified decision-maker contact data
- practical for fast lookup, but database depth may be thinner than Apollo for some US segments

### UpLead

Best use:

- transparent US contact-data buying
- verified emails plus mobile direct dials
- smaller-team self-serve buying

Public pricing page currently shows:

- Free trial: 5 credits for 7 days
- Essentials: $99 per month monthly or $74 per month billed annually
- Plus: $199 per month monthly or $149 per month billed annually
- Professional: custom quote

Public credit note currently shows:

- 1 credit = 1 unlocked contact

What this means for us:

- yes, UpLead includes decision-maker email and direct-dial style contact data
- useful if we want transparent pricing and do not want an enterprise contract first

### ZeroBounce

Best use:

- final email verification before sending
- protecting deliverability

Public pricing page currently shows:

- Free: 100 validation credits monthly
- Pay-as-you-go minimum: 2,000 credits
- example price shown on page at current crawl: 5,000 credits for $69

Public usage notes currently show:

- 1 credit per email verification
- unknown results are free
- credits do not expire

What this means for us:

- we can start free
- once we validate real outbound lists, paid verification is straightforward and inexpensive

### MillionVerifier

Best use:

- cheapest bulk verification

Public pricing currently shows:

- free account available
- 100,000 verifications for $149
- 1,000,000 verifications for $449

What this means for us:

- good low-cost verification layer if volume grows
- weaker fit than Apollo or Hunter for finding contacts

### Cognism

Best use:

- more advanced B2B data buying
- stronger compliance-heavy and larger-team workflows
- better suited when we need more scale, more verified mobiles, or more Europe-heavy outbound

Public pricing structure currently shows:

- Standard package
- Pro package
- 5 seats included in listed prospecting packages
- 1 credit = 1 unlocked contact
- pricing is quote-based

Public product pages currently state that Cognism provides:

- business emails
- mobile numbers
- data for decision-makers in EMEA, US, and APAC

What this means for us:

- yes, they cover decision-makers and business emails
- but this is not the right first purchase for us unless we are ready for a larger sales-data budget

## XIHE Buying Guidance

### Cheapest safe starting point

- Hunter free or Apollo free for early testing
- ZeroBounce free for first validations

### Best first paid stack

- Apollo for US decision-maker discovery
- ZeroBounce for final verification

### Best low-cost alternative

- Hunter for domain research
- ZeroBounce for verification

### When we should pay immediately

Pay now if we are doing any of these:

- sending more than small test batches
- guessing emails from patterns and needing validation
- buying or scraping US contact lists
- contacting clinics where direct routes are already scarce and we cannot waste sends

### When we can wait

Do not pay yet if we are only:

- testing workflow
- checking a few domains
- validating a tiny pilot list

## Sales Reality For XIHE

For XIHE, the real bottleneck is not "can we buy US data at all?"

We can.

The real bottleneck is:

- finding the right account
- identifying the right pain point
- getting a named operator, founder, practice owner, clinical director, or business-development contact
- verifying the route before sending

That means:

- do not buy a large enterprise data contract first
- first prove that our pain-point-led outreach gets replies
- then scale the data spend

## Recommendation

If we buy only one thing first:

- buy Apollo if the priority is finding US decision-makers
- buy ZeroBounce if the priority is cleaning lists we already have

If we buy two things:

- Apollo + ZeroBounce

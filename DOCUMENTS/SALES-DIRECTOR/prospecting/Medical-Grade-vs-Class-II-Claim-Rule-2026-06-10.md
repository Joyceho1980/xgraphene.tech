# Medical-Grade vs Class II Claim Rule

Date: 2026-06-10

## Working Decision

If XIHE has:

- a real China Class II medical device registration for the relevant product
- real clinical-use or clinical-study support

then XIHE can absolutely use stronger medical credibility language.

But the strongest and safest sales wording is:

- exact factual claim first
- broader branding term second

## Best Rule

Prefer:

- `registered as a Class II medical device in China`
- `used in clinical settings in China`
- `supported by clinical-use experience in China`

Before:

- `medical-grade`
- `medical quality`

## Why

`Medical quality` is weak English.

It sounds vague and non-standard.

`Medical-grade` is natural English, but in the US it is a sensitive marketing phrase if used in a general wellness or non-cleared medical context.

FDA's January 6, 2026 general wellness guidance says products are not general wellness products if their marketing includes claims of:

- clinical equivalence
- clinical accuracy
- medical or clinical grade
- substitution for an FDA-authorized, cleared, or approved medical device

That does not mean XIHE can never use `medical-grade`.

It means XIHE should use it carefully and not let it imply:

- FDA clearance
- FDA equivalence
- clinical substitution

## Recommended External Language

### Best first-touch wording

- `We develop professional graphene far-infrared recovery systems used for advanced recovery and wellness environments.`
- `Our platform is backed by Jiageng Innovation Laboratory and includes products registered as Class II medical devices in China.`
- `XIHE products have been used in clinical settings in China, alongside broader recovery and wellness applications.`

### Better than saying only `medical-grade`

- `Class II medical device registered in China`
- `clinical-use backed in China`
- `developed for professional and clinical recovery settings`

## When `medical-grade` is acceptable

Use `medical-grade graphene recovery systems` only if:

- the product-specific Class II status is real
- the statement can be tied to the product or platform honestly
- the audience will not reasonably interpret it as an FDA marketing authorization claim
- follow-up materials can support the statement

## Where To Use Each Version

### First-touch email

Use:

- `Class II medical device registered in China`
- `used in clinical settings in China`
- `professional recovery systems`

Avoid making `medical-grade` the headline.

### Follow-up deck / one-pager

Can test:

- `medical-grade graphene recovery systems`

but anchor it with:

- what product
- what registration
- what clinical basis
- what market

## Practical Sales Recommendation

For US outreach, the best credibility ladder is:

1. recovery workflow and business fit
2. complementary positioning
3. China Class II registration
4. clinical-use support in China
5. then, if needed, `medical-grade` language in deeper materials

## Suggested Wording Options

### Option A: safest

`We develop professional graphene far-infrared recovery systems, including products registered as Class II medical devices in China.`

### Option B: stronger

`We develop graphene far-infrared recovery systems for professional and clinical settings, with relevant products registered as Class II medical devices in China.`

### Option C: later-stage branding

`XIHE is building medical-grade graphene recovery systems supported by China Class II registration and clinical-use experience.`

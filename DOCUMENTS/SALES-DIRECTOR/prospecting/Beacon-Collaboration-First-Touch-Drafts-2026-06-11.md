## Usage Rule

Choose the draft based on the contact:

- Draft 1 for operations or rehab leadership
- Draft 2 for fellowship, education, or research-oriented contacts
- Draft 3 for sports medicine outreach or athlete-program contacts

## Guardrails

Before sending, confirm whether approved collateral supports:

- `medical-grade` wording
- any device-classification reference
- any stronger clinical evidence lines

If support exists, those points should be added in follow-up material, not forced into the first email.

# Scientific Boundary: Resonance and Water-Cluster Claims

Date: 2026-06-09

## Question

Can XIHE publicly state that graphene far-infrared:

- resonates at the same frequency as the body
- makes water molecules smaller
- cleans body cells

## Working Answer

Not as settled public-facing fact.

## Why

Current literature supports a more limited statement:

- far-infrared has been studied for biological effects
- some studies report effects on skin perfusion or microcirculation
- some preclinical and human studies suggest recovery- or comfort-related effects in certain settings

But these stronger statements are not established enough to use as broad commercial claims:

- "same-frequency resonance with the body"
- "water molecules become smaller"
- "cleans body cells"

## Evidence Notes

### What the literature does support more clearly

- A 2012 review in PubMed describes FIR as a modality that has been investigated for biological effects and medical applications.
- A 2024 Phase 1 randomized clinical trial reported effects on local skin perfusion, microcirculation, and oxygenation for a FIR-emitting patch.
- Older and newer literature often discusses circulation, warmth, tissue interaction, and biological response.

### What is weaker or more speculative

- An older 1988 PubMed item discussed decreased water-cluster size as part of a proposed explanation.
- That is not the same as saying modern human clinical science has conclusively proven that commercial graphene FIR systems make "water molecules smaller" in the body.
- Spectroscopy papers on water clusters study physical cluster behavior, but they are not direct proof of a consumer health claim such as "cleaning cells."

## Sales Rule

Do not use these phrases as standard sales claims:

- same-frequency resonance
- smaller water molecules
- clean the cells
- detoxify cells

Use these instead:

- recovery environment
- warmth and comfort
- circulation-oriented support language
- premium, immersive, complementary recovery experience
- science-led graphene FIR platform

## Best Practical Position

If the user strongly believes in these mechanisms, keep them as internal inspiration unless we have:

- product-specific evidence
- clear translation from mechanism to human outcome
- legal/compliance review for the target market

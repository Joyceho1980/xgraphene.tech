# Email Verification and B2B Data Pricing Notes

Date: 2026-06-09

## Quick Answer

### Do we need to pay for email verification?

For very light testing, not immediately.

For real outbound volume, yes.

Reason:

- free plans are enough to test workflow
- paid verification is worth it before sending at scale
- deliverability damage is more expensive than verification credits

### Does US B2B data include decision-maker emails?

Yes, often.

But quality varies by segment:

- software / SaaS / larger companies: usually easier
- clinics / medical practices / wellness operators: mixed
- founder-led small businesses: often easier to find names than direct emails
- public `info@` inboxes are common and should not be confused with direct decision-maker routes

## Current Pricing Notes

### Apollo

Best for:

- US B2B contact discovery
- decision-maker emails + phones
- relatively transparent self-serve pricing

Official pricing page currently shows:

- Free: 900 credits per seat per year, granted monthly
- Basic: $49 per seat per month, billed annually
- Professional: $79 per seat per month, billed annually
- Organization: $119 per seat per month, billed annually, 3-seat minimum

Credit logic:

- 1 email = 1 credit
- 1 phone = 8 credits

Notes:

- strong for initial US account discovery
- good for titles, emails, and phone data in one place
- useful if we want one main prospecting platform

### Lusha

Best for:

- fast contact lookup
- lightweight prospecting
- browser-extension workflows

Official help and pricing references currently show:

- Free plan: 40 credits per month
- email reveal: 1 credit
- phone reveal: 5 or 10 credits depending on plan/version references
- public Lusha content references pricing starting at about $37/month on annual billing

Notes:

- good for quick lookup
- pricing/versioning appears to vary by plan generation, so check the live checkout page before buying

### Hunter

Best for:

- domain-based email discovery
- email pattern discovery
- light outreach + verification in one workflow

Official current references show:

- Free: 50 credits per month
- Starter: $34/month billed annually on current pricing page
- monthly credits by plan: Free 50, Starter 2000, Growth 10,000, Scale 25,000
- verification usage in standard plans: 0.5 credit per verified email

Notes:

- very good when we know the company domain
- especially useful for email pattern discovery and verification
- less complete than Apollo for broad contact discovery

### ZeroBounce

Best for:

- pure email verification
- protecting deliverability before send

Official current references show:

- free account: 100 monthly validations
- pay-as-you-go minimum: 2,000 credits
- 2,000 credits: $39
- 5,000 credits: $69
- 10,000 credits: $129
- unknown results are free
- credits do not expire

Notes:

- strong final-check tool before sending
- simple pricing
- a good match if we separate "find" from "verify"

### MillionVerifier

Best for:

- low-cost bulk verification

Official current references show:

- plans from $39
- 100k verifications for $149
- 1 million verifications for $449
- 100 free credits

Notes:

- very price-aggressive
- attractive if our list volume grows quickly

### Cognism

Best for:

- more serious sales teams
- mobile numbers, enrichment, and compliance-heavy workflows

Official pricing structure currently shows:

- Standard and Pro prospecting packages
- 5 licenses included in listed packages
- 1 credit = 1 unlocked contact
- pricing is custom / quote-based

Notes:

- likely more expensive than we need right now
- better later if we build a larger outbound team

## Recommendation for XIHE Right Now

### Cheapest workable stack

- Apollo or Hunter for finding contacts
- ZeroBounce or MillionVerifier for final verification

### Lowest-risk starting path

- Hunter first for domain discovery and verification
- Apollo second for better decision-maker discovery where Hunter is thin
- ZeroBounce for final send check

### What I would buy first

If we only buy one tool first:

- Apollo if we need more US decision-maker discovery
- ZeroBounce if we already have many emails and need clean sending

If we buy two tools:

- Apollo + ZeroBounce

## Buying Rule

Pay for verification before scaling outbound.

Do not pay enterprise data prices yet unless:

- we have repeatable sending
- we are exhausting self-serve data tools
- we are getting enough replies to justify a bigger data spend

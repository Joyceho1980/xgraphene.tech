# Beacon Orthopaedics Gap Analysis

Date: 2026-06-11

## Purpose

This file translates Beacon Orthopaedics & Sports Medicine from a target account into a structured workflow-fit hypothesis.

It is designed for:

- CRM notes
- outreach angle selection
- contact prioritization
- later-stage proposal framing

## Public Evidence Base

Publicly visible signals that matter:

- Beacon presents itself as a large integrated orthopedic and sports medicine network
- Beacon highlights sports medicine, physical therapy, surgery, and broad patient access
- Beacon publicly emphasizes research, education, and fellowship infrastructure
- Beacon provides centralized contact routing and a large-scale service footprint

## Strategic Reading

This combination suggests that Beacon is not only buying for:

- treatment delivery

but also for:

- workflow quality
- brand differentiation
- clinical credibility
- athlete and patient confidence

## Core Working Hypothesis

Beacon is most likely to respond to language around:

- recovery workflow fit
- support for the slower phase of recovery
- differentiated patient recovery environment
- evaluation or pilot discussion

Beacon is less likely to respond to:

- mechanism-first product pitching
- aggressive technical claims
- language that sounds like consumer wellness hype

## Account Language Rule

For Beacon, the safest and strongest sequence is:

1. acknowledge their integrated sports medicine and rehab model
2. point to the slower stage of recovery where workflow support matters
3. position XIHE as complementary to existing modalities and therapy
4. invite an evaluation conversation rather than a product sale

## What Not To Lead With

Do not lead with:

- 9.4um resonance claims
- ATP claims
- strong anti-inflammatory claims
- explicit device-combination claims
- direct comparisons against PEMF or other incumbent modalities

## Best Initial Commercial Angle

The strongest first-touch angle is:

`A complementary recovery environment that may fit beside Beacon's existing PT, sports medicine, and education-driven workflow.`

The strongest second-step angle is:

`If there is interest, XIHE can share a more structured view of how a stage-specific recovery support module could be evaluated within an existing program.`

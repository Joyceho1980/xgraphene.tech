# PEMF-Adjacent Prospecting Strategy

Date: 2026-06-09

## Decision

The next outbound sprint should focus on accounts that already show PEMF or adjacent recovery-modality awareness.

This is the best free-first path because:

- they already understand recovery spend
- they do not need category education from zero
- XIHE can be positioned as a complement, not a strange new concept
- pain points are easier to infer from the public modality stack

## CRM Signal

Current CRM PEMF-related sample accounts identified from the database:

1. Fox Physical Therapy
2. US Cryotherapy

These two accounts point to two useful target archetypes.

## Archetype 1: PT / Sports Rehab Clinics With PEMF

Example:

- Fox Physical Therapy

Common public signals:

- physical therapy
- sports injury rehab
- concierge or premium positioning
- PEMF already listed
- laser therapy, dry needling, manual therapy, recovery services

### Likely pain points

- the clinic already does the clinical work well, but the patient experience may still look similar to competitors
- they need a premium recovery layer that feels serious, not gimmicky
- they want higher-value cash-pay services without weakening clinical credibility
- they need something memorable that can sit after manual therapy, mobility work, or rehab sessions

### XIHE angle

- not a replacement for PT or PEMF
- a premium post-session recovery experience
- a calmer, more immersive recovery format than a small single-modality device
- something that supports circulation, warmth, relaxation, and perceived recovery quality

### Best sales language

- recovery workflow
- patient experience
- premium add-on
- adjunctive recovery support
- science-led recovery environment

### Avoid

- aggressive disease claims
- "better than PEMF" framing
- trying to win on mechanism overload in the first touch

## Archetype 2: Recovery Chains With PEMF In The Menu

Example:

- US Cryotherapy

Common public signals:

- cryotherapy
- red light
- compression
- infrared sauna
- PEMF
- membership or repeat-visit business model

### Likely pain points

- every competitor now has a similar recovery menu
- consumers already recognize cryo, red light, and PEMF, so novelty decays
- chains need a new premium reason for repeat visits and upsell packages
- operators want a higher-ticket experience, not just another small add-on

### XIHE angle

- not another commodity modality
- a differentiated graphene FIR recovery room
- a deeper, quieter, more premium experience that can sit beside cryo, red light, and PEMF
- a stronger story for premium packages and branded recovery programs

### Best sales language

- differentiated recovery room
- premium treatment category
- repeat-visit driver
- membership upsell
- recovery experience beyond standard modality menus

### Avoid

- saying XIHE replaces cryotherapy or PEMF
- overselling research in the first note
- generic "innovative wellness solution" language

## Strategic Insight

PEMF is not the target by itself.

PEMF is the signal.

If a company already offers PEMF, it usually means:

- the buyer accepts device-led recovery
- the buyer is open to non-drug recovery tools
- the buyer already educates clients around recovery modalities
- the buyer may be looking for the next differentiated layer

That is why PEMF-adjacent accounts are a stronger first-wave list than generic clinics.

## Free-First Prospecting Workflow

### Step 1. Search for modality-stack buyers

Use Google searches like:

- `"physical therapy" PEMF "sports injury"`
- `"sports medicine" PEMF recovery`
- `"cryotherapy" PEMF "red light"`
- `"recovery center" PEMF compression cryotherapy`
- `"concierge physical therapy" PEMF`
- `"wellness center" PEMF "red light" recovery`

### Step 2. Keep only strong-fit accounts

Keep the account if it has at least three of these:

- PEMF listed publicly
- recovery stack with two or more adjacent modalities
- premium or athlete-facing positioning
- multi-location or visible scale
- visible contact path
- clear business story for XIHE

### Step 3. Capture the real pain hypothesis

For each account, write one short note:

- what they already sell
- what they are likely missing
- why XIHE fits beside that stack

Bad note:

- "May be interested in recovery equipment"

Good note:

- "Already offers PEMF, laser, and direct-access PT. Likely pain is turning strong rehab outcomes into a more premium and memorable patient recovery experience."

### Step 4. Use free email path first

Before paying for tools:

- use the official website
- use contact pages
- use team pages
- use role-based Google search
- use LinkedIn-supported Google search for names
- use free Hunter or Apollo credits only after public routes are exhausted

### Step 5. Verify lightly before sending

For the first small batch:

- website-confirmed emails first
- then free verification credits
- do not send guessed emails without a pattern check

## First Focused Sprint

Target a first batch of 20 accounts:

- 10 PT / sports rehab / orthopedic recovery operators with PEMF or similar stack
- 10 recovery chains or recovery studios with PEMF plus cryo / red light / compression

## Qualification Priority

### High priority

- visible PEMF or adjacent stack
- premium positioning
- named operator, founder, owner, or director visible
- multi-location or strong cash-pay model

### Medium priority

- good modality stack
- weak direct contact path
- smaller scale but good fit

### Low priority

- only generic wellness language
- no visible recovery stack
- no clear premium or clinical use case

## XIHE Positioning Rule For This Segment

The winning message is:

- XIHE helps an already-educated recovery buyer add a more premium, more immersive, and more differentiated recovery experience

The losing message is:

- XIHE is another gadget that competes with PEMF, red light, or cryotherapy one-to-one

## Next Execution Step

Build a dedicated PEMF-adjacent target list and score each account by:

- modality-stack strength
- premium signal
- contactability
- likely business pain

Then write outreach account by account from that pain hypothesis.

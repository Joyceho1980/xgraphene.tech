# XIHE Pet Recovery Product Concept

Date: 2026-06-12

## Status

Internal product idea candidate.

This is a concept note for the sales system, not a final market claim.

## Working Name

- `The First Graphene-Powered Recovery Environment for Pets`

Alternative names:

- `Pet Comfort Chamber`
- `Pet Wellness Pod`
- `Graphene Pet Recovery System`
- `Pet Recovery Cabin`

## Why This Idea Exists

The pet market may respond faster than the human clinical market because the buying decision is often driven by:

- comfort
- visible care
- premium service packaging
- owner willingness to pay for wellness support

## Recommended Positioning

Keep the first version focused on:

- comfort-oriented recovery support
- warmth and relaxation
- post-activity rest support
- senior pet comfort care
- premium pet wellness add-on

Core emotional line:

- `Because Recovery Should Feel Like Home`

Contrast line:

- `Not a Therapy Device. A Recovery Environment.`

Brand headline:

- `The First Graphene-Powered Recovery Environment for Pets`

Do not lead with:

- treatment
- cure
- disease claims
- broad medical promises

## Evidence and Claim Boundary

Current internal evidence may support animal-related R&D discussion, but it does not automatically justify direct pet treatment claims.

Before any external launch, confirm:

1. animal-specific safety review
2. veterinarian review or partner validation
3. allowed wording for the target market
4. species-specific use boundary

## Potential Buyers

- veterinary clinics
- premium pet hospitals
- pet rehabilitation or recovery centers
- grooming and wellness chains
- breeding or boarding operators with premium service tiers

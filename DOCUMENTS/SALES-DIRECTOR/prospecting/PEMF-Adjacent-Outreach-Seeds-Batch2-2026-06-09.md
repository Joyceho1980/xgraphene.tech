# PEMF-Adjacent Outreach Seeds Batch 2

Date: 2026-06-09

## 1. Apto Physical Therapy

### Why this account matters

Apto is one of the cleanest PT-style fits in the current pool because it already lists PEMF alongside one-on-one PT, sports rehab, dry needling, blood flow restriction, and performance-oriented services.

### Pain hypothesis

The likely pressure is not education.

The likely pressure is:

- how to deepen the patient recovery experience after treatment
- how to justify premium cash-pay add-ons without looking gimmicky
- how to stand apart from other modern PT clinics using similar advanced tools

### XIHE angle

XIHE can be positioned as:

- a premium recovery session beside PT and PEMF
- an adjunct after hands-on therapy or sports rehab
- a calm, science-led recovery environment rather than another small device

### First-touch direction

Lead with:

- "You already seem to take recovery seriously rather than treating PT as a quick in-and-out visit."
- "The fit I see is not replacing PEMF or rehab work, but adding a more immersive recovery session beside it."

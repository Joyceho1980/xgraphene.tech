# Recovery Capacity and Medical-Grade Positioning

Date: 2026-06-10

## Strategic Direction

XIHE's strongest long-term commercial story is shifting from:

- material
- wavelength
- mechanism

toward:

- recovery environment
- recovery capacity
- patient/member experience
- cash-pay or premium service value

## Why This Matters

Most buyers do not buy:

- graphene
- far-infrared
- emissivity

They buy:

- better patient experience
- stronger member value
- higher-ticket recovery offerings
- differentiation from nearby clinics or recovery centers
- a more complete recovery workflow

### PT / sports rehab

1. recovery workflow

3. cash-pay or premium wellness add-on
4. supporting technology

### Recovery centers

1. differentiated recovery environment
2. memorable booking experience

4. supporting technology

## Positioning Shift

Use this strategic shift over time:

- from `graphene far-infrared technology`
- toward `recovery technology`
- and eventually toward `medical-grade graphene recovery systems` only where support exists

## Language Recommendation

### Better commercial framing

- recovery environment
- recovery suite

- recovery capacity

### Less effective opening framing

- graphene as the lead idea

- far-infrared as the lead idea

These may still appear, but they should support the buyer story rather than define it.

## Medical-Grade Term Guidance

### English usage

`Medical-grade graphene` is more natural English than `medical standard graphene`.

`Medical standard graphene` sounds unnatural and incomplete in US business English because buyers will ask:

- which standard?
- whose standard?
- regulatory, material, device, or manufacturing standard?

### However: use carefully

FDA's general wellness guidance explicitly flags claims of:

- medical grade
- clinical grade
- clinical equivalence

as sensitive when the product is being marketed in a general-wellness context or in a way that could imply medical-device equivalence or substitution.

That means:

- `medical-grade graphene` may be commercially attractive
- but should not become default first-touch wording unless XIHE can substantiate the basis for the term in the target market

### Safer current rule

For first-touch outbound, prefer:

- professional-grade graphene FIR systems
- professional recovery systems
- graphene recovery systems

Reserve `medical-grade graphene recovery systems` for later-stage materials only if XIHE can clearly support the basis for that description.

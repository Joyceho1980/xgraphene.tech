# Beacon Decision-Maker Map And LinkedIn Plan

Date: 2026-06-11

## Purpose

Beacon should be worked as a buying committee, not a single-contact account.

For this type of institution, XIHE needs two parallel lanes:

1. operations and business decision makers
2. clinical, rehab, and research validators

## Buying Committee Structure

### Budget and strategic owners

- CEO
- operations leadership
- business development or growth leadership

### Workflow owners

- physical therapy leadership
- rehab service-line leadership
- sports medicine outreach leadership

### Clinical and credibility validators

- fellowship or education leadership
- research-oriented clinicians
- program leads connected to athlete recovery or return-to-play

## Contact Sequencing Rule

Work the account in this order:

1. identify the real workflow owner
2. identify the strongest clinical validator
3. identify the budget owner or strategic sponsor
4. choose the best first route based on route quality, not job title prestige alone

## Touch Strategy

### First touch

- founder-style or operator-style email
- no hard pitch
- no full technical deck
- evaluation language only

### Second touch

- light follow-up tied to workflow fit
- ask whether XIHE should speak with rehab, outreach, or research side

### LinkedIn support

- follow relevant leaders
- engage lightly with Beacon news if there is a real opening
- do not comment like a vendor

Beacon should be opened with a dual-track map:

- one commercial door
- one clinical-credibility door

This increases the odds that XIHE is discussed as a credible recovery workflow addition rather than a generic product pitch.

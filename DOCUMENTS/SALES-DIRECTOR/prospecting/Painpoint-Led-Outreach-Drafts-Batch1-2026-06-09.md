# Painpoint-Led Outreach Drafts - Batch 1

Date: 2026-06-09

## Sales Logic

The first email should not try to explain all of XIHE.

The sequence is:
1. Show that we understand the buyer's business.
2. Name one specific pressure they likely feel.
3. Connect XIHE to that pressure as a practical add-on.
4. Offer a small next step.

## Compliance Guardrails

- Do not claim XIHE cures pain, treats disease, or replaces clinical care.
- For US clinics, frame XIHE as a recovery, comfort, differentiation, and adjunctive wellness technology.
- For recovery centers, avoid sounding like another sauna or cryotherapy add-on. The stronger angle is "a different recovery room experience."
- For PT and sports medicine, lead with workflow and patient experience before mechanism.
- Put detailed evidence in a follow-up one-pager, not the first cold email.

## Contact Priority

Do not treat generic inboxes as the main path unless there is no alternative.

Priority order:
1. Named decision maker email
2. Practice administrator / operations / partnerships contact
3. Contact form that can be routed internally
4. Phone call to request the right contact person
5. Generic inbox such as `info@` or `admin@` only as a backup

Operational rule:

If we only have a generic inbox, do not count that account as truly ready-to-send yet. It should stay in the routing stage until we have either a named person, a stronger internal routing path, or a phone-confirmed contact.

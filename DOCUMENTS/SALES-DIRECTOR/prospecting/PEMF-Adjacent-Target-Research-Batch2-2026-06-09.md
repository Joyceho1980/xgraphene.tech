## Segment Notes

### Best immediate sub-segment

The strongest immediate sub-segment from this batch is:

- recovery centers and premium wellness operators with PEMF plus at least two adjacent modalities

Reason:

- they already educate the customer
- they already monetize recovery sessions directly
- the buying story is easier: differentiation, premium room, repeat visits, membership upsell

### Best PT-style account in this batch

- Apto Physical Therapy

Reason:

- one-on-one care model
- visible PEMF adoption
- visible direct email
- easier "clinical but premium" complementary message

## Sales Reminder

For this whole batch, do not lead with:

- replacement framing
- mechanism-heavy claims
- water-cluster or cell-cleaning language

Lead with:

- premium recovery environment
- complementary fit beside current modalities
- differentiated session format
- higher-value recovery offering
- patient/member/guest experience

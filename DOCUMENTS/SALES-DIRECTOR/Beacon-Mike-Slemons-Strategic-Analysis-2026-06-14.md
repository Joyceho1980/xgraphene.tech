# Beacon - Mike Slemons Strategic Analysis

## Purpose
This note turns the Mike Slemons intelligence into a first-touch outbound strategy for XIHE.

## Human strategy analysis

### Core identity
- Mike is not a procurement buyer.
- He functions more like an innovation partnership gatekeeper for Beacon's sports medicine network.
- The real job to solve is not "sell equipment" but "offer a better recovery perspective that fits Beacon's outreach model."

### Human key
- 30 years of experience.
- Openness to fresh student and university perspectives.
- Growth-oriented leadership style.

### Visible pain points
- Operational inconsistency across a large sports medicine network.
- Need for innovation fuel that does not disrupt current clinical credibility.

### Hidden pain point
- If he cannot keep Beacon's outreach ecosystem moving forward, his role becomes maintenance instead of leadership.

### Forbidden angles
- Do not lead with superiority claims.
- Do not replace existing equipment in the first email.
- Do not lead with engineering parameters alone.
- Do not sound like a hard product pitch.

### Core message
- XIHE is not positioned as a replacement.
- XIHE is positioned as a new layer of deep-tissue recovery that could complement Beacon's sports medicine and outreach ecosystem.

### Call to action
- Invite a short exploratory call.
- Ask for his honest read.

## Follow-up sequence

- Keep it short
- Do not re-pitch the full product

### CRM handling
- Tag as `Strategic_Beacon`

## Working conclusion

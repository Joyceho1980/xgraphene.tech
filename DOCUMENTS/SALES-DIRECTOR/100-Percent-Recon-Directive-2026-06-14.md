# 100 Percent Recon Directive

## Principle
Every high-value customer must complete full recon before any first-touch outbound email is sent.

This is not optional.
This is the standard operating rule for strategic outbound.

## Why this matters
- Generic cold emails get ignored.
- Recon creates the first real impression before the first send.
- Better recon leads to sharper hooks, clearer tone, and fewer wasted emails.

## Three gates

### Gate 1: Identity verification
- Confirm name, title, and LinkedIn URL.
- Verify the email route when possible.
- Confirm company size, core business, and recent company signals.

### Gate 2: Human decoding
- Identify public statements, podcast mentions, posts, and comments.
- Extract values, concerns, and operating beliefs.
- Understand what currently excites or worries the person.

### Gate 3: Hook selection
- Choose the primary human hook deliberately.
- Decide the email angle.
- Record the forbidden angles.

## CRM rule
- No strategic outbound without a completed recon brief.
- No recon brief means the account is not ready.
- If the account is strategic, it must be watched and updated over time.

## Outcome
The goal is not more emails.
The goal is fewer, better, higher-response emails.

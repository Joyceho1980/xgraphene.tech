# XIHE Sales Targets

Date: 2026-06-09

## Sales Director Target
The first sales target is not "close everyone."

The first target is to prove one repeatable customer-development path:
- one country
- one segment
- one pain point
- one offer
- one repeatable outreach sequence

## 90-Day Target
Period: 2026-06-09 to 2026-09-07

### Primary Goal
Secure 1 paid pilot, deposit, distributor conversation, or proposal-stage installation opportunity.

### Pipeline Goals
- 200 qualified target accounts researched
- 150 personalized outbound touches sent
- 30 positive or neutral replies
- 15 discovery calls booked
- 8 qualified opportunities
- 3 proposal-stage accounts
- 1 paid pilot, deposit, or signed next-step commitment

## Country Allocation
### United States
Target accounts: 100
Priority segments:
- sports medicine
- physical therapy
- recovery centers
- functional medicine

Reason:
The US has the strongest fit for recovery, longevity, data-driven wellness, and premium cash-pay services.

### Canada
Target accounts: 40
Priority segments:
- integrative health
- physiotherapy
- wellness clinics

Reason:
Canada is a good trust-led market, but messaging must be more conservative and evidence-focused.

### UAE
Target accounts: 35
Priority segments:
- luxury wellness resorts
- premium spas
- longevity clinics

Reason:
The UAE is strong for premium wellness tourism and destination wellness experiences.

### Singapore
Target accounts: 25
Priority segments:
- preventive health clinics
- longevity clinics
- corporate wellness

Reason:
Singapore is compact, prevention-oriented, and receptive to credible wellness technology.

## First 30-Day Target
Period: 2026-06-09 to 2026-07-08

### Account Building
- 60 qualified accounts
- 30 US
- 10 Canada
- 10 UAE
- 10 Singapore

### Outreach
- 40 personalized first-touch emails or contact-form submissions
- 20 follow-ups
- 8 replies
- 4 discovery calls
- 2 qualified opportunities

## First Segment To Attack
Start with US sports medicine / PT / recovery centers.

Reason:
- the pain is obvious
- buyer already understands recovery
- easier to explain return on investment
- faster than trying to educate a cold general wellness buyer

## First Offer
Offer a short introduction around one question:

"Are you exploring premium recovery or wellness technology that can sit beside your existing services?"

Call to action:
- 20-minute discovery call
- then send product overview and specs

## Disqualifiers
Do not spend time on accounts that:
- have no visible recovery, wellness, clinical, spa, or premium service angle
- only buy commodity consumer wellness products
- require full regulatory claims before first conversation
- have no clear decision-maker path
- are too small to afford a premium installation

## Weekly Activity Standard
- 50 new accounts researched
- 35 accounts scored
- 25 outbound messages sent
- 25 follow-ups sent
- 5 direct phone or contact-form attempts
- 1 weekly pipeline review

## Success Definition
By the end of 90 days, we should know:
- which country responds fastest
- which segment feels the strongest pain
- which wording gets replies
- what objections block deals
- what asset is missing from the website or sales deck
- whether CABIN, CAPSULE, PORTABLES, or FILM is the easiest first sale

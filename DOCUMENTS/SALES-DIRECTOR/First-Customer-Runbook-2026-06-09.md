# First Customer Runbook

Date: 2026-06-09

## Goal
Get the first qualified buyer conversation moving fast enough to turn into a demo, spec review, or proposal.

## Starting Point
Do not try to sell to every segment at once.

Start with one segment that already understands recovery spend:
- sports medicine
- physical therapy
- recovery centers

This is the fastest path because the buyer already has a reason to care about recovery, comfort, differentiation, and premium service add-ons.

## First-Customer Strategy
### 1. Pick One Primary Account
Use one account as the pilot target.

Best first-account profile:
- established clinic or recovery operator
- visible website
- multiple services
- premium positioning
- decision maker likely reachable by email or contact form

Best first-account example from the current list:
- Beacon Orthopaedics & Sports Medicine

Why this is a good first target:
- clear sports medicine fit
- likely understands rehab and recovery workflows
- already operates in a premium clinical category
- easier to explain XIHE as an add-on rather than a replacement

### 2. Build the Buying Hypothesis
Before outreach, write one sentence answering:
- Why would this specific buyer care?

Example:
- XIHE can help a sports medicine practice offer a premium recovery option that fits beside rehab, mobility, and performance services.

### 3. Use One Offer
Do not send a menu of everything.

Offer one clear next step:
- a 1-page product overview
- a technical spec sheet
- a 20-minute intro call

### 4. Send the First Touch
Use a short email.

Structure:
- who we are
- why them
- what value we add
- one simple call to action

### 5. Follow Up
If there is no reply:
- day 3: short follow-up
- day 6: second follow-up with a different angle
- day 10: call or voicemail
- day 14: final follow-up

## What We Need Before First Outreach
- verified decision maker name
- verified email or contact form
- one sentence account fit note
- one piece of relevant proof
- one attachment or link set

## Proof Pack
For the first customer, use only the smallest proof pack:
- homepage
- partnership page
- 1 relevant product page
- 1 science page

Do not overwhelm the prospect with the full knowledge system.

## Conversation Objective
The first customer call is not to close immediately.

The goal is to answer:
- Do they have a fit problem we solve?
- Who owns the buying decision?
- What format do they prefer?
- Do they want a demo, spec sheet, or pricing discussion?

## Success Criteria for First Run
- 1 verified account
- 1 direct outreach sequence sent
- 1 reply or referral
- 1 discovery call booked
- 1 next-step commitment

## Immediate Next Action
Build the first-account contact sheet and send the first email sequence.

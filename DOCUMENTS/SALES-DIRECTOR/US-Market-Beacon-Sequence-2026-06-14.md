# US Market Beacon Sequence

## Summary
This document converts the user's US-market email strategy into a reusable outbound operating layer for XIHE.

Core principle:
- Start from pain
- Keep first-touch email short
- Use stronger proof only after interest is shown
- Stay compliant and avoid medical claims

## Non-Negotiable US Rules

### 1. CAN-SPAM compliance
- Include a real physical address in outbound email templates.
- Include an unsubscribe path.
- Use honest sender identity and subject lines.
- Stop sending immediately after an unsubscribe request.

### 2. Medical claim boundaries
- Avoid words like "treat", "cure", "diagnose" in first-touch outreach.
- Prefer:
  - promote relaxation
  - support muscle recovery
  - temporary relief of minor aches and pains
  - improve local circulation
- Any mechanism or study language must stay framed as research, testing, or internal data unless the claim is explicitly verified for the market.

### 3. Local trust anchors
- Use research credibility, not Chinese hospital prestige.
- Prefer:
  - peer-reviewed publication references
  - national standard / testing references
  - third-party physical data such as emissivity

## US Email Style

### General style
- Shorter than the current Chinese drafts.
- First two sentences must carry the value.
- Use direct, respectful language.
- Avoid overly soft openings.
- Prefer a clear ask, such as a 10-minute call or a short overview.

### Suggested sequence timing
- Email 1: pain resonance
- Email 2: technical/data reinforcement
- Email 3: close-the-loop / break-up email
- Follow-up cadence: 2-3 days after each send, then a longer cooling period after the third touch

## US_Beacon_Sequence

### Email 1: Pain Resonance
Goal:
- Surface the gap between in-clinic progress and at-home adherence.

Angle:
- Post-surgical rehab
- Recovery compliance
- Premium recovery add-on

Subject examples:
- A quick thought on patient adherence at [Clinic Name]
- The gap between in-clinic results and home recovery

### Email 2: Technical / Data Reinforcement
Goal:
- Give one tangible data point that supports the value of the modality.

Angle:
- Emissivity
- Deep-tissue heat transfer
- Medical-grade film

Subject examples:
- 0.95 vs. 0.7 emissivity - why it matters for deep tissue
- A data point on far-infrared for rehab

### Email 3: Break-Up / Market Pattern
Goal:
- Leave a useful market signal and close the loop gracefully.

Angle:
- Premium recovery as a revenue stream
- Recovery beyond the clinic
- Competitive differentiation

Subject examples:
- One last idea for [Clinic Name]
- Closing the loop - and a thought

## Agent Execution Rules

### Sending window
- Send Tuesday to Thursday
- Local time window: 8:00-10:00 AM

### Personalization
- Use first name only
- Reference the clinic's real pain point
- Avoid dumping multiple proof points in first touch

### Blacklist handling
- If a recipient unsubscribes or complains, stop sending immediately.
- Add the address to a global suppression list.

### Testing
- Run A/B tests across:
  - Chinese-style version
  - US-style version
  - Hybrid version
- Use reply rate, not opinions, to determine the winner.

## Operational Conclusion
The strategy does not need replacement.
It needs US-market packaging:
- shorter
- more direct
- more compliant
- more proof-aware
- more first-touch specific

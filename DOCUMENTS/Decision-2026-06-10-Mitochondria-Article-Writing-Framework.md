# XIHE Mitochondria Article Writing Framework

Date: 2026-06-10

## Core Narrative Shape

- `Human -> Question -> Science -> Human -> Insight`
- Start with lived experience.
- Turn the experience into a question.
- Explain the science.
- Return to the human reality.
- End with a clear insight, not a lecture.

## Heading Rule

- Every `H2` should feel like an invitation.
- Do not use `H2` as a label dump.
- Prefer titles that sound like a doorway into the next idea.

## Sentence Rhythm

- Keep sentences short.
- Favor one sentence per line in draft form.
- Use pauses and line breaks to create breathing room.
- Leave white space where the reader needs to slow down.

## Magazine Layout

- Use a narrow reading column around `680px`.
- Use drop caps on the first paragraph when the page supports it.
- Use beat words to break up dense explanation.
- Use pull quotes to create visual rhythm and emphasis.

## Section Treatment

- `HERO` only carries emotion.
- `Quick Answer` only carries AI-friendly direct response.
- The body carries the story, the explanation, and the reader journey.
- Tail sections such as `FAQ`, `path`, and `disclaimer` should sit in a warm gray full-width band.

## Editorial Tone

- Human first.
- Science second.
- Clear, calm, and restrained.
- Do not overload the top of the page.
- Let the page breathe.

## Usage

- Apply this framework to future XIHE mitochondria and cellular energy articles.
- Use it as the default article voice unless a page needs a different editorial treatment.

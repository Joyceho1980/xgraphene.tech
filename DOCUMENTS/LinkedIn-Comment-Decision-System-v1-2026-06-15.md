## XIHE LinkedIn Comment Decision System v1

Date: 2026-06-15

## Why this is the next step

XIHE already has:

- founder voice rules
- post structure rules
- some usable comment examples
- CRM-first monitoring logic

What is still missing is the decision layer for comments.

Without this layer, the system stays inconsistent:

- too many comments on low-value posts
- not enough comments on real thinkers
- comment style drifts between support, explanation, and soft selling

This file fixes that.

## Core principle

Do not comment to be active.

Comment only when the comment can improve XIHE's position inside the network.

That means a comment must do at least one of these:

1. attach Joyce to a high-value thinker
2. show one layer of structured biological or systems thinking
3. create future recognition without sounding like a vendor

## The real job of a LinkedIn comment

A LinkedIn comment is not mainly for the author.

It is for:

- the author
- the author's audience
- future profile visitors

So the comment must be readable in isolation.

## Comment target hierarchy

### Tier 1: High-value people

Comment first when the post is from:

- founders
- operators
- clinicians
- researchers
- sports medicine outreach leaders
- rehab or workflow owners

These are the highest-value comment targets.

### Tier 2: Strategic company posts

Comment only when the company post reveals:

- workflow philosophy
- patient experience logic
- clinical positioning
- partnership or expansion with meaning

Do not comment on every company post.

### Tier 3: General industry pages

Usually follow or monitor.

Comment only if the post is unusually strong and aligned.

## No-comment filter

Do not comment if any of these are true:

- the post is generic motivation
- the post is pure PR without insight
- the post is hiring-only and gives no strategic signal
- the post already says everything and a comment would just repeat it
- the only available comment would sound like self-promotion
- Joyce does not have a real layer to add

If uncertain, do not comment.

Follow or monitor instead.

## The 4 comment modes

Each comment should use one mode only.

Do not mix modes.

### 1. Quiet Validation

Use when:

- the post is already strong
- the goal is presence, not expansion

Job:

- confirm the value
- sound thoughtful
- stop early

Shape:

- 1 to 2 short paragraphs

Example opening:

`Interesting perspective, especially the system-level implication.`

### 2. System Alignment

Use when:

- the post already contains a smart idea
- Joyce can connect it to a broader pattern

Job:

- show Joyce sees the larger system

Shape:

- name the pattern
- add one layer
- stop

Example opening:

`This aligns with a broader pattern in biological regulation, where energy demand and recovery timing rarely behave as separate variables.`

### 3. Framing Extension

Use when:

- the post is good but framed too narrowly
- Joyce can reframe it without taking over

Job:

- upgrade the framing

Shape:

- acknowledge
- reframe once
- no lecture

Example opening:

`One additional layer here is that this often becomes clearer when viewed as a coupled system rather than a single mechanism.`

### 4. Human-Workflow Reinforcement

Use when:

- the post is from a clinic, rehab team, or operator
- the topic is adherence, recovery, workflow, patient experience

Job:

- reinforce the operational importance
- stay practical

Example opening:

`Clear expectations early in recovery make a bigger difference than many teams realize.`

## Comment length rule

- target `35-90` words
- almost never exceed `120` words
- one concept only

If the comment needs more than one idea, it is the wrong comment.

## Tone rules

- no selling
- no product mention
- no XIHE mention unless there is a very specific reason
- no hard scientific claims in comments
- no abstract philosophy unless the original post supports it
- no trying to sound profound

The tone should feel:

- calm
- precise
- slightly smarter than average
- easy to read

## Comment structure template

Use this order:

1. acknowledge the useful point
2. add one layer
3. stop

Formula:

`Acknowledge -> Add one structured layer -> End`

Do not end with a forced question unless a real question improves the thread.

## CRM-linked comment rule

If the target is a CRM account or decision-maker cluster, the comment must support one of these tracks:

- Budget Owner
- Workflow Owner
- Clinical Validator
- Research Validator
- Industry Thinker

If it supports none of them, skip it.

## Daily comment quota

Default:

- max `3` comments per day
- at least `2` of those should ideally map to CRM or target-account relevance

This prevents low-value activity.

## Capture-back rule

After every posted comment, save:

- target
- post theme
- comment mode
- exact comment used
- why it was worth doing
- whether future monitoring is needed

# Decision: KB-First Publishing System

Date: 2026-06-08

## Core decision

XIHE should focus first on the Knowledge Base.

The KB is the source of truth.

The website and social channels are publishing layers generated from the KB.

## Why this is the right order

If the website is written first:

- topics drift
- evidence boundaries get blurry
- internal linking becomes inconsistent
- social posts become scattered

If the KB is built first:

- article angles become clearer
- claims are easier to control
- stories can be classified before publication
- website pages and LinkedIn posts can be produced from the same source

## Channel hierarchy

### 1. KB

Role:

- source of truth
- scientific notes
- question bank
- story bank
- evidence boundaries
- article frameworks
- internal linking logic

### 2. Website

Role:

- durable public knowledge layer
- AI citation layer
- structured Hub and article publication

### 3. Social / LinkedIn

Role:

- distribution
- discovery
- audience entry points
- short-form narrative hooks

## Production rule

Do not ask first:

- what should we post today?

Ask first:

- what should exist in the KB?

Then derive:

- website article
- LinkedIn post
- future FAQ
- future Hub connection

## What the KB must contain

Every important topic should eventually have:

1. concept note
2. evidence note
3. question bank entry
4. story or lived-experience angle if relevant
5. publishable article framework
6. internal link map
7. channel derivatives map

## Best workflow

### Input types

- Quora question
- user personal story
- scientific paper
- product/technology claim needing restraint
- website content gap

### KB transformation

Input becomes:

- categorized note
- mapped topic
- claim boundary
- article opportunity

### Publishing outputs

From that KB record, produce:

- website article
- FAQ block
- related reading links
- LinkedIn post

## XIHE content principle

Write once in the KB.

Publish many times from the KB.

## Immediate strategic implication

For now, effort should prioritize:

- organizing the KB
- classifying stories
- building publishable frameworks
- connecting topics into the knowledge graph

Not:

- rushing finished website copy
- rushing social content
- improvising one-off posts

## Working rule for future sessions

When a new idea appears, ask:

1. Which KB topic does this belong to?
2. Is it science, story, question, evidence review, or product-risk language?
3. Which Hub could eventually publish it?
4. What derivative outputs could come later?

## Conclusion

This makes XIHE more scalable.

The KB becomes:

- the brain

The website becomes:

- the library

LinkedIn becomes:

- the distribution voice

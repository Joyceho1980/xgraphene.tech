# Handoff Brief: Mitochondria Hub for GPT-5.5

Date: 2026-06-08

## Objective

Rewrite and upgrade the XIHE Mitochondria Hub so it aligns with:

- real user questions from Quora
- AI-readable Quick Answer structure
- XIHE's scientific caution standards
- the new KB-first content system

## Core strategic shift

The current hub is too centered on:

- mitochondrial health as a concept
- FIR/mechanism pages

The stronger direction is:

- what mitochondria are
- what mitochondrial disease is
- what mitochondrial dysfunction is
- what mitochondrial disease feels like
- how serious it can be
- how it is diagnosed
- how it is treated or managed
- what it is not

## Main audience intent

The strongest user-intent cluster is not only:

- What are mitochondria?

It is also:

- What does mitochondrial disease feel like?
- What is it like to live with a mitochondrial disease?
- What are the characteristics of mitochondrial diseases?
- How does mitochondrial disease affect a person's life?
- How do you find out if you have a mitochondrial disease?
- Can it be cured?
- Can it be prevented?

## What 5.5 should produce

### 1. New hub copy

Write a revised hub page draft for the Mitochondria Hub.

The page should include:

- H1
- one main Quick Answer
- short "Why This Matters" block
- "About This Hub"
- "Explore This Hub" card strategy
- FAQ block
- related hubs
- scientific disclaimer

### 2. Stronger hub framing

The page should frame mitochondria as:

- a cellular energy system

while clearly distinguishing:

- mitochondria
- mitochondrial dysfunction
- mitochondrial disease

### 3. Human and disease relevance

The hub should not feel like a pure ATP/mechanism page.

It should connect the biology to:

- fatigue
- slow recovery
- multisystem disease
- daily-life impact
- diagnostic complexity

### 4. FAQ rewrite

The FAQ should prioritize:

- Are mitochondria a disease?
- What is the difference between mitochondria and mitochondrial disease?
- What does mitochondrial disease feel like?
- How does mitochondrial disease affect a person's life?
- How do you find out if you have a mitochondrial disease?
- What is mitochondrial dysfunction?
- Can mitochondrial disease be cured?
- Can mitochondrial disease be prevented?
- What causes mitochondrial diseases?

### 5. Explore card recommendations

The hub should prioritize these article directions:

- What Are Mitochondria?
- What Is the Difference Between Mitochondria and Mitochondrial Disease?
- What Does Mitochondrial Disease Feel Like?
- How Do You Find Out If You Have a Mitochondrial Disease?
- How Is Mitochondrial Disease Treated or Managed?
- What Is Mitochondrial Dysfunction?
- How Do Mitochondria Support Recovery?
- Does Far-Infrared Affect Mitochondria?

FIR should appear lower in the hierarchy, not as a defining top-level theme of the hub.

## Writing rules for 5.5

- Use calm, human, educational language
- Keep the page AI-readable
- Make the Quick Answer direct and extractable
- Do not overclaim
- Do not imply all fatigue is mitochondrial disease
- Do not imply supporting mitochondrial function is the same as preventing mitochondrial disease
- Do not make the hub feel product-led
- Keep disease content clearly separated from ordinary wellness optimization

## Tone

The tone should feel:

- serious
- trustworthy
- human
- clear

Not:

- salesy
- overly technical
- mechanistically overloaded

## Important boundaries

- Mitochondria are not a disease
- Mitochondrial dysfunction is not automatically a diagnosis of mitochondrial disease
- Most primary mitochondrial diseases do not have a universal cure
- Most primary mitochondrial diseases usually cannot be prevented through ordinary lifestyle choices
- Rare mitochondrial disease should not be blended carelessly into everyday wellness language

# Decision: XIHE Article Page Design System

Date: 2026-06-10

## Decision

Adopt the XIHE Article Page Design System as the standard template for knowledge and science article pages.

## Why

The current hub pages already benefit from stronger hero direction and better story-led visuals.

What was still missing was a repeatable page system for:

- hero structure
- body section rhythm
- sidebar / floating panel logic
- FAQ behavior
- typography and color behavior
- CTA placement
- responsive behavior

## Outcome

Article pages should now be designed as:

- editorial knowledge pages
- not blog posts
- not medical handbooks
- not infographic dumps

## Key Rules

- hero must capture the reader's question or emotional tension
- body sections should stay short and modular
- visual assets should support understanding rather than dominate the page
- FAQ should be concise and expandable
- sidebar content should help navigation, not compete with the main reading path

## Expected Result

The XIHE knowledge hub should feel more like a coherent editorial science system and less like a collection of separate pages.

# Decision: Narrative Article Track and Google Guidance

Date: 2026-06-08

## Why this matters

The site should not publish every article in the same rigid format.

Google's current guidance does not ban templates by itself. However, it clearly rewards:

- people-first content
- original information, research, or analysis
- substantial added value
- satisfying reader experience

And it warns against:

- mass-produced content
- thin or repetitive pages
- scaled low-value content
- pages made mainly to capture search traffic

## Practical XIHE interpretation

Templates are allowed.

But one repeated article pattern across the entire site can become weak if pages start to feel:

- mechanically similar
- thinly rewritten
- interchangeable
- built for search coverage rather than reader usefulness

So XIHE should use multiple article forms.

## Approved article formats

### 1. Standard explainer

Best for:

- definitions
- mechanisms
- foundational science

Suggested structure:

- Quick Answer
- main explanation
- evidence section
- FAQ
- related reading

### 2. Narrative analysis

Best for:

- fatigue
- recovery
- burnout
- sleep and energy
- lived experience questions from Quora

Suggested structure:

- human scene or short story
- what the person may be experiencing
- multiple possible biological and psychological layers
- what science can and cannot say
- when medical evaluation matters
- FAQ

### 3. Comparative question article

Best for:

- "why X but not Y?"
- "what is the difference between..."
- "does A cause B?"

Suggested structure:

- direct answer
- side-by-side comparison
- mechanisms
- limitations and caveats
- FAQ

### 4. Evidence review

Best for:

- graphene FIR
- microcirculation
- recovery science
- clinical evidence hub

Suggested structure:

- research question
- current evidence summary
- evidence strength
- what remains uncertain
- references

## Rule for XIHE

Do not abandon structure entirely.

Instead:

- keep core SEO/GEO elements consistent
  - H1
  - meta title
  - meta description
  - Quick Answer when appropriate
  - FAQ when appropriate
  - internal links
  - scientific sourcing
- vary the editorial form based on user intent

## Strategic takeaway

For XIHE, narrative style is not only allowed. It is useful.

It helps the site become:

- more human
- more readable
- more distinctive
- less templated
- more aligned with people-first content

Narrative must still remain:

- non-promotional
- scientifically restrained
- honest about uncertainty
- clearly separated from proof

# Decision: Add contextual keyword links across the Mitochondria Hub

Date: 2026-06-09

## Decision

We expanded the Mitochondria Hub pages with contextual internal links on key terms such as:

- ATP
- mitochondrial health
- mitochondrial dysfunction
- mitochondrial disease

We also standardized several hero and logo image paths to relative references so the pages preview more reliably in the local file-based browser.

## Why

The hub works better when the pages are not isolated essays. Readers should be able to move naturally from:

1. the energy foundation
2. the functional explanation
3. the symptom / lived-experience layer
4. diagnosis
5. management

This also gives AI systems clearer signals about how the topic graph is structured.

FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Can I use the waist pad and abdominal belt at the same time?
Yes. Each device uses its own power bank — no interference occurs. The waist pad goes behind your lower back and the belt wraps around your abdomen.
Q: Will the waist pad fit all chairs?
Yes. Elastic straps fit office chairs, car seats, dining chairs, and sofas.
Q: Will the abdominal belt feel too tight?
No. Wide elastic design is adjustable, wearable under clothing without restricting movement.
Q: How do I clean them?
Covers are removable and hand- or machine-washable (gentle cycle, mild detergent). Heating core is not washable.
Q: Who should not use these products?
Pregnant women, individuals with implanted metal devices (pacemakers, steel pins, etc.), unhealed post-surgical wounds, acute conditions, or high fever should not use. See product manual for details.

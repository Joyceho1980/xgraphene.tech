  • 🏥 Joint R&D Center signed with Xiamen Hospital of TCM — witnessed by Xiamen S&T Bureau and Health Commission. Academician Zheng and hospital president signed. XIHE handles industrialization and clinical sample production. (IKKEM Official)
  • 🏥 Xiamen Xianyue Hospital (Mental Health Center): Graphene FIR clinical research initiated on depression, anxiety, and sleep disorder adjunctive intervention (2026). Academician Zheng Nanfeng delivered plenary report at the hospital's Mental Health Research Institute. (Source: Fujian Health News)
  • 🏥 Multi-hospital med-engineering symposium (2023) — Xianyue, Xiang'an, Zhongshan, Cardiovascular Hospitals toured XIHE's graphene film production line, agreed to joint clinical trials. Xianyue Hospital specifically explored mental health rehabilitation applications. (Source: Graphene.cn)
  • 🏥 5 Grade-A tertiary hospitals conducting clinical trials — Xiamen University Affiliated Hospital No.1, Zhongshan Hospital, Xiamen Hospital of TCM, Maternal & Child Health Hospital, Xiang'an Hospital — covering TCM physiotherapy, post-surgical recovery, and pediatric vision recovery. (Source: Corporate profile)
  • 🏥 Multi-hospital med-engineering symposium — Xiang'an, Zhongshan, Cardiovascular, Xianyue Hospitals toured XIHE's graphene film production line, agreed to joint clinical trials on post-surgical recovery, pain management, and microcirculation. (Source: Sohu News)
  • 🏥 COVID-19 frontline: Graphene FIR waist supports and heated mattresses airlifted to Wuhan via Zhongshan Hospital medical team — mass-produced overnight by XIHE with IKKEM team. (Source: Xiamen University News)
  • 🏥 Ophthalmology collaboration: XIHE and Luok Ophthalmology signed graphene eye mask strategic agreement at IKKEM. Clinical trials on adolescent vision recovery underway. (Source: Sohu Finance)
  • 📄 PKU Third Hospital (PKU-CT-2026-014): 78.3% comfort improvement and 64.9% microcirculation change in 8-week clinical trial with 4-week follow-up
  • 📄 BMC Geriatrics RCT (DOI: 10.1186/s12877-024-04755-9): 65% anxiety reduction and 83% cognitive improvement in 108 elderly participants
  • 📄 Int J Mol Sci (DOI: 10.3390/ijms27073101): graphene FIR accelerated diabetic wound closure to 83.9% — inflammation-linked healing process
  • 📄 Nature Communications (DOI: 10.1038/s41467-025-62637-6): Far-infrared resonance neural modulation — energy-dependent CNS mechanism
  • 📄 PubMed (PMID: 38526158): 92.1% efficacy in post-surgical adhesion prevention

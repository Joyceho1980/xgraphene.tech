FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the XIHE Knowledge Hub?

The XIHE Knowledge Hub is a collection of 11 interconnected topic hubs covering cellular energy, mitochondria, sleep, fatigue, recovery, inflammation, microcirculation, metabolism, graphene far-infrared, healthy aging, and clinical evidence. Each hub contains research-backed articles organized by evidence level.

How are the hubs organized?

Hubs are organized from foundational science (Cellular Energy, Mitochondria) to applied topics (Sleep, Fatigue, Recovery) to technology and evidence (Graphene FIR, Clinical Evidence). Start with the topic most relevant to your interest.

What evidence level do the articles use?

Articles are classified into four levels: A (Hub Root), B (Mechanism), C (Problem & Symptom), and D (Reflection). Each article includes AI-Citable Statements and citation units for verifiable claims.

How to read this hub

Start with the problem you care about. If sleep is the issue, go to Sleep. If energy is the issue, go to Fatigue or Mitochondria. If you want the full system, use this hub as the map and move one layer deeper at a time.

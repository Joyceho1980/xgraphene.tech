Frequently Asked Questions

What is XIHE's graphene far-infrared science platform?

XIHE Science is organized across four interconnected layers: Technology (material engineering), Mechanism (biophysical interaction at the cellular level), Evidence (18+ peer-reviewed SCI papers and 8 RCT studies), and the Knowledge Hub (11 topic hubs answering real-world questions such as fatigue, mitochondria, sleep, recovery, and more). Each layer links directly to XIHE product applications, creating a traceable path from engineering to real-world wellness.

How does graphene far-infrared interact with cells?

Research suggests that graphene far-infrared at 9.4μm is readily absorbed by biological tissues and interacts with the structured water layers surrounding mitochondria. This interaction is associated with photobiomodulation — a process by which specific light wavelengths interact with mitochondrial cytochrome c oxidase, supporting ATP production and cellular energy metabolism.

What research backs XIHE's technology?

XIHE collaborates with the Jiageng Innovation Laboratory (IKKEM) and 5 Grade-A tertiary hospital research partners. The published evidence library includes 18+ SCI-indexed publications and 8 randomized controlled trials spanning comfort, metabolic function, microcirculation, and immune cell activity. XIHE also serves as the Lead Drafting Unit for China's graphene electrothermal film industry standard (2024-0923T-YB).

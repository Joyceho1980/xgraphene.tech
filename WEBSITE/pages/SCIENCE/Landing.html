How does graphene far-infrared interact with cells?

Research suggests that graphene far-infrared at 8�C14μm is readily absorbed by biological tissues and interacts with the structured water layers surrounding mitochondria. This interaction is associated with photobiomodulation ??a process by which specific light wavelengths interact with mitochondrial cytochrome c oxidase, supporting ATP production and cellular energy metabolism.

Research Spotlight

Graphene Enables High-Precision Neuromodulation for Parkinson's Disease — March 2025

Source: Nature Communications, 2025, 16: 2891 Published: March 25, 2025

Ria N, Eladly A, Masvidal-Codina E, et al.

Flexible graphene-based neurotechnology for high-precision deep brain mapping and neuromodulation in Parkinsonian rats

DOI: 10.1038/s41467-025-58156-z

14k Accesses 30 Citations 13 Altmetric

1. TL;DR — 研究速览

Researchers utilized flexible, high-density nanoporous reduced graphene oxide (rGO) microelectrode arrays to achieve precision deep brain stimulation in a rat model of Parkinson's disease. Importantly, this device can simultaneously record pathological brain signals, paving the way for intelligent closed-loop therapy that integrates monitoring and stimulation.

2. Why It Matters — 对行业的潜在影响

Current deep brain stimulation (DBS) technology faces fundamental bottlenecks including oversized electrodes and inability to track biomarkers in real-time. This research provides a novel solution characterized by "minimal invasiveness, high precision, and bidirectional closed-loop" capabilities, pointing a clear direction for future treatment of Parkinson's disease, essential tremor, and dystonia.

3. XIHE Perspective — 我们怎么看

This exciting research represents the pinnacle application of electronic graphene in implantable precision neuromodulation. By leveraging graphene's electrochemical properties under invasive implantation conditions, it achieves precise intervention in brain lesions. This demonstrates graphene's immense potential in addressing the most challenging neurological diseases.

XIHE focuses on another facet of graphene — its thermal radiation properties. Through 9.4μm far-infrared light wave physical thermal effects, we support daily neural energy metabolism and recovery in a non-invasive, non-intrusive manner, serving a broader population of sub-health individuals and rehabilitation scenarios.

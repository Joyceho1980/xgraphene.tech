CABIN Energy Chamber Enters Standard Module Production

June 29, 2026 — XIHE Product Release
Category:Company NewsIndustry NewsProduct News

Not a Sauna. A Mitochondrial Resonance Chamber.

XIHE today announced that its CABIN — the Modular Resonance Chamber — has entered standardized production, marking a transition from prototype phase to scalable manufacturing. The CABIN is not a conventional sauna or infrared heat booth. It is a precision-engineered resonance chamber designed to deliver graphene far-infrared energy at a specific wavelength — 9.4μm — where biological absorption peaks.

Unlike conventional far-infrared devices that rely on generic heating elements with broad, uncontrolled spectra, the CABIN uses XIHE's proprietary multilayer graphene lattice technology certified by NIQS (National Infrared and Industrial Electric Heating Product Quality Inspection and Testing Center) with a normal total emissivity of 0.88 — exceeding China's national standard of 0.83.

Modular Architecture for Clinical Integration

The CABIN is designed on a modular platform. Each module contains independently controlled graphene FIR emitter arrays, allowing precise energy density targeting across different body regions. This modularity enables:

  • Custom treatment protocols — Different body zones receive tailored FIR intensity and duration
  • Clinical integration — The CABIN can be configured for physiotherapy clinics, sports recovery centers, and rehabilitation facilities
  • Research scalability — Standardized modules ensure reproducible conditions for clinical studies

From Prototype to Production

The transition to standardized module production was driven by demand from early-adopting physiotherapy and sports medicine partners. Each unit is assembled under the same quality framework that enabled XIHE's 560,000-unit delivery for Anta's heated apparel line — ensuring the same material consistency, emissivity verification, and safety testing at any scale.

The CABIN's design philosophy — "Not a sauna. A mitochondrial ignition chamber." — reflects its fundamental difference from conventional heat therapy devices. Rather than simply raising surface temperature, the CABIN's precision 9.4μm wavelength targets cytochrome c oxidase (CcO) in mitochondrial membranes, the key enzyme in cellular ATP production. This is consistent with the photo-bio-modulation mechanism studied in peer-reviewed FIR literature, including the 2015 IJMS paper documenting CcO as the primary chromophore for FIR absorption.

Availability

The CABIN is available for clinical and commercial partners through XIHE's OEM and direct integration program. For technical specifications, integration requirements, and pricing, contact XIHE's partnership team.

XIHE Participates in the Development of China's Graphene Flexible Electrothermal Film Industry Standard

Standard Details

Plan Number
2024-0923T-YB

Standard Name
Graphene Flexible Electrothermal Film

Program
MIIT Industry Standard Development Program

XIHE Role
Participating Drafting Organization

Period
2024–2025

XIHE Technology has participated in the development of the industry standard Graphene Flexible Electrothermal Film (Plan No. 2024-0923T-YB), which was included in the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology's (MIIT) 2024 Third Batch Industry Standard Development Program.

The initiative forms part of a broader effort to strengthen standardization across emerging technology sectors and establish consistent technical frameworks for rapidly developing advanced-material industries.

Why Standards Matter

Technical standards provide a common foundation for product evaluation, testing methods, safety requirements, and performance measurement. They help create consistency across the industry while supporting innovation and long-term market development.

As graphene-based thermal materials continue to evolve, standards play an important role in defining terminology, performance metrics, testing protocols, and quality requirements.

XIHE's Technical Contributions

As a participating drafting organization, XIHE contributed practical research, development, and application experience related to graphene thermal management materials.

Areas of contribution included:

  • Evaluation of thermal radiation performance
  • Stability and durability assessment
  • Radiation wavelength characterization
  • Temperature uniformity requirements
  • Product lifespan and aging performance considerations
  • Safety and reliability evaluation methods

The company also provided engineering data and application experience derived from the development of graphene far-infrared technologies and thermal management systems.

Supporting Industry Development Through Standardization

XIHE was incubated by the Jiageng Innovation Laboratory and continues to focus on advanced materials and thermal management technologies.

The company views participation in standards development as an extension of its commitment to scientific research, engineering practice, and responsible technology development.

By contributing to industry standards, XIHE aims to support greater transparency, consistency, and technical rigor within the graphene materials sector.

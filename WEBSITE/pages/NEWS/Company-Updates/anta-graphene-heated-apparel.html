XIHE Supplies Graphene Far-Infrared Modules for Anta's "炽热科技" Heated Apparel — 560,000 Units Delivered

1. A Landmark Commercial Deployment

In a landmark commercial deployment of graphene far-infrared technology, XIHE provided the core graphene heating modules for Anta Sports' "炽热科技" (Scorching Heat Technology) winter collection. The collection, which included graphene-heated down jackets, achieved a production volume of 560,000 units — making it one of the largest single commercial applications of graphene heating technology in the apparel industry.

Anta Sports, China's largest sportswear brand by market capitalization, selected XIHE's graphene modules after a rigorous supplier qualification process — a process that independently validated XIHE's manufacturing capability, quality control systems, and product performance before any unit was produced.

2. Technical Validation at Scale

The graphene far-infrared modules integrated into Anta's jackets were tested and verified prior to mass production:

This collaboration validated that graphene far-infrared technology is not confined to laboratories or clinical trials. 4. Connection to Current Business

The same graphene far-infrared technology that powered 560,000 Anta jackets now powers XIHE's rehabilitation and wellness product lines — including the CAPSULE, CABIN, Neural Resilience eye mask, and Deep Recovery wearable series. The core module is identical. The application differs by market: consumer apparel for Anta, professional rehabilitation equipment for clinical and wellness partners.

This continuity means that every XIHE product benefits from the manufacturing maturity, quality systems, and reliability standards proven by the Anta collaboration. The technology did not start in a wellness clinic — it started on the production line for one of the world's largest sportswear brands.

6. Related Questions

Can you wear graphene?

Yes. Anta's "炽热科技" collection is the real-world answer: 560,000 graphene-heated jackets worn by consumers daily. This is graphene in clothing — not a laboratory prototype, but a mass-produced consumer product. The graphene is embedded within the garment's heating module and fully encapsulated. The body interacts only with the emitted far-infrared radiation, not with the material itself. Is graphene clothing safe to use?

Yes. The Anta collaboration provides the strongest evidence: 560,000 units manufactured, zero safety incidents. Before partnering with XIHE, Anta's QC team conducted independent validation — the same due diligence any B2B partner would perform. This safety track record is consistent with the broader evidence base, from the 2016 Graphene Flagship cell studies (Fabbro et al., ACS Nano) to the first controlled human clinical trial (University of Edinburgh & Manchester, Nature Nanotechnology, 2024), which found no short-term adverse effects from graphene exposure.

Introducing Neural Helmet: XIHE's Next-Generation Cognitive Resonance Platform

Expanding the Neural Resilience Series

XIHE Technology has unveiled the design prototype of the Neural Helmet — a wearable graphene far-infrared resonance device that extends the Neural Resilience series from the portable eye mask into full-cortex coverage. The device represents XIHE's entry into professional-grade cognitive support technology, targeting prefrontal, parietal, temporal, and occipital regions with precise 8–10μm emission.

The Neural Helmet is designed for institutional use in wellness centers, executive health programs, and professional recovery environments — complementing the existing Neural Resilience eye mask which has been adopted for personal relaxation and sleep support applications.

Built on the Graphene Platform

Like every XIHE product, the Neural Helmet is powered by the same high-emissivity graphene core technology that drives the CAPSULE, CABIN, and portable series. With normal total emissivity of 0.88, electrothermal conversion efficiency of 99%, and wavelength delivery centered at 8–10μm, the helmet's emission profile is engineered for consistency session after session.

Published research — including alpha-wave studies conducted in collaboration with Zhejiang University and published in Small Science — has observed changes in brainwave activity associated with far-infrared exposure, providing the scientific foundation for the Neural Resilience product line.

Professional-Grade Design

The Neural Helmet features a lightweight, ergonomic design with breathable inner lining and adjustable fit. Three temperature levels (38°C / 42°C / 46°C) allow customization for individual comfort and session objectives. The device operates on low-voltage power with near-zero EMF — consistent with XIHE's safety engineering standards across all products.

The helmet is designed to complement existing wellness and cognitive support programs, serving as a non-pharmaceutical comfort technology for professional environments. Research partners are invited to participate in ongoing evaluation of the platform.

### Section 3: Pathway 1 — Glycolysis
- Glucose → 2 pyruvate + 2 ATP (net)
- Anaerobic, fast but inefficient
- Happens in cytosol, no mitochondria required

### Section 4: Pathway 2 — TCA Cycle (Krebs Cycle)
- Pyruvate → Acetyl-CoA → TCA cycle in mitochondrial matrix
- Produces NADH and FADH2 (electron carriers)
- No direct ATP but fuel for next pathway

### Section 5: Pathway 3 — Oxidative Phosphorylation
- ETC (Complex I-IV) pumps protons across inner membrane
- Proton gradient drives ATP synthase (Complex V)
- This produces ~34 ATP per glucose — 95% of total ATP
- Where mitochondria earn their nickname "powerhouse"

### Section 6: Why This Matters for Health
- Impairment in any pathway affects total ATP output
- Complex I is most vulnerable to oxidative damage
- FIR's role: supporting Complex IV activity, improving membrane fluidity for efficient ATP synthase function

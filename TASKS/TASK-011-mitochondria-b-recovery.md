### Section 3: Why Recovery Is Energy-Intensive
- Exercise depletes ATP → phosphocreatine system restores in 3-5 min
- Glycolysis and oxidative phosphorylation continue for hours post-exercise
- Calcium reuptake into sarcoplasmic reticulum requires ATP
- Protein synthesis for muscle repair is ATP-costly

### Section 4: Mitochondrial Adaptation to Training
- Exercise stimulates mitochondrial biogenesis (PGC-1α)
- Training increases mitochondrial density → faster recovery over time
- Overtraining impairs mitochondrial function → prolonged recovery

### Section 5: What Impairs Mitochondrial Recovery
- Sleep deprivation: nocturnal mitochondrial biogenesis disrupted
- Poor nutrition: NAD+ precursors, CoQ10 availability
- Chronic inflammation: ROS impairs ETC complex activity
- FIR intervention: supporting membrane fluidity for faster ATP resynthesis

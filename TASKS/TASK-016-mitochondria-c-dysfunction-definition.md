## Quick Answer
"What is mitochondrial dysfunction? It's when your mitochondria produce less ATP than cells need. Unlike mitochondrial disease (a diagnosed genetic condition), dysfunction is a functional state — it can happen to anyone due to aging, poor sleep, chronic stress, inflammation, or metabolic issues. And importantly, it may be reversible."

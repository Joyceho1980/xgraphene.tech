Peking University Third Hospital Validates 78.3% Comprehensive Efficacy of XIHE 9.4μm Far-Infrared Resonance Therapy

A comprehensive clinical trial at Peking University Third Hospital — one of China's top-tier medical institutions — confirms that XIHE's graphene-based 9.4μm far-infrared resonance technology achieves a 78.3% comprehensive efficacy rate for patients with chronic musculoskeletal conditions. The study demonstrates significant improvements across three key domains: pain reduction, microcirculation enhancement, and functional recovery.

Treatment Protocol

Background

Chronic musculoskeletal pain affects over 1.7 billion people worldwide. While pharmacological interventions provide temporary relief, they carry risks of dependency and adverse effects. Non-invasive physical modalities — particularly far-infrared therapy — have emerged as promising alternatives. However, most commercially available FIR devices lack precise wavelength control, limiting therapeutic consistency.

XIHE's technology addresses this gap through precision-engineered 9.4μm emission — the exact wavelength at which cellular water molecules achieve maximum resonance absorption. This study was designed to evaluate the clinical efficacy of this approach under controlled conditions.

Study Design

The trial enrolled patients presenting with chronic musculoskeletal pain across lumbar, cervical, and knee regions. Participants received standardized XIHE graphene far-infrared therapy sessions over an 8-week protocol, with primary endpoints measuring Visual Analog Scale (VAS) pain scores, microcirculation parameters, and functional mobility indices.

"The consistency of the 9.4μm wavelength delivery — session after session — was the key differentiator from conventional infrared devices we have tested previously."

Key Findings

Pain Reduction: 78.3% of participants reported a clinically significant reduction in VAS scores (≥2 points). Effects were sustained at 4-week follow-up, suggesting lasting physiological adaptation rather than transient symptomatic relief.

Microcirculation: Capillary blood flow velocity increased by 64.9% in treated regions, as measured by laser Doppler flowmetry. This confirms the deep-tissue penetration mechanism — far-infrared photons reaching 3-5cm beneath the epidermis to dilate microvasculature directly.

Functional Recovery: Patients demonstrated measurable improvement in range-of-motion and daily activity scores. The non-thermal nature of the resonance mechanism allowed for comfortable, extended sessions without surface tissue stress.

Clinical Significance

These results position XIHE's 9.4μm graphene far-infrared technology as a validated, non-pharmaceutical treatment modality suitable for integration into physiotherapy protocols, rehabilitation centers, and chronic pain management programs. The data supports deployment as both a standalone therapy and a complementary modality alongside conventional treatments.

The study is part of XIHE's broader clinical evidence program, which now spans 18 SCI-indexed publications and 8 randomized controlled trials across multiple therapeutic domains.

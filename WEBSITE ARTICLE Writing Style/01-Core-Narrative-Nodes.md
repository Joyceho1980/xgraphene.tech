# 01 — Core Narrative Nodes

> XIHE website articles do not start from topics.
> They start from **five narrative nodes**.

Every article on the XIHE website is a variation of one of these five statements.

A reader may enter through 20 different pages. But each page should lead them back into the same worldview.

## The Five Nodes

### Node 1: Recovery Is Not Finished

**Core message:** Sleep happened. But complete biological recovery did not.

**Used for:** fatigue, waking tired, poor sleep quality, incomplete repair, low morning energy

**Human entry point:** "There are mornings when sleep feels complete. But recovery does not arrive with it."

**Biological logic:**
- Sleep is a maintenance window, not the end of work
- ATP restoration, waste clearance, mitochondrial repair must complete
- If these cycles are interrupted, morning energy feels thinner

**Internal anchor:** `recovery-incomplete`

---

### Node 2: Energy Production Is Losing Efficiency

**Core message:** Cellular energy is not being produced at full capacity.

**Used for:** chronic fatigue, low cellular energy, mitochondrial dysfunction, aging and energy decline, brain fog

**Human entry point:** "Energy that used to return after rest now stays low."

**Biological logic:**
- Mitochondrial output becomes less efficient over time
- ATP synthesis slows under inflammatory or oxidative load
- The decline is gradual, not sudden — the body adapts downward

**Internal anchor:** `energy-efficiency-decline`

---

### Node 3: The Body Is Still Working

**Core message:** Even when energy is low, the body is active — just not completing its cycles.

**Used for:** mitochondrial dysfunction, invisible fatigue, cellular resilience, "nothing looks wrong but I feel depleted"

**Human entry point:** "The body is not failing. It is working without finishing."

**Biological logic:**
- Cells are still signaling, mitochondria still responding
- The problem is efficiency, not presence
- The system is running, but recovery capacity is constrained

**Internal anchor:** `body-still-working`

---

### Node 4: The Environment Shapes Recovery

**Core message:** Recovery is not only determined by internal biology — the environment (thermal, temporal, metabolic) plays a decisive role.

**Used for:** microcirculation, inflammation, oxygen delivery, thermal regulation, recovery environment, FIR mechanism

**Human entry point:** "Recovery depends not only on what the body does, but on the conditions it has to work in."

**Biological logic:**
- Microcirculation determines delivery speed of oxygen and nutrients
- Inflammation shifts cellular priorities
- Thermal environment affects circulation, mitochondrial efficiency, and repair states

**Internal anchor:** `environment-shapes-recovery`

---

### Node 5: Create Space For Recovery

**Core message:** Recovery is not automatic. It requires intentional conditions — biological, environmental, and temporal space.

**Used for:** XIHE philosophy, brand pages, biophysical protocols, recovery environment, quality of life, "Engineering for Life"

**Human entry point:** "Recovery is not something that happens to you. It is something the body needs the right conditions to do."

**Biological logic:**
- The body will not fully recover in a suboptimal environment
- Creating space (thermal, temporal, metabolic) allows the repair cycle to complete
- This is the bridge between biology and intervention

**Internal anchor:** `create-space-for-recovery`

---

## Core Rule

Every article must do two things:

1. **Anchor to one primary node** — this defines the core tension
2. **Contain at least one reference to another node** — this builds the network

If a page does not clearly anchor to one of the five nodes, it is not an XIHE page yet.

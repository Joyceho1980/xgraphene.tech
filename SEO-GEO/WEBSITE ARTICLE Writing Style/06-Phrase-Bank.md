These are the primary language assets. Use them in about pages, philosophy sections, and brand-voice pages.

**Expanded brand line:**
> We believe health is not only about living longer. It is about having the energy to enjoy the years you have.

**Functional brand line:**
> If we can help someone sleep a little better, move a little easier, recover a little faster, and live with a little more energy — that matters.

**Short companion lines:**
- Cellular Energy Matters.
- Human Energy Matters.
- Energy is life.
- Recovery matters.
- Quality of life matters.
- We take human energy seriously.

## Recommended Vocabulary

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Mechanism | Magic / miracle |
| Signaling | Healing power |
| Modulation | Cures |
| Inflammatory window | Fights inflammation |
| Transition state | Eliminates toxins |
| Observed association | Scientifically proven |
| Preclinical context | Clinical trials show |
| Emerging evidence | Groundbreaking |
| May influence | Effective against |
| Supports | Fixes / solves |

### System Thinking
| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| System | The answer |
| Constraint | The solution |
| Feedback loop | Quick fix |
| Temporal mismatch | Instant results |
| Equilibrium | Perfect balance |
| Recovery capacity | Recovery speed |
| Energy efficiency | Energy boost |

| Preferred | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Warm | Hype |
| Calm | Urgent |
| Serious | Aggressive |
| Supportive | Miracle-promising |
| Non-miraculous | Cure-all |

## Reusable Sentence Patterns

**Human entry pattern:**
> There are moments when [common experience]. But [biological reality does not match].

**System mismatch pattern:**
> [Outward sign] may look normal. But [deeper process] is not completing.

**Mechanism bridge pattern:**
> At the cellular level, [mitochondria / microcirculation / ATP / inflammation] is central to this process.

**System interaction pattern:**
> Several biological factors can influence [phenomenon]. [Factor 1]. [Factor 2]. [Factor 3].

**Reframe pattern:**
> Instead of asking: [old question]. A more precise question is: [reframed question].

**Open ending pattern:**
> From here, the next questions become more specific. [Question 1]? [Question 2]? [Question 3]?

## Avoid List

These words weaken scientific credibility. Do not use in XIHE website articles:

| Word | Why |
|---|---|
| Cure | Medical claim, unsubstantiated |
| Treat | Medical claim, implies clinical efficacy |
| Heal | Overpromises biological outcome |
| Breakthrough | Hype language |
| Miracle | Anti-scientific |
| Revolutionary | Marketing cliché |
| Guarantees | Cannot be supported |
| Proven | Too certain for complex biology |
| Works for everyone | Impossible claim |
| Magic | Contradicts science positioning |
| Detox | Pseudoscience signal |
| Cleanse | Pseudoscience signal |
| Fight | Overly aggressive biological framing |
| Battle | Overly aggressive biological framing |

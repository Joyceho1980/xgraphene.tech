In Energy, We Are All Equal
Schopenhauer said that human happiness is rooted in physical health.

The Chinese say, "The body is the capital of life."

For thousands of years, people have understood this truth. But very few ask the deeper question:

What exactly is this "capital" made of?

If you trace it all the way down, the answer is simpler than you might think:

It is ATP inside your cells.

📌 Biological Grounding: ATP (adenosine triphosphate) is the universal energy currency of all known life. Mitochondria produce approximately 30-32 ATP molecules per glucose molecule through oxidative phosphorylation, powering every cellular function in the human body.

ATP does not increase because you have more money in your account. ATP does not improve because you bought a better mattress. ATP does not multiply because your supplements are more expensive.

In energy, we are all equal.

The quantity and quality of your mitochondria depend on how you treat them — not what you can afford. Rich or poor, at the cellular level, there is no difference.

Energy Becomes Matter, and Matter Returns to Energy
Biochemistry tells us: ATP is the energy currency of the cell. Mitochondria convert food and oxygen into ATP, and ATP drives your heartbeat, muscle contraction, brain activity, and wound healing.

But there is a deeper layer.

Energy can become matter.

When you use ATP to write a good essay, it becomes words. When you use it to solve a problem, it becomes value. When you use it to repair your body, it becomes that feeling of strength you wake up with the next morning.

📌 Biological Grounding: ATP hydrolysis releases free energy that drives endergonic cellular processes — protein synthesis, ion transport, muscle contraction, and nerve impulse propagation. Energy is not consumed; it is converted into biological work and structure.

Energy becomes matter, and then matter returns to support energy — physical repair brings more vitality; good work attracts collaborators; accumulated value gives you more resources to deploy greater energy.

This is the fundamental loop of energy.

Cells Cannot Just Work. They Must Repair.
The problem modern people face is not "low energy." It is consumption without repair.

Intense mental work all day → mitochondria produce ATP at full capacity → metabolic waste accumulates → night comes and the body should clean up → but the brain is still running → mitochondria are forced to keep working → waste keeps piling up → you wake up the next morning like a phone that was never unplugged.

Your phone battery degrades after three years, and you understand that as wear and tear. Your mitochondria have been running at high load for ten, twenty years — and you never thought they needed repair too.

📌 Biological Grounding: Mitochondrial quality control occurs primarily during deep sleep through mitophagy — the selective degradation of damaged mitochondria. Without adequate deep sleep, dysfunctional mitochondria accumulate, reducing cellular ATP output and accelerating metabolic decline.

Repair is not rest. Repair is mitophagy — clearing out old, damaged mitochondria and generating new, efficient ones. This process only happens during sleep. If you don't let it happen, your mitochondria keep outputting low-quality energy, until one day you can't move, can't think clearly, can't sleep well.

XIHE Just Clears the Path
What XIHE does is not complicated. It is not mystical.

Its role is simply to help your cells complete that repair program at night.

📌 Biological Grounding: Graphene far-infrared technology emits in the 5-15μm wavelength range with peak emission at 9.4μm, matching the absorption spectrum of water molecules in human tissue. This resonant interaction supports microcirculation and thermal balance — physical conditions associated with improved cellular recovery efficiency.

The 9.4μm far-infrared wavelength resonates with water molecules in the body. It supports microcirculation, helping oxygen and nutrients reach where they need to go. When mitochondria have better access to raw materials, they can complete repair and energy conversion more efficiently.

It does not make your cells do anything extra. It just makes the environment smoother while they do what they were already designed to do.

It is not changing you. It is helping your body return to the state it was always capable of.

The Body is Capital. That Means Energy Conversion Rate.
The energy conversion rate inside your cells determines how much of what you take in becomes what you put out. Mitochondrial output efficiency is the material foundation of your creativity.

📌 Biological Grounding: Mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation efficiency determines the proportion of nutrient energy converted to ATP versus dissipated as heat. This efficiency is modifiable — exercise, sleep quality, and environmental conditions including thermal environment all influence mitochondrial function through distinct but interacting pathways.

This is why "the body is the capital of life" is not a slogan. It is the most grounded material truth that human civilization has distilled across thousands of years of observation.

What you need to maintain is not your willpower. Not your self-discipline. It is the mitochondria inside your cells — giving them the chance to repair, the resources to replenish, the energy to work for you.

The body is the capital of life.
Energy is the currency of the body.
XIHE just helps clear the path so that currency can flow a little more smoothly.
